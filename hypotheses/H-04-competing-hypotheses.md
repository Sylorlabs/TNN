# H-04 — Competing hypotheses with traced support and contradiction

## Claim
Maintain a population of competing world/entity interpretations with support,
contradiction, provenance, and predicted consequences — never a single
confidence scalar. Consequence models learn what hypotheses imply and what
actions may reveal.

## Why it matters
It's the substrate that makes inquiry (H-05) well-defined: live alternatives
are needed to know what observation would discriminate them.

## Evidence
Partially tested, **negatively**, in native form:

| Experiment | Manipulation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| E47 (native) | Two grounded co-presence features (epoch two-hypothesis co-viability; option support/contradiction co-mass) added to terminal controller | Both varied substantially on training data, yet every joint-model no-unique decision still committed wrongly under the blocked online linear head (`NO_TESTED_REPRESENTATION_RESCUE`) |
| E49 (native) | Quadratic conjunctions of the same statistics | Negative |
| E50 (native) | Provenance/contention conjunctions | Negative |

The repo's bounded reading: these features are insufficient *under the tested
linear value geometry and training dynamics*, not refuted in general. The
hypothesis as a general claim is not rejected; the tested operationalizations
failed.

## Open questions
- Whether a grounded nonzero UNKNOWN value geometry (the proposed post-E50
  mechanism) rescues co-presence features.
- Consequence-model learning (what hypotheses imply) is untested natively.
