# H-06 — Learner-owned memory and representation under trainer-set constraints

## Claim
The learner should increasingly own *how* it learns — replay, inquiry, memory,
representation, reversible structural proposals — while the trainer
(a human/person/group) retains goals, values, permissions, provenance, rollback,
and safety authority. Autonomy is measured by decision ownership, earned through
evidence, across levels A0 (passive) → A5 (developmental), with the mega-plan's
M0–M7 milestone ladder gating structural self-modification (shadow proposals →
reversible canary → motif-level plasticity). A generic heuristic may seed a
birth/default policy; LRU is an eviction primitive, never representation
authority.

## Why it matters
It's the governance theory that lets the program pursue self-modification
without self-grant of authority: "TNN should become more autonomous in deciding
how to learn, not in silently deciding what it is allowed to do."
