## Change

The R34 v1 immediate-reward learner was advanced into the existing R33 persistent world. The v2 learner now acts from observation packets, navigates to physically distinct objects, uses inspection-derived local visual detail, issues touch actions, and receives the consequence only after the world delay. Credit is accepted only when the delayed consequence carries the exact pending initiating action id.

The learner maintains two latent context memories. A negative surprise on an exploit decision can allocate or reselect a context; no hidden regime label is passed to the learner. Learner state, pending causal credit, world state, and ingress state are carried through the existing 12-section checkpoint format. Learner bytes additionally carry an internal SHA-256 digest.
