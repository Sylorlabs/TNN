# R34 native held-out behavioral qualification V3 preregistration

V2 development passed five of six gates. Its association arm failed because a single undifferentiated association strength has no forgetting or latent-context mechanism: a forced mapping reversal causes old and new consequence credit to cancel. That limitation is retained as evidence and is not hidden.

V3 changes only the association evaluator: five learner-visible cue nodes retain a stationary hidden neighbor mapping. This gate asks the primitive claim actually supported by `r34_memory_association_v1.zag`: whether retrieval-linked delayed consequence credit organizes primitive links better than co-use alone. Nonstationary source reliability remains tested separately by the provenance gate.

All six learner implementations, all numerical thresholds, and held-out seeds remain unchanged. Held-out seeds `34211`, `34213`, `34217` have not been executed before this preregistration.
