Before any development exposure, N16 may use only namespace810000 for engineering smoke/authoring. Development910000, validation1010000, and confirmation1110000 remain untouched until independent review, preregistration, freeze, and separate stage admission.

Smoke01 on the initial12-arm candidate found that four-cluster routing left old gate hits unchanged at82 on the engineering fixture, while eight-cluster routing reduced old gate hits to49 with high new gate coverage442/512. However, immediately combining the localized gate with tolerance0 preservation reduced new gain to10-29. Before scientific exposure, the four-cluster branches were removed and the matrix was revised to include an eight-cluster no-preservation control plus dual-support tolerance variants. Smoke01 is retained separately and is not scientific evidence.

Smoke02/03 on the revised13-arm candidate reproduced exactly across byte-identical builds. The broad preservation arms still traded too much plasticity for support protection. Before development, a final orthogonal occupancy-routing family was therefore added to test whether explicit old-support region vetoes can reduce leakage without relying on more output-preservation rejection.
