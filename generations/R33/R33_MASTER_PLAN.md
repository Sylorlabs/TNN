Status: first-deliverable program. Canonical R27 remains unchanged.

## Operating hierarchy

The [current state](R33_CURRENT_STATE.json) names the active work, the
[experiment registry](R33_EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.json) owns experiment identity and
execution permission, and the [consumed-evidence registry](R33_CONSUMED_EVIDENCE_REGISTRY.json)
owns freshness. Neither a plan nor a favorable review grants execution authority.
The [journal](R33_EXECUTION_JOURNAL.md) records changes and the
[handoff](R33_HANDOFF.md) explains the next justified action.

## First batch

The first batch, [R33-B000](R33_B000_PREREGISTRATION.md), is a bounded, non-training audit of representation boundaries,
trace saturation, and protected-memory behavior. It consumes no historical
worlds and cannot promote a learner. Independent architecture, training, and
safety reviews precede its preregistration. Its native witnesses may demonstrate
an existing deficiency; a passing audit harness is not a passing sensory gate.
The registry distinguishes preregistered protocol, source-frozen executable,
running, completed, invalid, and blocked states. All three
[independent reviews](R33_INDEPENDENT_REVIEWS.md) are integrated; the audit source
has passed exact-copy checks and native compilation, without fixture execution.
The [completion record](R33_FIRST_DELIVERABLE.md) covers the first deliverable,
not completion of the eighteen-stage research program.

## No-repeat and freshness protocol

Before assigning any experiment, inspect this registry, R32's master ledger,
the relevant earlier result/preregistration, and the inherited evidence index.
Search mechanisms and causal questions, not only generation labels. E51M/N
already tested calibration dose/capacity; E51AD already tested a preservation-
constrained router; AH/AI/AJ already tested related residual/replay designs.

Identity must include hypothesis, changed variable, parent checkpoint, cognitive
source hash, evaluator source, data-generator version, effective RNG-domain and
world identities, train/dev/validation/confirmation split, schedule, controls,
authority, and measurement definitions. A new seed number is not by itself
independent evidence. Unknown or previously inspected populations are quarantined
from fresh validation. Reproduction of archival analysis is labeled reproduction,
not new learning evidence. No silent release of reserved sealed partitions.

Each run leaves preregistration, exact source/configuration, changed-variable,
trainer-input and hardcoding ledgers, authority grant, evidence identities,
status, full results including negatives, reproduction/analysis, resource report,
architecture diff, consumed-data update, next rationale and handoff update.
Allocation is single-writer; no subagent launches experiments.

## Stopping and promotion

Abort safely on trace loss, identity mismatch, forbidden information flow,
checkpoint inconsistency, protected-write attempt, resource exhaustion or
unaccounted data exposure. Preserve partial logs and quarantine touched evidence.
Performance stopping rules are preregistered, not selected after seeing a peak.

R27 dominance retains the obligations in
[the historical dominance program](R32_R27_DOMINANCE_PROGRAM.md), with R33 as
candidate and human-trainer terminology corrected. Frozen benchmarks do not
replace fresh-process continuation or new sealed challenge populations. Gains
cannot average away regressions, loss of transparency, or extra teacher help.
