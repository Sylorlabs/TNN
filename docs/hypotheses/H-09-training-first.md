# H-09 — Training-first diagnosis; hardcoding minimized and ledgered

## Claim
When a result is weak, diagnose training first (data, lesson quality,
contrasts, curriculum, rehearsal, teacher) and change architecture only for a
measured plateau. Hardcoding is allowed only for core infrastructure/safety or
with strong controlled evidence; every hardcoded component is recorded with its
justification; hardcoded teachers are an acceptable special case *only* as
attributed teaching aids whose knowledge must never be copied into the learner
as answer tables — and teacher dependence must be measured after withdrawal.
(User's explicit preference, recorded 2026-08-20.)

## Why it matters
It's the program's defense against architecture-churn and against smuggling
answers into the learner.

## Evidence
