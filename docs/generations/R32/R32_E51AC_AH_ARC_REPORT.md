# R32 E51AC–E51AH — Preservation and Residual-Learning Arc

Date: 2026-09-04 Pacific.

Scope: six experiment identifiers, E51AC through E51AH. This is a synthesis of
the identified historical reports plus the newly verified E51AH artifact, not
a new execution or independent re-audit of every historical artifact. E51AF
is a zero-execution integrity closure, not a learned-treatment negative.
R27 remains canonical and Baseline V1 is unchanged.

## What was known before this arc

The mature terminal learner had achieved exact full-tape reachability, but this
did not solve resource-feasible action support or online stopping. A direct
candidate learner supplied some complementary behavior. The central question
became whether those mechanisms could be combined and extended without
destroying already available successful behavior.

## What the arc established

| Experiment | Identified evidence | Supported conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E51AC | Run `33371199421`; `R32_E51AC_RESULT.md` | Direct support is complementary. The deployable hybrid reached 5,213 versus 5,156 for the mature controller, but lost 22 no-unique successes; the evaluator union still missed 140 known trajectories. |
| E51AD | Run `33412828590`; `R32_E51AD_RESULT.md` | The tested conservative router preserved its development subset but failed its held-out comparison: local-384 reached 5,175 versus 5,204 for score-max and 5,247 for the evaluator union. Routing cannot create support outside the fixed union. |
| E51AE | Run `33452596868`; `R32_E51AE_RESULT.md` | Residuals rescued 230 development misses but lost 509 union successes. Their reported validation total was 5,132 versus 5,260 for the union. The tested candidate correction was not a loss-free repair. |
| E51AF | `R32_E51AF_RESULT.md`; execution zero | Historical source/ledger prerequisites did not match. The instance is permanently invalid, with no partition-sensitivity or treatment-performance conclusion. |

| E51AH | Run `33944536498`, artifact `9963143498`; `R32_E51AH_RESULT.md` | Adding the specified grounded replay changed development behavior but neither replay arm preserved all 12,622 union successes. Local replay rescued 159 and lost 121; validation and confirmation correctly stayed sealed. |

The numbers in different rows refer to different stages, populations, and
controls. They are not a cross-experiment learning curve. E51AH supplies no
held-out comparison against E51AG, because stages 109/110 did not execute.

## What genuinely improved, and what did not

Complementary direct-candidate support was observed in E51AC, but safe
deployable composition was not established. E51AH's replay treatments retained
more development union successes than its critical-only controls. The local
replay arm's total was 12,660 versus the evaluator union's 12,622, a net gain of
38 on development. That is not a successful preservation repair: 121 prior
successes were lost, and the predeclared gate required zero losses.

No E51AH transfer claim is available. E51AG supplies same-generator fresh-world
replication of a negative, not distribution-shift or cross-task generalization.
Frozen controller parameters also do not demonstrate continual retention:
the deployed residual policy can override correct frozen behavior despite
unchanged controller hashes.

## Hypotheses closed and alternatives still open

The tested conservative router did not recover the frozen support on held-out
data. The current critical-only residual failed preservation and reproduced that
failure across fresh partitions. E51AH now closes the more specific hypothesis
that this one-record-per-trajectory grounded replay treatment, at the existing
features, targets, sampler, doses, and residual geometry, suffices for exact
development preservation with nonzero rescue.

It does not close preservation credit as a mechanism family. Remaining
explanations include omitted information in the current state, inadequate use
of information already present, selection of too few or unsuitable trajectory
states, an objective misaligned with worst-case trajectory preservation,
optimization limitations, and routing/conditional-geometry limitations.
The next experiment should separate these explanations rather than assume
that memory is the missing ingredient.

An additional evidence boundary matters: historical E51AE/E51AG oracle counters
assigned success by construction. Their learned/control comparisons remain
usable, but the oracle counters are not independent proof of action
expressivity. E51AH fixed its own oracle before execution; that held-out branch
was not executed. Do not turn a code correction into an observed ceiling result.

## What should remain frozen

Preserve the E51AH scientific commit, preregistration, thresholds, raw ledger,
native binaries, artifact ZIP, and explicit zero-exposure record. Retain all
negative results and the E51AF invalid-instance record. The E51X–E51AH master
ledger is a frontier index, not a claim that all earlier E51 work was reindexed
or rerun. Baseline V1 and R27 canonical status remain unchanged.

Do not repeat the same residual recipe on more consumed partitions merely to
obtain a favorable score, rehabilitate E51AF by changing its prerequisites,
or open E51AH validation after seeing a favorable net development total.
Do not resume compiler recovery: the required native pathway executed and its
artifact passed both identity and scientific verification.

## Highest-information next step

Next-stage planning is a proposal, not a frozen preregistration or a launched
experiment. The most useful next discriminator should first measure what the
present learner-visible state and action interface can support on a fresh,
separately reserved development population, without granting the learner any
evaluator metadata. Score evaluator-only candidate choices through their actual
grounded consequences; do not manufacture an exact oracle counter.

Then distinguish a current-state fitting limitation from genuine incremental
history information. Any short-context treatment needs both a capacity-matched
current-state control and a destroyed/shuffled-history control, with matching
targets, data counts, budgets, action interface, and preservation gates. Existence
of feature differences is not proof that the current learner can exploit them;
absence of exact collisions is not proof that memory is unnecessary. A history
gain without its ablations could be a capacity or sampling effect.

Choose and pin the actual implementation, new identifier, fresh world/domain
ranges, primary comparison, doses, and failure/confirmation rules before any
new validation exposure. This report does not reserve a new stage or authorize
reuse of E51AH's sealed populations. The experiment should remain a bounded
causal diagnostic; topology and protected-core redesign are not yet justified.

## Arc-level conclusion

The arc separates useful candidate support from the ability to deploy it
safely. Training on grounded preservation examples changed the residual fit,
but this replay treatment still traded away previously reachable successes.
The next research question is how to represent and fit that preservation
constraint while discriminating safe corrections—not whether a larger net
development total can replace the frozen safety criterion.

The broader E51 program remains open. This arc establishes bounded causal
closures, not safe stopping, general memory, composition, lifelong learning,
integrated autonomy, or R32 promotion.
