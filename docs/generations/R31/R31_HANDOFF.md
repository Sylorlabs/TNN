# R31 Handoff — Endogenous Chunking + Native Zag Development

R31 should begin by correcting R30's speech abstraction. The main question is no longer how accurately TNN predicts an externally fixed motif/token sequence. The question is whether TNN can discover useful temporal chunks directly from raw sensory streams and use those chunks for grounding, prediction, memory, communication, and action.

## Binding architecture constraints

- No transformers, LLM wrappers, BPE, fixed tokenizer, next-token objective, or externally supplied linguistic unit inventory in TNN cognition.
- Human word/phoneme/boundary annotations are evaluator-only microscopes.
- No supplied VAD or speech boundaries.
- No graph cognition.
- Promotable mechanisms execute in native Zag.
- Continue exact developmental lineage; zero newborn restart.
- Any exact 0%/100% result triggers the extreme-score diagnostic battery.

## Phase 0 — Native Zag authority

Recover/clone official `Sylorlabs/zag`, persist a non-zero verified `znc`, hash/provenance it, compile probes, compile R31 twice, check deterministic output, checked/release parity, and persist the compiler artifact immediately.

## Phase 1 — Recover and port the successful Adaptive Motif lineage

Port/reconcile the old Adaptive Motif, motif confidence, motif-gated temporal workspace, Grounded Contrastive Schema Memory, support-gap recruitment, reversible longest-match cells, context-indexed exact spans, shadow replay, and rollback mechanisms into the current graph-free R30/R31 Zag branch.

The recovered evidence to reproduce first:
- exact reversible learned segmentation;
- chunks frequently cross human boundaries;
- held-out raw-stream compression without information loss;
- motif-gated workspace improves multi-event/reference reasoning;
- support-gap recruitment permits one/few-shot novel grounded chunks without rewriting established knowledge.

## Phase 2 — Acoustic self-chunking tournament

Feed raw continuous waveform only. Compare matched variants:

1. raw sample/recurrent baseline;
2. fixed-width acoustic windows;
3. fixed anonymous CTC unit inventory from R30 (control);
4. adaptive variable-duration acoustic motifs;
5. overlapping adaptive motifs;
6. reversible longest-match motifs;
7. support-gap acoustic recruitment;
8. hierarchical motifs (micro -> meso -> construction);
9. grounded contrastive motifs selected by entity/action/effect consequences;
10. predictive motifs selected by future sensory reduction;
11. memory-value motifs selected by future retrieval/regret;
12. Foundry-generated chunking mechanisms using only generic non-graph primitives;
13. hybrids of alignment + adaptive motifs where CTC-style monotonic alignment aligns self-discovered chunks rather than defining them.

All variants receive the exact same raw waveforms, training dose, speakers, corruption, Master budget, memory budget, and hidden tests.

## Phase 3 — What makes a good chunk?

Do not hand-code the answer. Give TNN competing endogenous objectives and measure downstream capability:

- reversible reconstruction / information preservation;
- predictive compression;
- stability across speaker/view/noise transformations;
- discriminative grounding to entities/actions/consequences;
- reuse across contexts;
- future memory/retrieval utility;
- causal intervention utility;
- communication utility between independent siblings;
- resource cost;
- interference/regression cost.

Run single-objective and learned multi-objective chunk policies. Let delayed regret revise chunk boundaries, split motifs, merge motifs, or abandon motifs.

## Phase 4 — Chunk dynamics, not a vocabulary

Chunks must be mutable structures, not a permanent vocabulary. TNN can:
- recruit;
- extend/shorten;
- split;
- merge;
- overlap;
- specialize by context;
- form hierarchical constructions;
- decay/archive unused chunks;
- restore exact episodes when compressed chunks fail;
- create a new chunk after an unsupported span is grounded.

Track the full causal history for every chunk operation.

## Phase 5 — Evaluation without token accuracy

Primary metrics:
- raw-stream exact/reconstruction error;
- information lost by chunking;
- number/length distribution of learned chunks;
- chunk reuse and stability across transformations;
- chunk split/merge/regret rates;
- grounded entity/action/effect discrimination;
- unseen sequence/composition transfer;
- speaker/noise/coarticulation transfer;
- long-delay retrieval;
- one-shot support-gap transfer;
- sibling communication success;
- downstream meaning/action success;
- active-observation efficiency;
- compute/memory cost;
- full integrated scenario success.

Evaluator-only microscope metrics may report how often learned motifs happen to align with human phonemes/words, but alignment is never the objective.

## Phase 6 — Long R31 training

Run a genuinely long native developmental life. Checkpoints at approximately 1k, 4k, 10k, 25k, 50k, 100k, 250k, and continue while learning remains strong. Chunking, memory, world model, speech perception, grounding, Master policy, and architecture search all continue learning in the same brain.

At each checkpoint run counterfactuals:
- same sound different context;
- different sound same referent;
- same learned chunk spoken by new speaker;
- human word boundary shifted while meaning unchanged;
- chunk boundary destroyed by coarticulation;
- a recurring span stops being predictive;
- two chunks become better represented as one;
- one chunk becomes polysemous and should split;
- exact episode later proves a compression/chunk decision harmful;
- new grounded concept appears inside a familiar construction;
- sibling uses a different chunking history for the same world.

## Phase 7 — Master becomes chunk-aware but never supplies chunks

The Master can select experiences that expose confusions, contrasts, and information gaps, but may not provide word/phoneme/chunk boundaries. Capacity-aware teaching must preserve diversity and not flood episodic/chunk memory with repeated hard examples.

## Phase 8 — Cross-modal chunk emergence

Test whether useful chunks emerge across:
- acoustic spans;
- visual event fragments;
- action sequences;
- multimodal episodes.

A strong system may discover different chunk scales for different modalities. Do not force a universal linguistic segmentation.

## Phase 9 — Foundry and architecture learning

Let TNN create new non-core chunking PAMs from generic primitives. Contextual architecture-history retrieval is keyed to failure telemetry. Random/evolution remain controls. Any learned search loss to random triggers credit/context investigation before abandoning learning.

## Phase 10 — Native integrated qualification

One uninterrupted graph-free life with self-chunking, episodic identity, action/effect learning, adaptive memory, names, raw speech, siblings, teacher withdrawal, changed physics, long delays, save/reload, adversarial confidence, and self-revision.

Promotion requires native Zag execution, exact lineage/state continuity, no hidden tokenizer/VAD/boundary leakage, regression parity, trace integrity, sealed hidden qualification, and clean-room reproducibility.
