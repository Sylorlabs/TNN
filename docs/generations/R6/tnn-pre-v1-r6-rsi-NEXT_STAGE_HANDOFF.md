# Exact R6 continuation handoff

Continue from the selected R6 state and protected Grounded Contrastive Schema
Memory. Do not restart from a newborn state, restore the rejected broad novelty
branch, or relabel bounded one-shot results as teenager English.

## Priority 1 — saturation-aware self-directed learning

Use R6's exposure curve as the failure signal. Let the brain estimate marginal
information gain, source independence, cross-modal agreement, and replay
stability. Shadow-test policies for:

- consolidate now;
- request one more example;
- re-inspect the sensors;
- preserve alternatives;
- stop learning this motif;
- split a context-specific meaning.

The accepted policy must improve delayed retention without reducing one-shot
learning or established-language regression.

## Priority 2 — repair the grounding bottleneck

R6's new-label update uses the brain's own video/audio/action estimate, but that
estimate is not always exact. Add active reinspection and per-modality consensus.
A low-agreement episode should produce abstention or a targeted request rather
than a confident wrong binding.

## Priority 3 — natural developmental media

Freeze the selected learner and expose it to continuous natural video, raw
speech, environmental audio, demonstrator action, and controlled caption timing.
No pretrained OCR, speech-to-text, vision model, transformer, or LLM may supply
TNN cognition. Evaluator-only annotations are allowed for scoring.

## Priority 4 — emergent logical operators

Create consequences that require the same shared brain to discover reusable
structure for absence, polarity, conditionals, counterfactual branches,
universals, counterexamples, ambiguity, and source conflict. Do not add named
English modes.

## Priority 5 — learned generation and dialogue

Train a reversible production path from shared event/world representations to
raw speech and text. Require explanation, questions, correction, narrative,
and delayed conversation. External language generation is not permitted.

## Priority 6 — expand recursive self-improvement

Replace the finite candidate list with a compositional mutation grammar that can
create new local operators, memory organizations, sensory policies, and training
policies. The verifier and rollback mechanism must remain independent of any
candidate under test.

## Priority 7 — preregistered human comparison

Only after the natural, frozen learner passes the task battery should the matched
human study in `HUMAN_COMPARISON_PROTOCOL.md` begin.
