# R33 structural plasticity and hierarchical Foundry

Status: roadmap; execution requires [milestone qualification](R33_AUTHORITY_MILESTONE_LADDER.md).
No new module is live and no protected root is modified by this plan.

## Representation freedom within current constraints

Graph-free candidate families include associative banks, local experts/cells,
recurrent fields, executable processes, temporal kernels and shared workspaces.
Generic primitives may support weights, gates, routing, memory associations and
generated links without a mandatory world-knowledge graph. Do not force a
one-to-one analogy to biological neurons or synapses.

TNN proposals arise from its failure/regret and experiment history. A researcher-
selected controlled candidate remains a researcher-controlled arm, not proof of
autonomous architecture invention. Human domain answers, evaluator labels and
handwritten task routes remain prohibited.

## Progression

M2 creates shadow candidates; M3 allows bounded verified routing. M4 introduces
hierarchical motifs: a small program specifies regions, repeat counts, local
connectivity, sharing, sparsity and activation rules. Materialization can create
thousands or millions of effective interactions without asking a central
executive to choose every edge. The motif, expansion seed, version, counts and
instance ancestry are inspectable and reproducible.

Report possible/effective, materialized and active connections separately.
Count shared parameters once in storage but every executed use in computation.
Validate expansion bounds before allocation and charge lazy expansion when it
occurs. A declared million-link motif is not a measured million-connection
capability or a million independently learned parameters.

M5 learns a proposal policy from prior proposals, training costs, failures,
ablations, regressions and outcomes. Compare with random sampling, plain
evolution and fixed resource allocation. Hold out failure families so the
proposer cannot merely recall a previously successful program. M6 allows bounded
non-core rule proposals under the same protected gate; M7 integrates previously
qualified capabilities over a continuing life.

## Acceptance

Every proposal follows isolated construction, training, regression/resource
evaluation, protected canary, promotion, monitoring and rollback. Learner-written
internal tests can inform proposal selection but cannot replace the root
verifier. Never expose sealed assessment results as proposal-training history
while still treating them as held-out evidence.

Falsifiers: gains require manual task routing; no improvement over random at
matched search cost; untracked mutable state; failure after reloading; loss of
prior skills; resource growth without capability; failed canary/rollback; or
unauthorized scope expansion. Preserve failed architectures in the history.
