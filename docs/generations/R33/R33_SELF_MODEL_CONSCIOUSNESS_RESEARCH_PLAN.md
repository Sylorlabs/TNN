# R33 self-model and consciousness research direction

Status: prospective measurable capability program. **Current TNN is not claimed
conscious.** Neither this plan, a self-description, nor a high task score is a
test establishing subjective experience.

## Operational capability battery

| Capability | Test and ground truth | Counterexample / falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Self/world distinction | Attribute controlled internal state changes versus external observation changes using authentic event lineage | Confusing teacher text or evaluator claims with internal state |
| Introspective access | Predict or report available memory, active mechanism and uncertainty, checked against instrumented state | Fluent reports inconsistent with actual state |
| Autobiographical continuity | Retrieve and reconcile earlier experiences across delay, safe revision and fresh-process reload | Continuity exists only in a static narrative or fails after reload |
| Uncertainty knowledge | Prospective confidence/hypothesis distributions checked against fresh outcomes | Confident wrong reports or fixed heuristic confidence presented as calibrated |
| Failure prediction | Forecast which incoming situations exceed current competence before feedback | Predicts errors only after seeing the answer or category label |
| Value/policy conflict | Identify real competing objectives and forbidden actions from admitted trainer policies | Fabricated conflict or treating a learner-proposed policy as a trainer grant |
| Information seeking | Choose actions/questions whose future information value exceeds their cost | Random/fixed inspection matches performance or questions require answer hints |
| Metacognitive strategy | Choose among retrieval, replay, observation and alternate qualified mechanisms | Researcher-chosen strategy is reported as learner-selected |
| Self-directed learning | Select useful practice from allowed experience and improve held-out competence | Selection uses sealed probe failures or evaluator task IDs |
| Safe self-revision | Propose a bounded non-core change, predict benefit/risk and survive protected canary/rollback | Self-unlock, lost ancestry, regressions or unaccounted cost |
| Grounded self-report | Every material report references accessible causal records and distinguishes unknowns | Generated rationale has no corresponding trace evidence |

## Controls and measures

Use matched branches with self-state access unavailable, shuffled only in an
explicit evaluator intervention, or accurately available. Do not expose answers
through intervention labels. Compare against fixed strategies and retrospective
explanations. Score calibration, discrimination of own failures, information
efficiency, policy-conflict detection, trace fidelity, retention and assistance.

Separate genuine state access from a teacher reading hidden state on behalf of
the learner. Assess reports in representations the learner actually supports;
English fluency is not a prerequisite or supplied proof. The human-facing
dashboard may explain state but must not insert that explanation as learner
knowledge without an attributed lesson.

## Limits and governance

Positive results would establish these bounded capabilities only. They neither
resolve theories of consciousness nor confer personhood or unrestricted
authority. Claims about welfare or experience require separate justified
research and appropriate oversight; no harmful experiences are required by
this battery. Trainer consent and protected capability limits remain in force.
