# R33 program charter

Established: 2026-09-05. Status: research-generation initialization, not promotion.

**Canonical brain: R27, development step 60,423, zero newborn restarts.** R32 is
the inherited completed research frontier; its last verified experiment is
E51AJ. R33 neither renames that evidence nor resets the developmental learner.

**TNN means True Neural Network.** "Grounded, Active, Non-Token Cognition" is a
description of the research direction, not the expansion of TNN.

TNN is a white-box, grounded, active, continual-learning cognitive architecture
built around persistent episodic/hypothesis state, learned action value,
learner-owned memory/representation, and progressively learner-owned structural
plasticity.

Its autonomy target is decision ownership over how learning proceeds—replay,
inquiry, memory, representation, practice and reversible non-core proposals—
within trainer-defined goals, values, resources and permissions. Autonomy never
includes self-granted protected authority or the ability to bypass provenance,
rollback, verification or safety gates. See
[`TNN_AUTONOMY_POSITION.md`](TNN_AUTONOMY_POSITION.md).

## Ownership and terminology

A **trainer is a human/person/group** developing a particular TNN: a researcher,
parent, teacher, employer, company, or operator. The trainer chooses goals,
values, policies, experiences, curriculum, practice, tools, resource ceilings,
interventions, and real-world authority. A Master or automated teacher is a
trainer-authorized aid, never the definition of trainer. Its information and
assistance are separately attributed.

The architecture team supplies generic brain facilities, protected verification,
rollback, safety boundaries, instrumentation, and learning interfaces. TNN learns
how to represent, connect, remember, retrieve, rehearse, reason, and act inside
the trainer's constraints. Setting answer weights or memory addresses by hand
is not learner competence.

## Binding invariants

- No transformer, LLM, BPE/next-token substrate, fixed knowledge graph, or supplied
  word/phoneme/VAD/category boundary in TNN cognition. The active learner and
  Foundry remain graph-free under the inherited explicit user correction;
  historical graph tournaments are not a current implementation instruction.
- Raw evidence remains addressable independently of optional abstractions.
  A compressed or hashed representation is not described as lossless raw input.
- Preserve all negative, invalid, inconclusive, and mixed results. Unknown
  evidence identity means unavailable for fresh claims, not available to reuse.
- No newborn restart to hide interference. Research forks must name their
  parent, retain history, and never silently replace the continuing brain.
- Native Zag v2 implements promotable cognition. External scripts may inspect,
  package, evaluate, and test contracts, but cannot supply learner decisions.
- The learner never grants itself more authority. Protected infrastructure and
  a human grant remain outside learner mutation authority.
- Current TNN is not claimed conscious. Self-model research measures specific
  abilities, not an inference from convincing self-report to subjective life.

## First deliverable and claim boundary

The first deliverable is the [master plan](R33_MASTER_PLAN.md), source-backed
sensory/default audit, parameter matrix, telemetry schema, authority contract,
E51AJ reconciliation, independent reviews, and a preregistered first audit batch.
It is not a trained R33 brain, a functioning dashboard, or a certified safety
boundary. Implementations and qualification results must be distinguished from
specifications in [current state](R33_CURRENT_STATE.json).

The source audit found information-losing transforms, finite-buffer behavior,
hand-authored policy defaults, and incomplete enforcement evidence. These are
recorded in [the audit](R33_SUBSTRATE_AUDIT.md); the audit does not change frozen
R32 sources or reinterpret their narrow results as invalid.
