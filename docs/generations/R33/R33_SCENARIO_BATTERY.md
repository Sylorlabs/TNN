## Evidence discipline

Training and probes differ at world/subject/context level, not merely shuffled
rows. Save group identity and exact overlap audit in the evaluator domain.
Counterfactual views of the same world are paired observations, not independent
replicas. No category, mode or hidden ambiguity label enters learner features.

Every family reports counts and worst required slices, not only means. Integrated
success means all required parts worked for the episode, while component results
remain visible to localize failure. Preserve failed probes as consumed evidence.
After nominal perfect performance, test a previously sealed harder family.

Qualification runs may observe fixed checkpoints but cannot choose stopping,
architecture or help from sealed feedback. Split training-development selection,
fresh validation, sealed confirmation and harder challenge roles explicitly.

Long-life duration is chosen after measured learning/retention curves justify
it. A large event count alone is not developmental progress. Final R27 comparison
requires every retained parent test, plus new independent scenarios, without
quietly widening resources or trainer support.
