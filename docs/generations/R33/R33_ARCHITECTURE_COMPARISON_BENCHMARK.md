# R33 fair architecture-family benchmark

Status: future benchmark design; no competitor selected or evaluated yet.
Do not compare a convenient TNN build with a cherry-picked old model and claim
an architecture victory. This plan names families, not current product rankings.

## Families and budgets

Compare dense transformers, mixture-of-experts transformers, recurrent/state-
space systems, memory-augmented agents, conventional recurrent RL, neuro-symbolic
or tool systems, associative-memory systems, and other relevant baselines.
External comparator cognition is not incorporated into TNN.

For each family use multiple viable implementations/budgets where practical,
with comparable training effort and tuning opportunity. Freeze implementation
versions, pretraining history, precision, data access, tools and resource limits
before fresh evaluation. Pretrained and from-experience-only regimes are separate
panels; undisclosed pretraining cannot be treated as equal experience.

## Matching ledger

Report training data/experience, training compute, active inference compute,
stored capacity, memory bytes, sensory information, tool/action opportunities,
human/teacher help and search/tuning effort. Mark each axis MATCHED, BOUNDED,
ESTIMATED, MISMATCHED or UNKNOWN with its measurement method. Do not discard
an inconvenient mismatch from the headline.

Use separate equal-experience, equal-compute, equal-memory and capability-matched
frontiers. Compare the same sensory information, not TNN raw observations against
competitor hand-labeled objects, or the reverse. An architecture may have its own
input representation, but its information advantage must be disclosed.

## Outcomes

Task capability, learning speed, changed-context transfer, continual adaptation,
retention/forgetting, active evidence efficiency, human intervention burden,
memory and compute efficiency, traceability, safe self-revision and robustness.
Report full curves, seed/group distributions and failed runs. Correlated frames
do not inflate independent sample counts.

TNN's explicit state is assessed by reconstruction/causal-trace coverage, not by
the persuasiveness of its explanation. Comparator explanations are assessed by
the same state-evidence principle where available; unavailable internals are a
measured limitation, not proof of zero capability.

## Decision rule

Publish Pareto frontiers under named budgets and floors. Report where each
family is stronger, weaker or incomparable. Include competitive tuning and
reasonable contemporary implementations at execution time, checked against
primary documentation then. Do not select winners on exposed confirmation data.

Architecture-family advantages do not waive R27 retention or promotion gates.
A specific benchmark win is neither general intelligence nor consciousness.
