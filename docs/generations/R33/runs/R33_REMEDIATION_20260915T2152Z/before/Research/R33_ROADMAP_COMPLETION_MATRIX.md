## Bottom line

The roadmap has real bounded progress, especially in native custody, telemetry,
encoded-file information preservation, registry discipline, and the synthetic
N16 mechanism qualification. It does not yet have a qualified continuing R27
descendant, a complete native learner runtime, a natural sensory/perception
qualification, or an R33-versus-R27 result. The next admissible critical path
is therefore parent continuity and native runtime/evaluator closure; later
workstreams remain design-only until their stated prerequisites are met.
