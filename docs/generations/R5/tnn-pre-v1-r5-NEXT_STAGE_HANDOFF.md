# Exact continuation handoff

Continue from the R5 canonical source and accepted training decision. Do not
restart from a newborn state, return to a hand-authored parser, or call the
controlled scores teenager English.

## Priority 1 — one-shot motif binding without interference

The next SAR tournament should compare generic mechanisms that can infer a new
surface span from a grounded experience while preserving all established
meanings:

- support-gap recruitment using explicit motif confidence;
- reversible longest-match cells rather than additive substring voting;
- context-indexed exact spans;
- shadow replay against old constructions and delayed interference;
- rollback on any established-language or sensory regression.

The promotion battery must include visually similar novel labels, shared byte
fragments, new framing, delayed recall, contradictory teachers, and unrelated
old vocabulary.

## Priority 2 — expand discourse without language rules

Extend the promoted temporal workspace to:

- several events and several entities;
- references that are clear, ambiguous, or intentionally unresolvable;
- question intent learned from consequences rather than fixed question enums;
- explicit abstention when local evidence cannot identify a unique continuation;
- source/provenance retrieval through the same language pathway.

## Priority 3 — natural developmental media

Run the same frozen candidate on independent natural streams:

- continuous egocentric or instructional video;
- synchronized raw speech and environmental audio;
- action/effect records or demonstrator actions;
- text/captions introduced in controlled timing variants;
- no pretrained vision, OCR, speech-to-text, or LLM inside TNN cognition.

Retain evaluator-only annotations for scoring. TNN must discover its own entities,
boundaries, and useful motifs.

## Priority 4 — language operator growth

Do not add `NEGATION_MODE`, `CONDITIONAL_MODE`, or other named cognitive modes.
Instead create developmental consequences where competing relational dynamics
must distinguish:

- presence versus absence;
- actual versus hypothetical events;
- universal claims versus counterexamples;
- literal versus nonliteral consequences;
- known versus ambiguous references.

Then test whether stable reusable operators emerge and transfer.

## Priority 5 — persistence, speech, and generation

The next accepted state needs deterministic save/reload, multi-session learning,
a raw-waveform speech path, and a learned text/speech production mechanism.
Generation must be grounded in the same persistent representations; an external
language model wrapper is not an acceptable substitute.
