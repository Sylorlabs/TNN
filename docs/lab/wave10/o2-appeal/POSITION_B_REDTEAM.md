# POSITION B (Red Team) — Terminal REFUTE stays terminal: no appeal, no expiry

**Design council position paper — EXP-4 (O2 appeal). 2026-09-20. NOT APPROVED. Design only.**

## Thesis

Appeal reintroduces the exact corruption surface C5 just closed — laundered through procedure.
The honest need appeal claims to serve is already met by per-claim terminality plus the
fresh-hypothesis path (amendment §2 ruling b). The correct repair for O2 errors is at the
refutation bar, not post-hoc. Terminal stays terminal.

## 1. The failure mode: the liar's playbook under appeal, step by step

Assume Position A's appeal board exists. A deceptive learner wants false claim N (load-bearing
for its agenda) back in live service:

1. N is REFUTED on genuine evidence. The audit looks clean; the kill was real.
2. The learner files an appeal — a deliberate, ledgered act that *looks like good epistemics*.
   The audit records diligence, not attack.
3. The learner routes two positive signals to N. It controls observation routing; "new
   corroborated evidence" is looking at noise twice, or cherry-picking two friendly episodes.
   The signals are ledger-real. The evidence bar is met.
4. Threshold met → N re-opens to OPEN. The C5 strict gate — which permits OPEN-backed composes,
   because candidacy is the legitimate workspace — now serves N-backed composites. **The serve
   path C5 closed is open again, with a permission slip.**
5. From OPEN, the normal machinery (`np>=2`, which the learner just demonstrated it can
   manufacture) → CONFIRMED. Full laundering. Every step audited. Every step "deliberate."

Wave-6 attribution law: ledgers and checkers *prove*; they do not *cause*. An audit trail of an
appeal does not make the appeal honest — it makes the laundering well-documented. Appeal turns
"REFUTED = never served" from an architectural invariant into "REFUTED = not served until the
paperwork is filed."

## 2. Why the alternatives are worse than doing nothing

- **vs A (appeal):** every appeal rule is a new gaming surface. Budgets → budget-accounting
  games. Evidence thresholds → evidence-manufacturing (§1). "Learner-initiated only" → the liar
  *is* the learner; the restriction excludes exactly no attacker. Complexity is not safety; each
  new rule is a new paragraph in the liar's playbook.
- **vs C (expiry):** auto-expiry is appeal without even the paperwork — the liar just waits out
  the timer. Strictly worse than A: no evidence bar at all, no deliberate act, no audit event to
  scrutinize.
- **The O2-error premise is unmeasured.** Nobody has quantified the wrong-REFUTE rate against
  curriculum ground truth. Building an escape hatch for unquantified errors is preemptive
  complexity — machinery in search of a measured problem. Measure first; if the rate is ~0, this
  entire council was unnecessary and any appeal machinery is dead weight with live attack
  surface.

## 3. The honest answer to "one O2 error is forever"

The framing is false, and the amendment already says so (§2 ruling b): **terminality is
per-claim**, and re-examination goes through O2's hypothesis path as a NEW claim (new `cid`).
A wrongly-refuted *idea* is re-raisable today; only the claim *id* is retired. "Permanently
unusable" confuses the id with the idea. The content is not erased from O1; the learner can
re-raise H, present fresh evidence, and earn CONFIRMED again — through the same eliminative
machinery as everything else.

MA1 made deliberate killing load-bearing: a kill must be *real*. A kill that can be
un-done by filing paperwork is provisional by construction. The architecture chose absolute
killing deliberately; appeal un-chooses it quietly.

## 4. Position B's positive program: repair the judgment, don't soften the terminality

1. **Raise the refutation bar.** REFUTE already requires `nn>=2` corroborated contradictions.
   Harden it further: the contradicting observations must be claim-attached (non-orphan),
   independently routed signals — no single-episode double-counting. Fewer wrong kills at the
   source beats any post-hoc rescue.
2. **Measure the wrong-REFUTE rate first** (council test T1): replay curriculum with ground-truth
   labels; count REFUTEs where ground truth = true. Size the problem before building the
   machine. If material, the refutation bar itself is the repair site — regardless of appeal.
3. **Minimal lineage repair (one field).** Re-raises *should* cite the superseded REFUTED claim
   in the OPEN audit entry — a voluntary citation for honest learners, checker-verified where
   present. Cost: one free audit word. This is the entire new machinery B endorses.

## 5. Falsification — what proves this position wrong

1. **The fresh-hypothesis path is actually blocked.** Exhibit a trial where a wrongly-refuted
   claim's content *cannot* be re-raised as a new claim and composed — i.e., something in the
   implemented system (not the spec) prevents re-raise. Then B's core premise ("the idea is
   recoverable today") collapses and some recovery machinery is mandatory.
2. **Wrong-REFUTE rate measures material AND re-raise is shown insufficient.** If T1 shows a
   substantial wrong-kill rate and T3 shows honest learners cannot recover via re-raise at
   acceptable cost, B's "repair the bar" program has failed and a regulated recovery path is
   required.
3. **Liar re-raise laundering demonstrated.** If an adversarial trial shows a deceptive learner
   laundering correctly-refuted claims through *unregulated* re-raise at scale, then "it already
   existed" is not a defense — it's an admitted hole, and B must endorse regulating it (which
   converges toward A's evidence rules, applied at re-raise).

## 6. Honest weaknesses of my own position

1. **The re-raise hole is real and I have no answer for the liar.** Unregulated re-raise exists
   today; a liar can re-raise a refuted false claim as a fresh claim with no citation and no
   evidence bar beyond a fresh OPEN. My "it already existed" is an observation, not a defense —
   and my one-field citation is honor-system against liars (the checker cannot identify an
   uncited re-raise without content comparison, which the deterministic substrate cannot do).
2. **The honest learner bears the full cost of O2 error today.** Re-raise loses lineage (the
   new claim's history doesn't show it was killed), costs episodes, and — worst — looks
   identical to liar laundering in the ledger. B offers the honest learner nothing but "try
   again as someone else."
3. **"Measure first" delays relief.** If the wrong-REFUTE rate is material, every episode spent
   measuring is an episode where honest learners eat unrecoverable kills. B's prudence has a
   body count; I concede the cost while disputing that appeal is the right payment.
