## M1: F3a3 BUILD-PASS

Skeptic's provenance probe: What is the provenance of the artifacts
under judgment, and what exactly is new versus inherited? Answer: the
mechanism (proc_learn.zag, 847a8f10f) is inherited from the 2321pdt
wave, untouched. The prereg (53256838f) is new this wave. The bar text
is the third draft: F3a text failed on a fixture collision, F3a2 text
failed on unsatisfiability, F3a3 text passes.

Attack: three rounds, two bar-text failures, one pass. Each correction
was debated and authorized, and each was textual. But step back. The
final passing bar is the third draft of a test the mechanism's own
lineage helped motivate. The advocate says the mechanism was
exonerated twice. True. But exoneration on K-F3-2/3/4 while K-F3-1 was
being rewritten means the mechanism has never yet faced a FIRST-DRAFT
adversary bar it passed unchanged. Every behavioral pass happened on a
bar that had already been edited at least once. That is a weaker claim
than "the mechanism survived its adversary." It is "the mechanism
survived the third draft of its adversary's test."

The skeptic does not allege bad faith. The skeptic demands a standing
caveat: future adversary bar texts must pass a fixture-collision
pre-check BEFORE freezing, and ideally be authored by a party with no
stake in the mechanism. Also: byte v exhausted the declared disjoint
set {k,m,r,v}. The next round needs a fresh disjoint set, or the
adversary is just cycling.

## M2: F3b BUILD-PASS

Skeptic's provenance probe: What is new versus inherited? Answer: the
enumerator, terminals (K/N/C0/C1/C2), ops (ADD/SUB), smallest-first
search, and eval_prog are inherited from F3a. New: the length-program
discovery slot, length-parameterized apply, and the drop-last training
family.

Attack: the "discovery" is smallest-first search over a
researcher-fixed 1055-program menu. The terminals are fixed. The ops
are fixed. The length program found is [N C1 SUB], an affine function
of n, which is exactly the form the menu makes expressible. The
mechanism does not invent the form; it selects it. The classification
claimed is bounded L2+, and the skeptic accepts the bars passed, but
presses the label: is this L2 (new structure from generic mechanisms)
or L1-with-search (parameters of a supplied template)? The g program
contains N because T2 killed the constant shortcut; that is structural
response to training pressure, which leans L2. But the skeptic narrows
the adoption: bounded L2+ on the drop-last family ONLY. There is no
evidence of transfer to any other transformation family, and the
verdict must not be quoted as general procedure invention.

new prereg inheriting only motivation from v1.

audit as a precondition to implementation.
