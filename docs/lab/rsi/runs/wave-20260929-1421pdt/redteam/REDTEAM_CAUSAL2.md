### F5. Attack on the invention headline: PARTIALLY SUSTAINED, bar unaffected

The skeptic's strongest attack: P-B2a, the headline K2-1 probe, is answered
by exact-episode recall of seq12, not by applying the invented conditional
rule. The prereg's expectation ("H-PRESS adopted") is satisfied in the
letter (the probe is correct) and the split structure H4/H5 exists in the
hypothesis state, but a reader who takes "invents the rule" to mean "derives
the answer from the rule" is misled on this probe. Counterweight: two probes
are answered by rule application on unseen states (P-A2 via v0+=-1 rule,
P-B2c via the s1=0 rule on an unseen lamp value while the memorize baseline
withholds). The verdict rule as frozen is satisfied; the invention claim is
narrowed to: the learner constructs the conditional structure and applies it
to unseen states, while the confirming instance is recalled. This travels as
a mandatory caveat, not a bar failure.
