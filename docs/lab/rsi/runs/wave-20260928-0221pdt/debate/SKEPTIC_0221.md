## Attack on M1: EXP1c attempt 4 VOID

The VOID itself is not attackable (0/56 < 0.50 is arithmetic), but the
verdict line's framing is, on three points.

### 1a. The attempt-4 K6 operationalization is not a clean confound fix

The advocate calls selection-side exclusion the cleanest available
fix. The record contradicts that, and the contradiction sits inside
the wave's own documents.

The binding evidence-note caveat (2321pdt judge, carried into the
Zag-generated M3 note) says the ablation "excludes COMBINE-containing
sketches, which changes both the invention channel and the
exploration-risk profile; survival gains are fully explained by the
exploration-risk change and cannot be separated from it."

The attempt-4 diagnostics confirm the exploration-risk channel is
still the whole story:

- All 5 completed-enumeration runs are on arm 4 (I-invent): v=2, v=3,
  v=6, v=8, v=10 at enum_tick 1152, 1152, 1183, 1185, 1171. Zero
  completions on arm 3 or on either ablation arm.
- Ablation arms enumerate only 258 distinct sketches (n_distinct=258
  across every ablation-arm row), while the I-invent arm reaches 399.
  The "all 399 sketches retained" claim is about the static sketch
  set, not about what the agent actually explores: excluding COMBINE
  sketches from xi_pick means the ablation arm never tries them, never
  pays their exploration cost, and never risks their plans. The 258
  figure is 399 minus the 141 COMBINE-containing sketches.
- The ablation survival gain (1200 vs 431 on survive; 1068 vs 282 on
  invent) is therefore still fully explained by reduced exploration
  risk, exactly as the binding caveat says.

REDTEAM_ITER4 section 5 claims the re-freeze "fixed the 2321pdt
confound," but that self-review is explicitly uncertified per K5, and
a K5-uncertified self-claim cannot amend a judge's binding caveat
carried in the Zag-generated evidence note. The verdict must not be
read as certifying the K6 operationalization clean. The firing
(ablation 1200/1068 vs I 431/282) is a measurement from a voided run,
and per precedent it kills nothing and certifies nothing, including
the ablation design.

### 1b. The C3 distinctness-qualifier strike is necessary but not sufficient

The strike removes an unsupported gloss, which is honest. It does not
repair C3's informational content. Measured: f0 = 1200, f1 = 1200,
f2 = 1200, all three calibrators pinned at the 1200 ceiling. The
per-variant vector diffs (d01 = 11, d02 = 12, d12 = 12) are end-energy
differences at the ceiling, discriminating nothing about survival
capability; the verdict's own evidence note says exactly this. A
calibration gate whose every input sits at the ceiling cannot
calibrate anything: no broken agent could fail it. PASS is therefore
vacuous as a measurement of calibration quality. The advocate's
defense (the bar is literal and frozen) is true but is a defense of
the procedure, not of the gate's meaning. Under VOID this is moot for
adoption, but the judge should record the ceiling vacuity as a
measurement limitation so no future wave cites C3-0221 as evidence
that calibration "passed" in any substantive sense.

### 1c. The B0 finding means K7 was untestable by construction, and four attempts paid for it

Measured: post-enumeration bonus always >= 10% of selection weight;
post-enumeration selections 56, learned 0. Under the frozen M3, the
B0/(1+n) bonus structurally prevents learned dominance within 1200
ticks. That is a defect of the instrument, not evidence about
invention, and it means the K7 bar (>= 0.50 learned fraction) was
unreachable by design in attempts 1 through 4. Four attempts ran
48-run experiments each against a bar that could not pass. The cost
is real, and the verdict's "next queue item" must not authorize a
fifth attempt under any variant-family retune, nor may the loop
self-authorize the exploration/exploitation redesign. The judge should
bank two explicit questions to Micah: (1) whether the
exploration/exploitation redesign should proceed as a new design
direction, and (2) whether the K7 bar itself (0.50 learned fraction
within 1200 ticks) is attainable under any frozen M3 or needs
re-specification. Both are his frontier-adjacent design space; the
loop recommends, never decides. The verdict's "next queue item"
phrasing should be downgraded to a banked recommendation so no worker
reads it as authorized work.

### 1d. K1 and K6 firings on a voided run

No dispute with the verdict's handling: the firings (I-survive 431 <=
R 1200; ablation 1200/1068 vs I 431/282) are literal measurements from
