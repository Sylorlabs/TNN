## M1: against H-PI-REV2 BUILD-PASS

Provenance: prereg inherited (7c11ac5af); fixtures inherited; new this
wave are the implementation, the binary, the evidence, the audits.

S1 (researcher-added-primitive) is not answered by the evidence, only
restated. Micah's NOT-count list bars "the researcher adding a primitive
after a hidden failure," and here the researcher wrote a kit whose only
possible output is a byte-equality test at a position, then froze a
position-ascending rank that happens to select position 0, then tested
exclusively in first-letter worlds. The F2 "adversary" was the same
implementer picking the first letter of a set the implementer knew; the
prereg's own text even mused about "mab"-class, betraying that the
designer imagined first-letter worlds throughout. A genuinely
independent adversary has not touched this. The kit cannot diagnose a
counterexample needing any other primitive shape (S2, conceded), so
"revision" here means "first-letter specialization," a single trick.

S3 stands: the diagnosis space is 64 positions x 256 bytes, finite, and
the composed conditional is data-selected from a researcher-bounded menu.
S4 stands: position-ascending is an unprincipled salience prior that
matches the tester's intent; a second-letter world would need a
different bias, and none was tested.

Further: the K-RV2-5 regression leans on a vacuous pair
("hello"->"olleh" constrains nothing under the ported extractor), so
"discovers reverse" rests on three pairs, the same three that motivated
the design. The impossibility recheck (K-RV2-1b) replays the designer's
own search on the designer's own port; it is a consistency check, not an
independent proof. And the conflict rule's frozen consequence
("xy"->"xx") is asserted, not derived: nothing in the evidence shows
the learner had a principled reason to prefer overriding "xy" beyond
the frozen rule the researcher wrote.

The skeptic does not claim the bars were mismeasured; the numbers are
what they are. The skeptic claims the bars certify a bounded,
researcher-shaped trick, and that BUILD-PASS must be read with all five
standing attacks (S1-S5) attached as live kill vectors for the
pipeline, not as footnotes.

## M5: against queuing the pipeline next

Provenance: queue inherited; new is the concrete next step.

The skeptic argues the highest-information next step is not step 4
(independent reproduction, which will likely just re-confirm) but the
alternative-explanation attack (step 6) and a harder F2: an independent
adversary picking the byte AND a non-first-letter world (second-letter
or relational), which is the cheapest way to kill S1/S4. Reproduction
without a harder adversary is motion without information. The skeptic
would reorder: independent adversary first, then reproduction.
