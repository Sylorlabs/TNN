ID: P10
DOMAIN: algebra
TYPE: find_error
STATEMENT: Consider the following argument, which claims to prove that 1 = 2. Find the error and explain why the conclusion does not follow.

Let a and b be arbitrary numbers with a = b.
(1) a^2 = a*b            (multiply both sides by a)
(2) a^2 - b^2 = a*b - b^2  (subtract b^2 from both sides)
(3) (a+b)(a-b) = b(a-b)  (factor both sides)
(4) a+b = b              (divide both sides by (a-b))
(5) 2b = b               (since a = b)
(6) 2 = 1                (divide both sides by b)

Therefore 1 = 2.
