STATEMENT: A game show has three doors. Behind one door is a car; behind each of the other two is a goat. You pick one door. The host, who knows where the car is, then opens a different door from the one you picked, revealing a goat, and offers you the choice: stay with your original door, or switch to the remaining closed door. Should you switch or stay? Give the probability of winning the car if you stay and if you switch, with justification.
