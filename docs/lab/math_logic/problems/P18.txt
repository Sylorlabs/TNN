ID: P18
DOMAIN: geometry
TYPE: find_error
STATEMENT: Consider the following argument, which claims to prove that every triangle is isosceles. Find the error and explain why the conclusion does not follow.

In triangle ABC, let the internal bisector of angle A meet the perpendicular bisector of side BC at a point O. Drop perpendiculars from O to lines AB and AC, meeting them at D and E respectively. Triangles ADO and AEO are congruent (same hypotenuse AO, equal angles at A), so AD = AE and OD = OE. Since O is on the perpendicular bisector of BC, OB = OC, so the right triangles ODB and OEC are congruent (hypotenuse-leg), giving DB = EC. Hence AB = AD + DB = AE + EC = AC. So AB = AC, and ABC is isosceles with AB = AC.
