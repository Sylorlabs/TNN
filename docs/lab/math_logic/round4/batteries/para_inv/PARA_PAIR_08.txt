# PARA_PAIR_08

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_13
Kind: paraphrase

S1. Look at every representation of the square root of 2 in the form p/q where p and q are integers with q != 0, and select one whose positive denominator q is minimal.
S2. An odd integer squared is odd.
S3. Establish that the square root of 2 is irrational.

TARGET: The square root of 2 is irrational.
