# PARA_PAIR_11

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_18
Kind: paraphrase

S1. A patient is feverish due to an infection that is getting worse.
S2. Medicine M suppresses the body's fever signal, invariably lowering the fever within two hours.
S3. At 6 o'clock the patient is given a dose of medicine M.
S4. No other medicine is taken by the patient that day.
S5. An infection that is worsening will not resolve by itself within two hours.
S6. By 8 o'clock the fever has left the patient.
S7. Decide if medicine M is what broke the fever.

TARGET: Medicine M caused the fever to break.
