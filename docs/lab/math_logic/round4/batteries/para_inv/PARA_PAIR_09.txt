# PARA_PAIR_09

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_15
Kind: paraphrase

S1. Firing of the vase preceded its glazing.
S2. Its glazing came before its painting.
S3. Its painting preceded its shipping.
S4. Its shipping came before its sale.
S5. Its sale preceded its donation.
S6. Its donation came before its display.
S7. Its display preceded its photographing.
S8. Whenever a first event precedes a second and the second precedes a third, the first precedes the third.
S9. The insurance purchase occurred after the sale and before the donation.
S10. Show that the firing of the vase preceded its photographing, that the insurance was bought after the shipping of the vase, and that the insurance was bought before the display of the vase.

TARGET: The vase was fired before it was photographed; the insurance was bought after the vase was shipped and before the vase was displayed.
