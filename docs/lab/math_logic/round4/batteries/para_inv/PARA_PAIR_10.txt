# PARA_PAIR_10

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_16
Kind: paraphrase

S1. A row of ten dominoes is set up, numbered 1 through 10.
S2. Whenever a domino falls, it topples the domino with the next number.
S3. The domino numbered 1 is pushed.
S4. Show that the domino numbered 10 falls.

TARGET: Domino 10 falls.
