# PARA_PAIR_03

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_04
Kind: paraphrase

S1. Mira arranges coins into 20 rows, with 1 coin in row 1, 2 coins in row 2, continuing in this way until row 20 with 20 coins.
S2. Group the rows into pairs (1,20), (2,19), (3,18), (4,17), (5,16), (6,15), (7,14), (8,13), (9,12), (10,11), making 10 pairs total.
S3. Compute how many coins there are altogether.

TARGET: The total number of coins is 210.
