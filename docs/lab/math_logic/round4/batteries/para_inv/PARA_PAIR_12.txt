# PARA_PAIR_12

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_20
Kind: paraphrase

S1. Train A departs the depot at 8:00, takes 3 hours to reach Midtown, waits there for 20 minutes, then takes 2 hours to reach Harbor.
S2. Train B departs Midtown at 11:30 and needs 2 hours to get to Harbor.
S3. Demonstrate that train A arrives at Harbor ahead of train B, and determine the time difference.

TARGET: Train A reaches Harbor 10 minutes before train B.
