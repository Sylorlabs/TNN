# PARA_PAIR_06

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_10
Kind: paraphrase

S1. Triangle ABC is isosceles, with sides AB and AC equal.
S2. Let M be the midpoint of BC; thus M is on the segment BC.
S3. Angles AMB and AMC jointly form a straight angle, 180 degrees.
S4. Any triangle's three interior angles sum to 180 degrees.
S5. The measure of angle ABC is 65 degrees.
S6. Show that AM meets BC at a right angle, and compute angle BAM.

TARGET: AM is perpendicular to BC and angle BAM is 25 degrees.
