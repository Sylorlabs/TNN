# PARA_PAIR_05

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_08
Kind: paraphrase

S1. Consider triangle ABC, and let D bisect side AB and E bisect side AC.
S2. In triangle ADE, let F bisect side AD and G bisect side AE.
S3. Give the length FG as a function of the length BC, and describe the direction of FG relative to BC.

TARGET: FG equals BC/4 and FG is parallel to BC.
