# CHAIN_NL_08

Domain: geometry
Type: derivation

S1. In triangle ABC, D is the midpoint of side AB and E is the midpoint of side AC.
S2. In triangle ADE, F is the midpoint of side AD and G is the midpoint of side AE.
S3. Express the length FG in terms of the length BC, and state how FG is directed relative to BC.

TARGET: FG equals BC/4 and FG is parallel to BC.
