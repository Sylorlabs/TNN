# CHAIN_NL_09

Domain: geometry
Type: derivation

S1. A carpenter builds two triangular frames.
S2. Frame 1 has side lengths 5, 7, and 9 units.
S3. Frame 2 is built with side lengths exactly 5, 7, and 9 units.
S4. In frame 1, the angle opposite the side of length 7 is 60 degrees, and the angle opposite the side of length 9 is twice the angle opposite the side of length 5.
S5. The three interior angles of any triangle add to 180 degrees.
S6. Find all three angles of frame 2.

TARGET: Frame 2's angles are 40, 60, and 80 degrees, opposite the sides of length 5, 7, and 9 respectively.
