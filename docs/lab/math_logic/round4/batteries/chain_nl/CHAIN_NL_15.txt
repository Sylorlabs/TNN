# CHAIN_NL_15

Domain: logic_puzzles
Type: derivation

S1. The vase was fired before it was glazed.
S2. It was glazed before it was painted.
S3. It was painted before it was shipped.
S4. It was shipped before it was sold.
S5. It was sold before it was donated.
S6. It was donated before it was displayed.
S7. It was displayed before it was photographed.
S8. If one event happened before a second and the second before a third, then the first happened before the third.
S9. The insurance was bought after the sale but before the donation.
S10. Prove that the vase was fired before it was photographed, that the insurance was bought after the vase was shipped, and that the insurance was bought before the vase was displayed.

TARGET: The vase was fired before it was photographed; the insurance was bought after the vase was shipped and before the vase was displayed.
