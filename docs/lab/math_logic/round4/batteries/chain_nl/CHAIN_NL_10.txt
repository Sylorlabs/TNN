# CHAIN_NL_10

Domain: geometry
Type: derivation

S1. Triangle ABC is isosceles with AB equal to AC.
S2. M is the midpoint of side BC, so M lies on segment BC.
S3. The angles AMB and AMC together make a straight angle of 180 degrees.
S4. The three interior angles of any triangle add to 180 degrees.
S5. Angle ABC measures 65 degrees.
S6. Prove that AM is perpendicular to BC, and find angle BAM.

TARGET: AM is perpendicular to BC and angle BAM is 25 degrees.
