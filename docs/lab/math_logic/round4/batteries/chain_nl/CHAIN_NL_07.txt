# CHAIN_NL_07

Domain: geometry
Type: derivation

S1. A straight road crosses two parallel railway lines.
S2. At the upper crossing, the inside angle on the left between the road and the rail is 35 degrees.
S3. A triangular warning sign at the lower crossing has one corner cut to exactly match the inside angle on the right at the lower crossing, and another corner of 60 degrees.
S4. The three interior angles of any triangle add to 180 degrees.
S5. Find the sign's third corner.

TARGET: The sign's third corner is 85 degrees.
