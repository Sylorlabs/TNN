## Recurring killers (stable across all 4 rounds, both paradigms — 20 judges)

1. **Pasted-disc celestial bodies.** The planet/moon reads as a sticker in
   EVERY round under both paradigms: hard perfect-circle edge, no
   atmospheric haze at the rim, lighting inconsistent with the landscape.
2. **Repeating mountain templates.** Pinstripe extrusions, zigzag
   striation walls, fractal ridges, 15× stamped peaks — never eroded rock.
3. **Floating foreground rocks.** Disc-boulders with drop shadows, no
   contact, no perspective variation, hovering above the ground plane.
4. **Inconsistent light.** Elements lit by different suns; terminator
   disagrees with landscape shading.

## Direction for round 5

Keep the composed paradigm (won 3–2) but attack the four killers
directly: (a) the sky planet must be atmospherically integrated or
removed — extinction haze at the rim, no hard perfect-circle edge,
terminator consistent with the landscape sun; (b) mountains with real
erosion structure AND aerial perspective (round 3's haze was better —
keep it); (c) boulders grounded with contact shadows, varied sizes,
perspective; (d) a single verified light vector checked across every
element. Round 3's strengths (haze, unified light) must not be lost in
the composed rebuild.
