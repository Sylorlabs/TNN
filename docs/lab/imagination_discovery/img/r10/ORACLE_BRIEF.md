## What was diagnosed (third-party verdict: "looks like 2000s CG")
BEAUTY_DIAGNOSIS.md verifies 8 tells against crops of the frozen r8b
baseline. The three strongest: (B1) the horizon slab — a dead gray band
with razor edges pretending to be a sea; (B2) foreground crushed to
black — the sky-dome sample ignored the bright sunward horizon, the
physical reason shadowed ground went black; (B3) waxy specular streaks
and sparkle chains — a power-60 glint lobe firing on sub-pixel normal
noise, i.e. aliasing, not light.

## What changed — all from deliberated scene truth (BEAUTY_MECHANISMS.md)
- **B-LAKE** (LOGIC-DERIVED): the graben floods. The far lowland that
  read as a painted sea-band is now a real rift lake in the SDF —
  water plane where the tapered ground drops below the water table,
  wave-normal field, fresnel sky reflection from the deliberated dome,
  half-vector sun glitter toward the one sun, shadow-marched,
  dissolved by the same dust fog as the land. (Rift valleys on
  basaltic worlds flood below the water table — Tanganyika analog.
  Reconception, not a new asset.)
- **B-FILL** (LOGIC-DERIVED): ground skylight now integrates two lobes
  of the sky dome — the normal direction AND the bright sunward
  horizon band. Shadowed ground stops going black because the light
  was always there; the old code sampled only the zenith, the darkest
  part of the dusk sky.
- **B-GLINT** (LOGIC-DERIVED): the glint lobe weakens with distance
  (pixel-footprint integration). Near volcanic glass still glints; far
  sparkle is gone because it was aliasing.
- **B-AERIAL** (LOGIC-DERIVED): honest exponential scatter at the
  deliberated 0.6-bar dust load. Far ranges pale and lose contrast;
  the far plane dissolves instead of ending in an edge. This is the
  distance relationship: near reads near, far reads far.
- **B-MOON** (LOGIC-DERIVED): stronger planetshine (earthshine is real
  light transport); terminator stays razor — Cinder is deliberated
  airless, so no invented limb glow. The decline is documented.
- **B-CLOUD** (LOGIC-DERIVED): silver lining on sunward cirrus edges
  (forward scatter); wind-sheared filaments kept.
- **B-STRATA** (LOGIC-DERIVED): flood-basalt flow banding on steep faces.
- **r9 detail revs**: M-F/M-P/M-D shipped are the r9 technical winners;
  M-e rev1 and M-b are documented known limits (see below).

## What was deliberately NOT done

- No cosmetic grain boost in shadows (M-g excluded — B-FILL answers
  the same tell from scene truth).
- No invented second cloud deck; no 2048² (11–14 hr render for a bar
  judged at display size).

## Honest known limits (stated, not hidden)
- The wave field is statistical, not a simulated surface; the
  shoreline is a blend, not surf.
- B-FILL's lobe gains are deliberated, not measured — the direction
  (hemisphere integration of the deliberated dome) is the logic claim.
- Shipped talus is M-e rev1 (r9: MARGINAL) and striation is M-b (r9:
  MISS); the judged winners (M-E rev2) were a spec change, so this
  frame documents the limit. r11 swaps them in.
- The palette runs monochrome-orange in the mid-frame; the violet sky
  gradient survives up top.
