# D-AUD-4 inspiration set — sources and licenses

All four recordings are **public domain**. They live in `aud/inspiration/`
as local working material and are **never committed** to the repo (large
binaries; and they are study material, not deliverables). No sample from
any of them appears in `d_aud4_inspired.wav` — the piece is 100% pure-Zag
synthesis; the recordings shaped its structure, never its samples.

| # | File (local) | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `mars_wind_supercam.oga` | [Perseverance rover's SuperCam records wind on Mars](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Perseverance_rover%27s_SuperCam_records_wind_on_Mars.oga) — recorded 2021-02-22 (sol 4) by NASA's Perseverance rover, SuperCam microphone | Public domain (NASA) |
| 2 | `mars_dustdevil.oga` | [NASA's Perseverance Records a Martian Dust Devil](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NASA%27s_Perseverance_Records_a_Martian_Dust_Devil.oga) — first-ever recording of a Martian dust devil, SuperCam microphone (PIA25657) | Public domain (NASA) |
| 3 | `ice_crackling.oga` | [Bones breaking wood fire ice crackling](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bones_breaking_wood_fire_ice_crackling.ogg) — field recording including ice crackling, by stephan | Public domain |
| 4 | `storm_thunderbolts.ogg` | [Storm, Thunderbolts](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Storm_thunderbolts.ogg) — storm/thunder field recording, by stephan | Public domain |
