# JUDGE_BRIEF.md — Test 1 blind listening (for the 5 fresh native critics)

**You are a blind critic.** You did not build any of these clips. Judge only
what you hear.

## The brief (what each clip was asked to be)

**30 seconds of children playing and laughing** — a playground scene with:
- **≥3 distinct child voices** (you should hear different children, not one
  child sampled repeatedly),
- **overlapping play** (children talking/laughing over each other, not
  taking turns),
- **running feet** (footsteps, chasing),
- **one laugh tumbling into another** (laughter that catches, overlaps,
  cascades — not isolated giggles with silence between).

## The clips

Three 30-second clips, labeled A, B, C (order randomized by hash — the
mapping is sealed until you report):

- `blind/judge_A.wav`
- `blind/judge_B.wav`
- `blind/judge_C.wav`

One of them is a **real playground recording** (your calibration — this is
what the real thing sounds like; use it to anchor your ears, not to rank).
The other two are **machine-made by different methods** attempting the same
brief.

## What to report (per clip, then comparative)

1. **Does it sound like real children playing?** (yes / mostly / no)
   If no: what breaks the illusion, in one sentence?
2. **Do you hear ≥3 distinct child voices?** (count what you hear)
3. **Does it sound like a synthesizer?** — the kill question. "Sounds like
   a synth" means: you can hear oscillators, obvious looping, artificial
   regularity, or that uncanny electronic sheen. Answer plainly: **does
   this sound like a synth to you, yes or no?**
4. **The laughs:** do they tumble into each other, or do they sound placed?
5. **The feet:** do you hear running? Does it belong in the scene?
6. **Rank the two machine-made clips** (once you identify the real one):
   which is more convincing as children playing, and by how much?
7. **One-sentence verdict per clip.**

## Rules

- Listen on decent headphones/speakers, once through each, then A/B the
  two machine clips directly.
- Do not try to guess which method made which — judge the sound.
- Do not discuss with the other critics before reporting.
- Report in a table. No hedging essays; short answers are better.
