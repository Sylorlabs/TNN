BLIND A/B — brief: "an alien planet's voice" (Kethra, 21 seconds).

Two 21-second mono recordings, labeled A and B. Both claim to be the voice
of the same imagined alien planet (ringed super-Earth, argon atmosphere,
tidal moon, magnetosphere chorus). One is synthesized (oscillators,
resonators, filtered noise, chirps). One is assembled entirely from real
captured sources (ocean, loon, whale, throat-singing) with no synthesis.

For each of A/B, answer:
1. Does it sound like a synthesizer to you? (yes/no + one line why)
2. Does it sound like an alien planet's voice — and NOT like Earth? (yes/no + why)
3. Which is more convincing as "an alien planet's voice"?

Kill bars: a "sounds like a synth" verdict on the B-beta piece kills its
claim. An "Earth pastiche" majority (sounds like Earth animals/beach)
kills it too.
