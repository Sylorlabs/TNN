## 2. Diagnosis → mechanism changes

Each change below is demanded by a measured diagnosis, not a bar-tune.
The round-1 prereg's frozen core is kept: 3 voice channels, additive 1..12
true-harmonic source, 5-resonator child-tract cascade per channel (sample-wise
excitation), parallel tract-shaped aspiration cascade, 30 s scene arc
(yard → chase → trip → laughter → wind-down), zero RNG (h32/h01 only).

### M1. Discrete irregular syllable giggles (targets T2, T4; diagnosis: MOD4)
Round-1 diagnosis: "the bar's short-lag (8 Hz) peak is driven by the synthesis
architecture's inherent rhythmicity" — the giggle AM is a continuous 5–5.4 Hz
sinusoid, the laugh bed purrs at 4.8 Hz, and every voice runs 5.5 Hz vibrato.
Real giggles are NOT a sine: they are discrete syllable pulses with irregular
intervals and per-syllable amplitudes.
**Change:** replace the continuous `(1−amd+amd·sin)²` giggle AM with a
deterministic syllable train — hash-determined inter-syllable intervals
170–260 ms, per-syllable C1 attack/decay envelope, per-syllable amplitude
0.5–1.0, per-syllable vowel target held during the syllable (aids T4).
The 4.8 Hz laugh-bed purr becomes slow irregular wander. F0 vibrato becomes
per-channel rate (5.2/5.9/6.3 Hz) with slow deterministic rate/depth wander —
breaks the sharp 5.5 Hz envelope-breathing peak while keeping natural pitch
movement. MOD4 punishes regularity, not modulation; irregular child-like
modulation is the fix.

### M2. Sustained-vowel utterances + syllable-hold articulation (targets T4, T3)
Diagnosis: D3 universal failure — frac_static 0.00, F1 var 43× anchor. Real
children HOLD vowels; round-1 morphs vA→vB continuously through every
utterance and sweeps the giggle /a/→/e/ nonstop.
**Change:** (a) new event kind 4 = sustained utterance: 1.0–1.8 s, ONE vowel
held (no morph), F0 contour + prosodic amplitude only; (b) kind-0 utterances
get syllable structure: hold vA ~55%, C1 transition ~15%, hold vB ~30%,
instead of full-duration morph. Transition jumps are brief (60–80 ms), so
>50% of D3 1-s windows sit inside holds (var ~ vibrato/jitter only, ~1–3k Hz²
≪ 29,656 budget) and hold windows score static (var1+var2 < 20,000).
This is how real speech works — steady nuclei, quick transitions — not a
bar hack.

### M3. Consonant-like transient events (target T5; diagnosis: crew's own steelman)
Round-1 has zero consonants by design (D2 0.43/s vs anchor 1.50/s). D2's gate
excludes vowel onsets (≥10 voiced frames in the next 200 ms), so transients
must be standalone: (a) kind 5 = plosive burst: 15–30 ms broadband burst
through the channel's tract cascade (noise drive, no voicing), placed at
utterance ends / in gaps; (b) kind 6 = fricative: 120–250 ms unvoiced shaped
noise ("shh"/"sss"-like) injected as noise drive into the tract cascade with
the vowel target held; (c) kind 7 = clap/impact: <60 ms, HF-weighted,
env rise kept < 9 dB so D4 (burst 6–50 frames) does not catch them while D2's
hb-rise gate does. Noise drive enters the SAME per-channel cascade —
consonants shaped by the tract, architecturally, not post-hoc.

### M4. Source de-buzzing (target T6; diagnosis: buzzer-source steelman)
(a) Per-cycle spectral flutter: 3 harmonic bands × hash(cyc) amplitude
modulation ±6% — cycle-to-cycle waveshape variation, no static sawtooth.
(b) Breathiness episodes: slow deterministic (0.3–0.5 Hz) per-channel
aspiration-gain wander ±40% — HNR varies across frames (toward anchor
hnr_std 2.16), breathy episodes audible. (c) Roughness episodes:
hash-selected 0.3–0.6 s episodes every ~4–7 s per channel where alternate
glottal cycles are ×0.88 — subharmonic roughness. All deterministic.

### M5. F3 program raise (target T1; diagnosis: round-1 §5 F3B ceiling)
Round-1 measured a 150–270 Hz systematic estimator shortfall and aligned F3
to the 4th harmonic. Raise programmed F3 by ~+8%: /a/ 2780→3000, /e/ 2850→3050,
/i/ 2900→3100, /o/ 2750→2950, /u/ 2700→2900 (within the prereg's ±25% vowel
tunable allowance; the draft table had 3200–3900). B3 stays 200 Hz (no further
narrowing — note: round-1's 200 Hz was already beyond the nominal ±30%
bandwidth allowance; documented as deviation, not extended).
F2-band crosstalk watch: F2B currently 2168, strict ceiling 2283 — measure
after each candidate; back off F3 if F2B > 2270.

### M6. Scene sparsity (targets T3, T5; diagnosis: D6 voiced_frac 0.25 vs 0.06)
Thin the near-continuous murmur bed (24 → 6–8 short utterances), shorten
regular utterances (17 → 12), add the M2 sustains (6 × ~1.2 s) and M3
transients (target ~30 events). Fewer always-loud passages also opens the
quiet gaps D2's "not already loud" gate needs. Aim voiced_frac ~0.12–0.15
(anchor 0.06 not fully reachable without killing the scene; D6 needs ≤0.65,
not parity). Frozen bars are safe: voice_sig needs ≥25 clean frames; round-1
had 416.
