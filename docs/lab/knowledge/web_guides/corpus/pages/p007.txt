TITLE: Cheetah speed how fast cheetahs run fastest land animal
The cheetah is the fastest land animal alive today.
Cheetahs can run up to 120 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
They use their speed to chase antelope across the savanna.
A cheetah can only sprint for about 30 seconds at a time.
Their spotted coats hide them in tall grass while they hunt.
