**ALT3**

Mechanism:  
Build a DAG with web pages as nodes and a directed edge P→Q whenever P cites or outlinks to Q. Two supporting pages A and B count as independent iff the sets of nodes reachable from A and from B (their citation/outlink ancestors) are disjoint.

Page metadata needed:  
The complete list of outbound citation and outlink URLs for every page.

Weakness:  
Pages that simply omit citations (or whose cited URLs cannot be resolved) are treated as independent even when they derive from the identical uncited source.
