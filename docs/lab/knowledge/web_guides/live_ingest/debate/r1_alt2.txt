**ALT2**

Mechanism: Treat two pages as independent only if their first-observed (crawl) timestamps differ by ≥Δt (e.g. 24–48 h). Near-simultaneous first-seen times (lockstep appearance) are down-weighted or collapsed to a single supporting source, on the assumption they reflect coordinated posting, syndication, or rapid copying rather than independent origination.

Metadata needed: crawler first-seen / fetch timestamps (or HTTP Last-Modified / <time> / meta publication dates when available).

Weakness: Legitimate simultaneous publication (news wires, scheduled releases) or missing/spoofed timestamps can produce both false independence and false dependence.
