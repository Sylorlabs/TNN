S|Electricity can be dangerous, so it is important that we use it safely.
S|Electricity can give you an electric shock.
S|An electric shock can hurt you badly or even kill you.
S|[Image 8]High voltage is very dangerous.

S|Electricity can cause fires and injuries, even death.
S|Here are some rules for using electricity safely: 1.
S|Do not put anything into an outlet except a plug.
S|Do not pull on the cord to unplug an appliance, hold the plug and pull.
S|Dry your hands before you plug in or unplug a cord.
S|If a plug is broken or a cord is cut or worn, do not use it.
S|Do not plug too many cords into one outlet.
S|Keep appliances away from water.
S|Do not use a hair dryer if there is water nearby.
S|If there is an electrical storm (with lightning), turn off and disconnect electrical appliances, like the TV and computer.
S|Never touch any power lines.
S|Some power lines are buried underground.
S|If you are digging and find a wire, do not touch it.
S|Do not fly a kite or climb a tree near a power line.

S|# Kids science: Electricity 101 Science >> Physics for Kids | Intro | [Image 0] | | --- | --- | **What is Electricity?** In order to understand the basics of electricity, it helps to first understand about atoms.
S|Atoms are small particles that make up all matter.
S|They are so small that it takes billions and billions of them just to make something useful like a pencil.
S|Inside the atom are even smaller objects called electrons, protons, and neutrons.
S|Electrons have a negative charge (-) and the protons have a positive charge (+).
S|The protons and neutrons stick to together in the center of the atom, called the nucleus.
S|The electrons spin fast around the outside.
S|The positive charge of the protons keeps the electrons from flying off and leaving the atom.
S|The electrons in the atom are where electricity gets its name.
S|In some elements, there are electrons on the outside of the atom that, when a force is applied, can come loose and move to another atom.
S|When a bunch of atoms are together and electrons are moving from one atom to the other in the same direction, this is called electricity.
S|Electricity is the "flow" of electrons.
S|You can read more details about the atom here.
S|**How do we make electrons move to make electricity?** [Image 1] Current flows from positive to negative (the opposite direction of the electron flow) We make electricity by creating an electric circuit.

S|Never play with electricity.
S|Always follow the instructions on electrical equipment and ask your mom or dad for help if you don't understand.
S|Never stick anything but a proper plug into a wall outlet.
S|When you unplug something from the wall, don't pull on the cord, use the plug.
S|Never put electronic items into the water, especially if they are plugged in.
S|Don't place items on top of electrical cords.
S|The cords could get damaged and cause a fire.
S|These are just some safety precautions to take when using electricity.
