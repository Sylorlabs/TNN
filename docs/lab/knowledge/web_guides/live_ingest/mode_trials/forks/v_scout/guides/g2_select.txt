MODULE: G2
TITLE: Picking which search results to open
PRINCIPLE:
Search gives you many results. You cannot read them all, so open a small set, usually three. Three pages is enough to compare and find agreement.

Pick results whose titles and blurbs match your keywords. Skip a result if its title is exactly the same as one you already picked, since it is likely the same page showing up twice.

Prefer variety. Mix titles that look different so you are not reading three copies of one source. If two results share a title, open the first one and move on to the next different title.
WORKED:
W|NEED: How long do African elephants live in the wild?
W|QUERY: african elephants live wild long
W|RESULTS: p001, p002, p040
W|OPEN: p001 p002 p040
W|CLAIM: African elephants live 60 to 70 years in the wild. (p001, p002)
W|ANSWER: african elephants live 60 to 70 years in the wild.
DIRECTIVES:
D|SELECT-N|3
D|SKIP-DUPE-TITLES|on
CALIBRATION:
CRESULTS:
R|pA|Cheetah speed facts|cheetahs run fast
R|pB|Cheetah speed facts|cheetahs run fast
R|pC|Cheetah top speed measured|120 kilometers per hour
R|pD|Cheetah hunting behavior|hunts at dawn
EXPECT-OPEN: pA pC pD
