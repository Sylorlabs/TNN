## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: 1. What are Roman numerals?**

A: Ans. Roman numerals are a numbering system that originated in ancient Rome. They are represented by a combination of letters from the Latin alphabet, 
including I, V, X, L, C, D, and M, to represent different values.

**Q: 2. How do you read and write Roman numerals?**

A: Ans. To read Roman numerals, you add up the values of the letters. For example, II represents 2, and XIX represents 19. To write Roman numerals, you follow 
specific rules, such as not repeating more than three times in a row and using subtraction when a smaller value appears before a larger one.

**Q: 3. What are the basic Roman numeral symbols and their values?**

A: Ans. The basic Roman numeral symbols and their values are:- I represents 1- V represents 5- X represents 10- L represents 50- C represents 100- D represents 
500- M represents 1000

**Q: 4. How can I convert Roman numerals to Arabic numerals?**

A: Ans. To convert Roman numerals to Arabic numerals (regular numbers), assign values to each letter and add them up. For example, to convert XIV, you add 10 
(X) + 1 (I) + 5 (V) = 16.

**Q: 5. Are Roman numerals still used today?**

A: Ans. While Roman numerals are not commonly used in everyday life, they can still be found in various places, such as clock faces, movie credits, and the 
numbering of certain monarchs or popes. They also have historical and cultural significance.
