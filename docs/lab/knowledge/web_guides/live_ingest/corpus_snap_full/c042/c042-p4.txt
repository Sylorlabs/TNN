# What are constellations? - Twinkl Homework Help - Twinkl

This handy and helpful guide is full of fun astronomy facts - for kids who are looking to learn all about the earth and the solar system.

2 min

Updated: 20th January 2023

**Constellations are groups of stars.** Some of them you can see in the sky at night and some you can’t. What constellations you can see at any one time depends
 on the time of year and where you are on Earth. This is because as the Earth orbits around the sun, our view into space changes too and the northern and 
southern hemispheres are also pointing in different directions. This means that if you’re stargazing in Australia, the sky will look different to people 
stargazing in the UK or USA.

Constellations were first recorded thousands of years ago when astronomers started to take note of the different patterns and shapes they could see in the 
stars. They named them all after objects, people or even animals that are still used today to name the groups of stars. Some early astronomers told people 
stories about the constellations, which are now known by lots of people as myths and legends of astrology.

Today, there are 88 constellations that are named and can be recognised. An example of a well-known constellation is Draco. It’s pattern looks like a dragon and
 the Latin word for dragon was ‘Draco’. Some other examples of constellations are the 12 constellations that make up the signs of the zodiac. Read more about 
them here.
