In this "Force Masters" adventure, you will discover:

The Big Challenge: How bridges fight gravity and heavy loads.

The Two Secret Weapons: Compression: The "Squishing" Force (Push).

Tension: The "Stretching" Force (Pull).

Three Major Bridge Types:

Beam Bridge: Simple, strong, and stiff.

Arch Bridge: The clever curve that pushes weight into the ground.

Suspension Bridge: The champion of long distances (like the Golden Gate!).

Local Engineering: A special look at the massive San Mateo-Hayward Bridge right here in the Bay Area!

This educational STEM kids' video is packed with vibrant animations and simple diagrams that make complex physics concepts accessible. Discover why engineering 
is the art of balancing forces!

Key Learning Points (Parents, Students and Educators):

Load: The weight (cars, people, wind) that a bridge must support.

Compression: A force that squeezes or pushes a material together.

Tension: A force that stretches or pulls a material apart.

Beam Bridge: A rigid horizontal structure resting on two piers.

Arch Bridge: A curved structure that converts weight into compressive force.

Suspension Bridge: A bridge where the deck is hung below suspension cables on vertical suspenders.
