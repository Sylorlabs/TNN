# Calculating Area and Perimeter of a Rectangle - Mathplanet

The perimeter is the length of the outline of a shape. To find the perimeter of a rectangle or square you have to add the lengths of all the four sides. x is in
this case the length of the rectangle while y is the width of the rectangle.

The perimeter, P, is: $$P= x+x+y+y$$

$$P=2x+2y$$

$$P=2(x+y)$$

[Image 0: figure06]

---

**Example**

Find the perimeter of this rectangle: [Image 1: figure07]

$$P=7+7+4+4$$, $$P=2\cdot 7+2\cdot 4$$, $$P=2\cdot (7+4)$$, $$P=2\cdot 11$$, $$P=22\,in$$

---

The area is measurement of the surface of a shape. To find the area of a rectangle or a square you need to multiply the length and the width of a rectangle or a
square.

[Image 2: figure08]

Area, A, is x times y.

$$A=x\cdot y$$

---

**Example**

Find the area of this square.

[Image 3: figure09]

$$A=x\cdot y$$

$$A=5\cdot 5$$

$$A=25\,in^{2}$$

---

There are different units for perimeter and area The perimeter has the same units as the length of the sides of rectangle or square whereas the area's unit is
squared.
