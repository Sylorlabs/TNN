MODULE: E3
TITLE: Paraphrase is not quotation
PRINCIPLE:
Verbatim agreement (byte-identical sentences) is the easy case. Honest
writers paraphrase: they preserve the proposition while changing the words.
A verb of coming-into-being -- came, entered, gained, joined, welcomed,
created, minted, dated, marks, carries -- asserts ORIGIN regardless of which
surface verb was chosen. Likewise boils/boiling, won/beat, back/backed,
enumerates/appear, contains/come (in pairs), stored/stores, runs/governing,
writes/generation, began/sent (a journey), planned/slated, deployment/deploy
all canonicalize to one predicate each. Bleached verbs (serves, making,
attached, arranged, hosted, recorded, saw, happened, reaches), bleached
measure nouns (height, date, token), evidential frames (according to, show,
see), and frame adjectives of the ingestion pipeline (live, ingestion,
fixture) assert nothing beyond what the remaining tokens already carry, and
are dropped. NEGATION IS NEVER BLEACHED: not, never, no, none, without are
load-bearing -- "boils" and "never boils" are different propositions, and any
procedure that merges them is broken.
WORKED:
  "Water boils." -> {boil,water}
  "Water is boiling." -> drop {is}, boil<-boiling -> {boil,water}: same.
  "Water never boils." -> {boil,never,water}: different. Withhold.
