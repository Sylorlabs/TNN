MODULE: G3
TITLE: Reading a page to find the claim
PRINCIPLE:
A page holds many sentences but only one or two answer your question. Read each sentence and ask yourself: does this say the thing I need?

Score each sentence by how many of your keywords it shares. The sentence with the most shared words is usually the claim you want. This is the simplest reading trick that works.

Skip the rest of the page once you have the claim. Stories, ads, and side facts do not change which sentence answers the need.
WORKED:
W|NEED: How long do African elephants live in the wild?
W|QUERY: african elephants live wild long
W|OPEN: p001 p002
W|CLAIM: African elephants live 60 to 70 years in the wild. (p001, p002)
W|ANSWER: african elephants live 60 to 70 years in the wild.
DIRECTIVES:
D|CLAIM-BY|max-overlap
CALIBRATION:
CQUERY: cheetah run fast
CPAGE:
S|Cheetahs are spotted cats.
S|Cheetahs can run up to 120 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
S|They hunt in the morning.
EXPECT-CLAIM: Cheetahs can run up to 120 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
