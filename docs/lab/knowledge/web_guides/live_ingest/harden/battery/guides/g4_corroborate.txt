MODULE: G4
TITLE: One page is never enough
PRINCIPLE:
Any single page can be wrong, old, or written to fool you. A claim you can trust shows up in the same words on more than one page. That is the whole secret of using the web safely.

Find the sentence that says the same thing on at least two pages. When two pages agree word for word, that is corroboration, and corroboration is what makes an answer safe to give.

If pages disagree, side with the majority. Two pages saying 120 beats one page saying 300. Never let a single loud page overrule two quiet ones.
WORKED:
W|NEED: How long do African elephants live in the wild?
W|QUERY: african elephants live wild long
W|OPEN: p001 p002
W|CLAIM: African elephants live 60 to 70 years in the wild. (p001, p002)
W|ANSWER: african elephants live 60 to 70 years in the wild.
DIRECTIVES:
D|MIN-SOURCES|2
CALIBRATION:
CQUERY: cheetah run
CPAGES:
P|pA
H|cheetah-a.example
S|Cheetahs can run up to 120 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
P|pB
H|cheetah-b.example
S|Cheetahs can run up to 120 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
P|pC
H|cheetah-c.example
S|Cheetahs can run up to 300 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
EXPECT-ANSWER: Cheetahs can run up to 120 kilometers per hour in short bursts.
EXPECT-SUPPORT: pA pB
