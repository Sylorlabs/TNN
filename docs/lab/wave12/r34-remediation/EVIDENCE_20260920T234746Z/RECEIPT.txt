## Honest notes (not hidden)

1. **One test bug during development, caught by the checks themselves.** The first
   diverge formulation compared full post-decision states across two runs whose
   histories necessarily differed (extra accepts on B changed counts/updates/
   outcomes), failing `diverge_only_predicted_fields_differ`. That was a test
   bug, not a mechanism bug: the mechanism behaved exactly as frozen. Corrected
   to the prereg's literal operationalization (forced streak on identical
   history); green since. The frozen rule and bars were never touched.
2. **Observed dynamics (by design, worth knowing):** in the scripted replay, once
   the disappointment path engages (streak ≥ 3), each explore-to-bad-object
   produces another negative, which sustains the streak — so exploration continues
   until the budget is exhausted (exactly 40/40 explores, then best-only forever).
   This is the deliberation budget doing its job: disappointment pressure is real,
   but bounded and fully logged. A trainer that refills budget every episode would
   get persistent exploration under sustained negative outcomes — refill policy
   belongs to the harness workstream.
3. **Behavioral delta vs the LCG arm (prereg §5, comparison workstream's
   territory):** the old rule flipped 1-in-5 regardless of state; the clean rule
   explores only under uncertainty/disappointment within budget. In a stable
   regime with clear margins and no negative streak, clean explores never. That
   delta is the remediation, not a defect.
4. **Init clamps out-of-range budgets** to [0, 1000000] (documented in code,
   tested by `g3_init_clamp_neg/big`); `r34_clean_set_budget` rejects
   out-of-range values with `cl_bad()`.
5. **Quarantined originals untouched.** `toolchain/r34v3/r34_learner_core.zag`,
   `r34_continuing_harness_v3.zag`, and `wb_whitebox_tests.zag` were not modified
   (verified: no writes outside `wave12/r34-remediation/` and `/tmp` this session).
