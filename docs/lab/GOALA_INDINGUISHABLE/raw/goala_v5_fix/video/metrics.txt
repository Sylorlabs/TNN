# Goal-A video fix crew — metrics (final, frozen-grain build)

Renders: f3gvid1.avi (v1 glacier sunrise / bird), f3gvid2.avi (v2 harbor / boat)
Format: 480x480, 36 frames, 12 fps, 44.1 kHz, 36 audio chunks each.
Determinism: two independent renders byte-identical (see sha256sums.txt).
Audio: bit-identical to the shipped videos (V-T7 deferred, audio code untouched).

Method: vidmetrics.py (pure-Python measurement only; generation is pure Zag).
- Frame diffs: mean abs RGB diff over full frame, consecutive pairs (35 values).
- Subject presence: exact background-only renders (deterministic morph+grain cancel);
  mask = pixels differing >25 luma inside the analytic subject bbox; silhouette =
  full-frame fraction differing >25.
- Template-persistence self-attack: per-frame analytic subject boxes, translation
  search ±40/±12/±6, interior MAD over mask union. Sanity: frame vs itself shifted
  by (-10,-5) scores 0.00 at the exact expected offset -> a rigid translated
  template would score ~0; measured MADs far above that = articulated (PASS).
