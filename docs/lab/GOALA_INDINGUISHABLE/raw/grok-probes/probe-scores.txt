**IMAGES**

**(a) Focal Hierarchy**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — humans notice flat attention distribution.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — radial distance and hashed noise control contrast and detail density.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — reads naturally when contrast falloff remains subtle.

**(b) Asymmetric Deliberate Composition**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — centered balance often feels generic.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — fixed off-center anchors, counterweights, and coordinate-defined negative space.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — can look mechanically composed if asymmetry is too obvious.

**(c) Limited Purposeful Palette**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — uncontrolled color variety quickly feels synthetic.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — fixed palette lookup with one coordinate-selected focal accent.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — restrained color usually reads as intentional.

**(d) Motif Development**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — repeated related forms imply authored structure.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — reuse one seed motif under fixed scale, rotation, and occlusion transforms.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — repetition can feel stamped if transformations lack variation.

**(e) Stroke Wobble**
DETECTABILITY: 3/5 — perfectly uniform strokes can look machine-made.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — low-frequency coordinate noise offsets sampled stroke points.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — excessive wobble reads as a filter rather than hand motion.

**(f) Pentimenti**
DETECTABILITY: 3/5 — edge remnants suggest revision and process.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — preserve fixed earlier layers with deterministic opacity masks near boundaries.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — can look like accidental clutter or poor cleanup.

**AUDIO**

**(g) Phrasing Arcs**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — constant intensity lacks human breath and direction.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — apply a fixed four-to-eight-bar envelope to velocity, brightness, and density.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — phrase shaping is broadly perceived as musical intent.

**(h) Motivic Development**
DETECTABILITY: 5/5 — transformation strongly signals coherent authorship.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — derive transposition, inversion, rhythm change, and register from phrase index.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — low risk when transformations remain musically related.

**(i) Micro-timing Plus Articulation**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — rigid timing and uniform attacks sound sequenced.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — hash(note_index) maps offsets to plus-minus a few milliseconds and selects articulation by context.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — uncorrelated offsets or abrupt articulation choices expose the rule.

**(j) Timbral Evolution**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — static notes often sound synthetic.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — fixed per-note envelopes vary filter cutoff and harmonic decay over time.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — natural spectral motion is rarely interpreted as an artificial tell.

**(k) Rubato**
DETECTABILITY: 3/5 — modest tempo flexibility improves phrase realism.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — cadence and peak markers drive bounded one-to-four-percent tempo curves.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — poorly aligned flex can sound like timing instability.

**(l) Sparse Expressive Imperfections**
DETECTABILITY: 3/5 — rare deviations can prevent sterile uniformity.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — phrase hashes select one motivated event every 32-64 notes.
BACKFIRE: 3/5 — conspicuous rarity patterns can reveal deliberate simulation.

**VIDEO**

**(m) Narrative Beat Structure**
DETECTABILITY: 5/5 — unstructured gestures quickly feel algorithmic.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — fixed phase timelines define establish, initiate, peak, and resolve states.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — clear progression generally reads as authored movement.

**(n) Easing**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — linear motion is visibly mechanical.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — fixed smoothstep and power-curve functions vary by object index.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — easing is expected unless applied uniformly everywhere.

**(o) Motivated Camera**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — aimless or perfectly rigid cameras feel synthetic.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — follow a fixed interest point with lag during predefined narrative events.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — excessive tracking can resemble a preset camera rig.

**(p) Anticipation and Overshoot**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — direct starts and stops lack physical intent.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — fixed pre-move offsets and bounded target overshoot shape each gesture.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — repeated identical overshoot becomes a visible signature.

**(q) Secondary Motion**
DETECTABILITY: 4/5 — rigidly attached parts reduce perceived physicality.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — each object follows the main path with a fixed indexed delay.
BACKFIRE: 2/5 — uniform delays can look like a procedural filter.

**(r) Holds**
DETECTABILITY: 3/5 — pauses clarify structure and reduce continuous mechanical flow.
DETERMINISM: 5/5 — structural markers select fixed two-to-eight-frame holds with deterministic settling.
BACKFIRE: 1/5 — natural pauses rarely create a new artificial tell.
