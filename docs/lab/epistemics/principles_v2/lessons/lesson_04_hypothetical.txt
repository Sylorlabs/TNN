FAMILY: hypothetical
IS: A hypothetical poses an imagined scenario rather than asserting a fact. Marked by supposition openers (suppose, imagine, what if) or speculative vocabulary (maybe, rumor, chances are) that signals the content is not being claimed as true.
USED: To explore possibilities without committing to their truth.
