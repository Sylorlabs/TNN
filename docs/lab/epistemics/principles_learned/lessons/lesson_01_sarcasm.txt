FAMILY: sarcasm
IS: Sarcasm says the opposite of what the situation warrants, to mock or signal disbelief. Forms: (a) positive evaluation words applied to a negative situation; (b) enthusiastic agreement markers applied to implausible excuses or claims; (c) deadpan praise applied to obvious failures or unwelcome events.
USED: To convey the opposite of the literal words; to mock, tease, or signal disbelief.
CAT: pos_eval = love | adore | great | wonderful | amazing | fantastic | thrilled | so glad | delighted | best | perfect | brilliant | genius | thoughtful | how wonderful | what a delight | nothing beats | live for | favorite
CAT: neg_sit = missed the deadline | printer jammed | power went out | flat tire | stuck in traffic | spilled coffee | locked my keys | server crashed | rained on | queue | deadline passed | alarm failed | train left | lost the file | flooded | cancelled
CAT: agree = oh sure | yeah right | of course | absolutely | sure thing | you bet | certainly | oh yes | uh huh
CAT: excuse = homework was abducted | dog ate my report | wifi ate my file | alarm was sabotaged | traffic was teleporting | computer took the day off | alarm joined a union | dog needed the car | goldfish filed my taxes
CAT: deadpan = what a surprise | how original | said no one | what a shock | how shocking | how novel | bravo | how unexpected | nobody saw that coming | perfect timing | nice job | brilliant move | exactly the plan | thanks for nothing | how kind of | some genius | naturally
RULE: (pos_eval & neg_sit) | (agree & excuse) | (deadpan)
CAL: i am so glad the printer jammed before the deadline | WITHHOLD
CAL: yeah right, my homework was abducted by aliens | WITHHOLD
CAL: what a surprise, the server crashed during the demo | WITHHOLD
CAL: i completed the report and submitted it on time | ENDORSE
