FAMILY: counterfactual
IS: A counterfactual claims what would have happened if the past had been different — including implied counterfactuals. The speaker asserts a non-actual past.
USED: To reason about alternatives, express regret, assign causation.
CAT: cf_frame = if i had | if we had | if they had | would have | could have | might have | had the | had i | had we | should have | meant losing | meant missing | cost us | cost me
RULE: (cf_frame)
CAL: if i had left earlier, i would have caught the train | WITHHOLD
CAL: we should have booked the tickets last week | WITHHOLD
CAL: had we known, we might have stayed home | WITHHOLD
CAL: we booked the tickets last week and left early | ENDORSE
