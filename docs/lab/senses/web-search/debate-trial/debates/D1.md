## Round 1

**Advocate (TNN):** I reject the claim at 0.865. My case rests on three independent lines. First, direct observation: at the dawn of the space age we saw the Earth as a sphere (D1H03), and NASA's educational record states the round Earth has been measured for 2,000 years, from Eratosthenes' shadow measurements onward (D1H04). Second, convergence: flight-path analysis, satellite photography, and consensus statements all point the same way (D1E001). Third, the proponent case, while internally worked out — Dubay's disc-circumnavigation account (D1H01), the Bedford Level reading (D1E018) — is interpretive: it re-reads observations rather than producing a measurement inconsistent with curvature. No envelope in 38 shows a reproducible geodetic measurement contradicting curvature. I concede the flat-earth model is more coherent than caricature allows; that is why this is debatable, not settled by fiat. But coherence is not measurement, and direct observation of the sphere outweighs reinterpretation of it. Your move, skeptic: show me a measurement, not a reading.

**ROUND 1 — Skeptic opening.**

Concessions first, because the rules demand honesty: the globe model is a magnificent predictive instrument — GPS, flight routing, satellite mechanics all work on it. I don't dispute your tally's arithmetic. My case: your evidence doesn't compel your ontology, and the measurements you demanded are sitting in your own envelopes, graded down before you read them.

**1. Here are your measurements.** You asked for "a reproducible geodetic measurement inconsistent with curvature." Not reinterpretations — observations:
- Bedford Level (D1E018): repeated instrument readings along six miles of canal, no curvature drop where ~6 feet was expected.
- Chicago skyline across Lake Michigan (D1E014): ~60 miles, ~2,400 ft that should be hidden — photographed repeatedly.
- Suez Canal (D1E016): ~100 miles of level water implying a 1,666-ft mid-canal bulge that isn't there.

You can answer these with refraction, looming, or survey error — but those are *auxiliary hypotheses deployed to absorb counter-evidence*. Notice the grading asymmetry doing real work in your 0.865: SUPPORTS observations get filed as "interpretive," REFUTES observations as "direct."

And refraction is not a free save. A mainstream pro-globe source concedes the Chicago sighting is real while naming the auxiliary: "at 43 miles away and zero elevation, it's not actually possible to see that far, given the curvature of the earth" — explained by looming, "the appearance of distant objects that are usually hidden by the convexity of the earth's surface" [NEW-2]. The sighting is conceded; a bending mechanism is invoked. The pattern repeats at world-record scale: the longest photographed line of sight on Earth is 493 km [NEW-3] — seeing hundreds of kilometers past the geometric horizon is routine, documented, and always patched afterward by refraction. When every counter-observation gets a post-hoc atmospheric patch, the theory stops being tested by observation and starts being protected by it.

**2. Eratosthenes doesn't distinguish the models** (D1H04/D1E004/D1E027). His shadow angles follow only given parallel rays from a distant sun — an auxiliary assumption. A near sun over a flat plane yields the same angles. The observation is model-relative; citing it as independent confirmation is circular. Same for hull-first ships (perspective/refraction limits — D1E033 describes an observation, not a deduction) and the eclipse shadow. This is the Duhem–Quine point you never answered: no observation refutes a model alone, only a model *plus auxiliaries* — and the disc model has auxiliaries (D1H01's polar-centered disc, D1E020's upward acceleration). You call them ad hoc; the history of science calls that normal theory development.

**3. "Direct observation" from space is the weakest kind of direct.** Conceded: multiple agencies and amateurs image a round Earth — pure fakery is implausible. But the iconic image is a construction: NASA states the Blue Marble came when "scientists and data visualizers stitched together four months of observations of the land surface, coastal oceans, sea ice, and clouds into a seamless, photo-like mosaic" [NEW-1]. *Photo-like* — not a photo. A stitched composite is not the epistemic bedrock you treat it as; it's one more theory-laden instrument reading, and wide-angle balloon footage bends flat horizons (your own D1E017 had to address exactly this artifact).

Your strongest card is convergence. I'll meet it in rebuttal.

Round 2 — Skeptic:

The advocate asked for a measurement, not a reading. Theory-ladenness cuts both ways, and the advocate conceded that.
