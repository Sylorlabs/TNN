## D1-Q1: Does the flat-earth side address east-west circumnavigation, and does the round-earth side answer that rebuttal?

**Question:** Do any round-earth sources directly address the flat-earth counterargument that east-west circumnavigation is possible on a disc model (sailing in a circle around a central North Pole)? Find a flat-earther rebuttal page on circumnavigation if one exists, and/or a round-earth response to that specific rebuttal.

**Answer:** Yes on both counts.

*Flat-earther rebuttal:* Eric Dubay's "How Maps, Compasses, and Circumnavigation Work on Flat Earth" (hive.blog) lays out the standard disc-model answer: the North Pole is the center, South is every direction outward, and **east/west are concentric circles around the Pole** — "A westerly circumnavigation of Earth is thus going around with Polaris continually on your right, while an easterly circumnavigation is going around with Polaris always at your left." Magellan-style east-west trips are therefore fully compatible with the disc model. The Flat Earth Society forums repeat this, adding the corollary that no *north-south* circumnavigation has ever been verified (they claim restricted zones prevent it).

*Round-earth response to that specific rebuttal:* In a Flat Earth Society forum thread, a round-earth participant countered with the "straight course" argument: an aircraft taking off from Quito and flying heading 270° on a *straight* course would circumnavigate the equator on a sphere, but "on your flat earth, to fly around the equator the pilot would have to keep making steering adjustments to fly in a circle. That is not a 'straight course', that is a circular course." This is the right kind of response to the disc rebuttal — it distinguishes a great-circle flight from a forced circle — though note that commercial flights routinely make small course adjustments anyway, so the argument is cleaner on a ship holding constant compass heading. A stronger round-earth answer, which I did not find stated on these pages, is the *distance* argument: on the azimuthal disc, southern-hemisphere routes are far longer than on the globe, and scheduled flight times contradict the disc distances.

**Key URLs:**
- https://hive.blog/nature/@ericdubay/how-maps-compasses-and-circumnavigation-work-on-flat-earth?sort=votes (flat-earther rebuttal, pro-claim side)
- https://www.theflatearthsociety.org/forum/index.php?PHPSESSID=27073618237857ea0b7542aceff6186d&topic=30408.msg744347 (round-earth response to the rebuttal)

## D1-Q2: Official space-agency or scientific-institution page stating Earth's shape

**Question:** Find an official space-agency or scientific-institution page (e.g., NASA, ESA) stating Earth's shape — the crews found only press/fact-checkers/blogs.

**Answer:** Found. NASA has a page that directly answers the question on its own domain: **"How Do We Know the Earth Isn't Flat? We Asked a NASA Expert: Episode 53"** (nasa.gov). A NASA scientist states that the early mariners "made the first orbit of Earth by sea, and they knew the Earth was round," and that "at the dawn of the space age, in the late 50s and 60s, we were able to see for ourselves that our beautiful home is a gorgeous round object known as a sphere." A second NASA page, "What Is Earth? (Grades 5–8)" (nasa.gov/learning-resources), says: "Humans have known that Earth is round for more than 2,000 years! The ancient Greeks measured shadows during summer solstice and also calculated Earth's circumference." Both are primary institutional statements, not press coverage.

## D2-Q1: Is there any peer-reviewed study that sampled alleged "chemtrail" spray sites with proper chain of custody and published null results?

**Question:** Is there any peer-reviewed study that directly sampled air, soil, or rainwater at locations where "chemtrail" spraying was alleged, with proper chain of custody, and published null results? Or does the "no evidence" case rest entirely on expert opinion + contrail physics?

**Answer:** Mostly the latter — with one important footnote.

The only peer-reviewed study *about the chemtrail theory itself* is the 2016 Carnegie Science/UCI survey (Shearer et al., *Environmental Research Letters*): 77 atmospheric scientists surveyed, 76 said they had encountered no evidence of a secret large-scale spraying program, and they judged the sampling evidence put forward by believers (rainwater/soil photos and lab reports) methodologically flawed — e.g., collecting samples in Mason jars with metal lids contaminates them. That is expert opinion + contrail physics, not a null-result field campaign; nobody funded a formal study to go sample "spray sites" because the physical mechanism alleged (tanker jets spraying at cruise altitude) is considered implausible and would leave operational footprints, not just rainwater traces.

The footnote: there *was* one peer-reviewed paper claiming positive sampling evidence — J. Marvin Herndon's "Evidence of Coal-Fly-Ash Toxic Chemical Geoengineering in the Troposphere" (IJERPH, 2015), comparing rainwater and HEPA-filter dust chemistry to coal fly ash. It was **retracted**: the journal found the aluminum normalization value was wrong (70,000 µg/kg used vs 140,000,000 µg/kg measured — invalidating the conclusions), that the paper compared samples only to coal-fly-ash leachates and never to any other potential chemical source, and that its language was unscientific. So the peer-reviewed *pro*-spraying sampling literature is empty, and the peer-reviewed *null* literature is a survey, not field sampling.

Honest caveat: the reason rainwater tests "show elevated metals" is mundane geochemistry — aluminum is ~8% of Earth's crust, barium/strontium are widespread in rock and groundwater — and believers' comparisons lack baselines. That critique comes from reviews and expert assessment, not from a peer-reviewed null-sampling campaign.

**Key URLs:**
- https://www.labmanager.com/surveyed-scientists-debunk-chemtrails-conspiracy-theory-9128 (Shearer et al. 2016 survey)
- http://retractionwatch.com/2015/09/03/paper-on-chemtrails-a-favorite-subject-of-conspiracy-theorists-retracted/ (Herndon retraction)
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4563576/ (the formal retraction notice)

## D2-Q2: Largest documented deliberate outdoor particle release, and any high-altitude commercial-aircraft spraying program

**Question:** What is the largest documented deliberate outdoor particle-release event (geoengineering tests, SPICE, Make Sunsets, etc.), and has any documented program ever operated at high altitude from commercial-style aircraft?

**Answer:** Everything documented is tiny — orders of magnitude below what either side of the debate imagines. SPICE (UK, 2011) was cancelled before its 1-km hose-balloon trial; Harvard's SCoPEx was cancelled before its first balloon flight. The only operation actually releasing material into the stratosphere is **Make Sunsets**, a private startup lofting grams of SO₂ on weather balloons: ~53 kg total since February 2023, with the EPA recording ~0.1 tons as of May 2025 — which the agency called "an extraordinarily low amount." For comparison, researchers' own simulations say real deployment would need **8–16 million tonnes of SO₂ per year**, and even the largest *hypothetical* experiment in the literature (Earth's Future, 2025) was ~1,000 tonnes — never executed.

As for high-altitude spraying from commercial-style aircraft: no program has ever operated one. A 2025 UCL/Yale study *proposed* modifying Boeing 777s to spray SO₂ at ~42,000 ft (lower than ideal because retrofitting jets for 65,000 ft is harder), but it is a paper proposal — never built, never flown. Real documented high-altitude releases are scientific tracer experiments (e.g., NASA sounding-rocket chemical releases in the 1960s–80s), which dispersed trace amounts in the upper atmosphere for auroral/wind research, not particles meant to linger. The gap between "proposed/executed at scale of grams to kilograms" and "the claimed continuous global program" is roughly seven orders of magnitude.

**Key URLs:**
- https://spectrum.ieee.org/amp/solar-geoengineering-2669008650-2669008650 (Make Sunsets scale, IEEE Spectrum)
- https://phys.org/news/2026-09-artificially-dim-sun-scientists-theyre.html (Earth's Future experiment/deployment scale)

## D4-Q2: Sources addressing the Lunokhod retroreflector qualification

**Question:** The Wikipedia third-party-evidence article qualifies the retroreflector proof: uncrewed Lunokhod missions also placed reflectors, so reflectors alone are "not, on their own, conclusive evidence". Find sources addressing this qualification directly.

**Answer:** Found two that engage it head-on.

The first, "The Moon and the Camera: A Reply to the Claim that Apollo's Gravity Was Manufactured" (libertarianism.uk), *accepts* the qualification explicitly: "A retroreflector does not by itself prove a manned landing. The Soviet Lunokhod rovers also carried reflectors to the Moon." Its response is cumulative-evidence reasoning: "Each piece of evidence must be allowed to prove only what it can. But the alternative account must explain the convergence" — reflectors *at the Apollo landing sites*, ranged continuously by observatories since days after the missions, plus 382 kg of samples with lunar cosmic-ray and solar-wind signatures, tracking data, and photographs tied to recognizable terrain. The hoax account would need secret robotic placements plus sample collection plus return, all undetected by the USSR.

The second, Robert Braeunig's long-running "The Moon Hoax Debate" (hosted at scss.tcd.ie), answers the hoax advocate's escape hatch directly: to explain the reflectors, "the hoax advocates have no choice but to claim they were placed on the Moon by robotic landers; a huge undertaking for which there is no supporting evidence." Apollo 15's array was particularly large (300 corner cubes), returns were detected by McDonald Observatory within days of Apollo 11, and the reflectors sit at coordinates LRO later photographed with landing hardware.

Honest note: the qualification itself is legitimate — a retroreflector is passive hardware and proves placement, not crewed placement. Neither source denies this; they argue the reflectors' *size, location, timing, and independent ranging* still bite. A fair pro-hoax reply (which I did not find made well anywhere) would be that uncrewed Soviet Luna missions prove robotic placement was feasible; the round-earth sources answer with the scale/timing/co-location evidence.

**Key URLs:**
- https://libertarianism.uk/2026/09/16/the-moon-and-the-camera-a-reply-to-the-claim-that-apollos-gravity-was-manufactured/
- https://www.scss.tcd.ie/Stephen.Farrell/ipn/background/Braeunig/hoax.htm

## D5-Q1: Fraction of AARO/Pentagon UAP cases unexplained, and what the unresolved ones look like

**Question:** Across the AARO/Pentagon UAP reviews, what fraction of cases remained unexplained after conventional identification, and is there any official description of what those unresolved cases look like?

**Answer:** The fraction varies by reporting period, but the pattern is stable: a majority resolve as conventional objects; the remainder are parked for lack of data rather than characterized as exotic.

- **FY2025 AARO report** (per The Debrief): of 319 new reports, **114 resolved** — every one as a conventional object (balloons, birds, satellites, aircraft, drones; 44–238 as satellite flares depending on counting). Close to **200 remained unexplained "due to insufficient data."** The report explicitly found no resolved case pointing to exotic technology.
- **FY2024 consolidated report** (per EarthSky): **757 new cases**; 21 were still "unexplained so far" at report time, with most of the rest resolved or archived for insufficient data.

On what the unresolved cases look like: AARO's official framing is that unresolved means *data-poor* — a video with no range, telemetry, or camera parameters "proves very little." Former director Kosloski said a small subset are, in his view, "true anomalies" requiring "significant scientific rigor," and AARO built the GREMLIN deployable multi-sensor system (2D/3D radar, EO/IR, RF monitoring) specifically to capture properly instrumented data on them. I found **no official description characterizing any unresolved case as a non-human craft** — the official vocabulary is "insufficient data" vs "true anomaly," never "extraterrestrial."

Caveat on sources: I deliberately avoided citing aggregator blogs with dubious provenance (e.g., "Pentagon 2026 UAP report" posts that may be synthetic); the two cited figures come from reputable outlets reporting the actual DoD releases.

## D5-Q2: Any publicly verifiable physical evidence for non-human craft, or does it reduce to testimony and inference?

**Question:** Is there any publicly verifiable physical evidence (recovered materials, raw multi-sensor data released in full) cited by a credible source for non-human craft — or does all pro-claim evidence reduce to testimony and inference?

**Answer:** On my honest read: **it reduces to testimony and inference.** No recovered material has been publicly released or independently analyzed by any credible source, and no full raw multi-sensor dataset is public.

The two strongest pro-claim items:
1. **David Grusch's sworn July 2023 congressional testimony** (under oath, DOPSR-cleared) about a multi-decade crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program and "non-human biologics" — but it is explicitly *secondhand*: he said he was "informed… of a multi-decade UAP crash retrieval and reverse-engineering program **to which I was denied access**," relaying assessments from ~40 witnesses. Testimony, not artifacts.
2. The declassified Navy FLIR clips (Tic Tac etc.) — real sensor data, but short excerpts, not raw full-sensor releases, and they show unidentified objects, not craft of non-human origin.

Against: AARO's 63-page historical review (1945–2023), reporting via NPR: "AARO has found no evidence that any U.S. government investigation, academic-sponsored research, or official review panel has confirmed that any sighting of a UAP represented extraterrestrial technology," and "no verifiable evidence for claims that the U.S. government and private companies have access to or have been reverse-engineering extraterrestrial technology." NASA's independent study team (Sept 2023) reached the same conclusion.

Caveat: absence of *public* verification is not proof of absence — the claim's strongest form ("the program exists but is classified") is unfalsifiable by design. But for the trial's purposes: the pro-claim evidence base I could find is sworn testimony plus inference from pilot encounters; the anti-claim base is the Pentagon's own comprehensive review stating no verifiable physical evidence exists.

**Key URLs:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_UFO_claims_by_David_Grusch (Grusch testimony, secondhand nature documented)
- https://www.knkx.org/2024-03-08/pentagon-finds-no-evidence-of-alien-technology-in-new-ufo-report (AARO historical review, NPR reporting)
