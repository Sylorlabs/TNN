1. Direct peer-reviewed sampling: the flagship rebuttal is the 2016 Shearer et al. survey (76 of 77 experts had "come across" no evidence) — an opinion survey, not a physical test. Is there any peer-reviewed study that directly sampled air, soil, or rainwater at locations where spraying was alleged, with proper chain of custody, and published null results? Or does the "no evidence" case rest entirely on expert opinion + contrail physics?

2. Geoengineering boundary: real small-scale deliberate releases exist (SPICE testbed, proposed stratospheric aerosol injection, Make Sunsets balloons). What is the largest documented deliberate outdoor particle-release event, and has any documented program ever operated at high altitude from commercial-style aircraft? Conflation of these real activities with the claim is the proponent case's most cited pillar, so its true scale matters.
