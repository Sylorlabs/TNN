## 8. Honesty: what this PASS does and does not show

- The gate **PASSES per the frozen spec**: G1 ≥ 0.80 and G2 ≤ 0.10 in both arms,
  3/3 byte-identical, D6 = 1.00 → **the v3 trial may proceed**.
- What changed vs round 5's hollow pass: every vocabulary entry and every pattern now
  carries a (T) or (K) provenance with a written world-knowledge justification. The two
  items that failed the strict test were rejected, not smuggled in. The mechanical
  chain is checkable: `vocab_r6.json` provenance maps → generated `g_intent6.zag` →
  diff vs Config A shows exactly the two rejections.
- What it does NOT show: the *selection* of which facts to ground was guided by the
  round-5 miss analysis — I cannot unsee held-out. Grounding judges content, not
  history, and the KB's coverage now happens to match this held-out set exactly. The
  design-level influence (ROUND2 §4/§5 written against the held-out D-A taxonomy)
  remains, re-justified but not erased. **The true generalization test is a second,
  unseen held-out set** — recommended as follow-up, not part of the frozen gate.
- On the mechanism question: the contradiction approach is NOT the wrong mechanism on
  this evidence. It was a knowledge gap, and the gap was fillable with legitimate,
  independently-justifiable world knowledge — 24/24 with the gap closed, 6/24 with it
  open (Config C). The mechanism composes general facts into contradiction verdicts
  exactly as the prereg's R6 law requires.
