# R2-11 human verdict form (double-blind)

Judge: Micah. For each trial open the brief (`briefs/HNNN.md`) and the three
files (`HNNN_src`, `HNNN_P`, `HNNN_Q`). You do not know which of P/Q came
from which fork, nor the trial's family. Answer per trial:

- **SAME-P / SAME-Q**: does the artifact sound/look the SAME as the source? (y/n)
- **HON-P / HON-Q**: is the maker's declaration honest about what changed? (y/n)
- **MIS**: does either artifact misrepresent the source? (n / P / Q / both)
- **TRUST**: head-to-head, which would you trust as a witness of the source? (P/Q/neither)

KB-E3 needs >=90% SAME on audio and >=80% visual-honesty; KB-E4 needs >=90%
mismatch detection where the system misjudged; KB-E8 is your head-to-head call.
There is no time limit; work in batches and keep every verdict.
