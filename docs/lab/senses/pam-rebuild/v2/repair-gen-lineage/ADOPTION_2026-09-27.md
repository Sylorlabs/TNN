## The two quirks

### Quirk 1: Ghost epoch (overwrite rollback rebirth)

**Before:** When a judgment was destroyed by OVERWRITE and the overwrite was ROLLED BACK (restoring the original), the epoch picker pointed at the OVERWRITE entry (the "ghost") instead of recognizing the restored judgment as a rebirth. The ghost carried the weak overwriting strength (10), so the restored strength-80 judgment priced destruction at 0 — a pricing hole.

**Fix (architectural):** The generation is now a first-class ledger-derived concept:
- `st_gen_is_birth`: ADD/OVERWRITE are births. ROLLBACK whose rolled-back op was OVERWRITE/KILL/KILL_EVIDENCED/DELETE_STRONG is a **REBIRTH** — a new generation born at rollback time, carrying the restored strength. ROLLBACK of ADD is **not** a birth (it restores death).
- `st_gen_epoch_start`: backward scan for the most recent birth (slot-filtered, OK-only).
- `st_gen_birth_strength`: strength installed at the birth (for rollback-births: the rolled-back entry's before-strength = the restored judgment's strength).
- All pricing (`st_epoch_highwater`, meter high-water, DELIB scopes) routes through the generation epoch.
- A rebirth classifies as **REVISED** in `st_meter_revs` — never a fresh judgment, so no UNPROVEN discount (fail-closed).

**After:** The rollback-restore is a rebirth. The restored strength-80 judgment prices at 2 (HELD_STRONG, REVISED), not 0. The price-0 delete is refused. The ghost epoch is unrepresentable.

### Quirk 2: DELIB HIST boundary

**Before:** In DELIB mode, HIST priced destruction using the corpse high-water (seeing through the overwrite reset), while FRESH was generation-scoped. HIST could price a weak overwriting judgment using the dead predecessor's strength.

**Fix (architectural):** The price path is generation-scoped for **every** personality. HIST and FRESH both price the current generation only. Personality remains a recorded binding attribute of the DELIBERATE record (the switch rule is unchanged); it no longer moves the priced lineage.

**After:** HIST and FRESH price identically on the same generation (weak overwrite, no cites, no trail: price 0, reasons WEAK|UNCONTESTED|NOTRAIL|OTHER). The corpse high-water no longer weighs on a destruction price.
