The baseline result does not support the categorical claim that TNN has “no compositional faculty at all.” F1 tested only four prompts, S1–S4, against “the existing dialogue QA system,” and the verdict itself says that this system “has no generative mode.” That establishes a failure of one QA mode under one prompt format, not an absence of composition in TNN generally. The later success of two newly built composers further shows that the broader system can be extended with compositional machinery, so “retrieval + templates alone cannot compose” is supported, but “TNN’s existing machinery has no compositional faculty at all — not a weak one, an absent one” is substantially broader than the evidence.

2. The claimed 16/16 success depends on an experimenter fix after observing failures, and the verdict understates how consequential that intervention was. F2 says 3/16 trials failed B1 because the emitter “dropped the pivot word entirely,” then reports 16/16 only “after fix.” Changing every affected rendering branch so that the missing pivot is emitted changes the generated stories and directly repairs the measured criterion; it is therefore not merely an observational verifier or neutral defect log. Calling it “only a missing render” does not resolve the concern, especially because the original pipeline had no self-check and the fix was informed by the observed failures. The post-fix result supports the repaired composer, but not an unqualified claim that the preregistered implementation passed as initially run.

3. The verdict attributes the DEL arc advantage to class-based planning even though its own design prevents that causal conclusion. It says “Role-aware placement ... is what creates the arc shape,” but later concedes: “this does NOT claim that C-DEL’s planning (vs its repair step) caused the arc gain — full-pipeline intervention.” C-DEL differs from C-POS in multiple bundled factors: class-based assignment, role-specific templates, decision-trace behavior, coverage verification, and bounded repair. The single comparison therefore shows that the complete DEL pipeline scored better under the judge, not that deliberative or role-aware planning itself caused the improvement; the causal language in §4 exceeds the experiment’s isolation of variables.
