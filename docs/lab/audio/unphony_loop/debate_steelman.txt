1. WHY THE MECHANISM MUST BE REAL (physics + psychoacoustics)

Every real instrumental tone factors as EXCITATION x RESONATOR: a hammer,
plectrum, bow, or breath column drives a coupled structure (string + bridge
+ body, or bore + bell). The ear is not a spectrum analyzer; it is a
CAUSE detector. We hear the hammer hitting, the bow biting, the breath
pressing — the event that set the air moving — and we infer the object
from the excitation's signature convolved with the body's modes. This is
not poetry: remove the excitation component from a recording (keep the
steady-state harmonic stack, delete the first 80 ms and the breath/bow
noise floor) and listeners report "synthesizer," even when the spectrum
is otherwise identical. The V11 voice result is the same mechanism in a
different organ: ARTIC had correct F0 (385 Hz) but 0% measurable formant
frames (real: 30%) and HNR 0.5 dB (real: 4.5 dB) — pitch with no
vocal-tract resonances, no glottal source physics. PARADD was additive
sines with no tract; SPECSTAT was filtered noise with no glottal source.
Three different forks, one shared defect, all judged phony: the renderer
synthesizes the resonator's steady state (or a noise shaped like it) with
no excitation physics and no time-varying body. A motif render built the
same way inherits the same defect at every note, which is exactly why
changing the NOTE SEQUENCE and the RECURRENCE (1.000000 vs 0.741083)
changed nothing about the verdict. Timbre defects manifest in 200 ms —
one note. Plan defects need a sequence. The judges needed no long
listening to say "horrible," and "1 = same as 3, slightly different" is
the fingerprint of identical broken timbre carrying different note
content: same horrible, different notes. A plan-side timing defect
predicts a DIFFERENT flavor of horror when timing is swept from grid to
drift; a renderer-side timbre defect predicts EXACTLY what was heard.

STEELMAN 2 — H2 "THE ATTACK IS MISSING"
(Thesis: the first 5-50 ms of every note is a different physical regime
from the sustain, and the renders never produce it. Timbre identity lives
in the transient.)
====================================================================

1. WHY THE MECHANISM MUST BE REAL (physics + psychoacoustics)

Strike physics: in the first 5-50 ms after a hammer/plectrum/bow engages,
the string is NOT in its standing-wave regime. It is stiffness-dominated:
partials follow f_n = n*f0*sqrt(1+B*n^2) with LARGE B (inharmonic strike
partials), each decaying with its own fast time constant (tau 15-60 ms),
plus a broadband burst from the hammer felt / plectrum snap / bow bite,
plus a pitch overshoot of ~+35 cents as the string settles (f0(t) =
f0_target*(1+0.02*exp(-t/20ms))). Then the regime changes: the standing
wave locks in, B drops to the sustain value, the noise burst dies. THE
PSYCHOACOUSTIC PRIOR IS THE STRONGEST IN THE FIELD: the classic
attack-swap experiments show that if you strip or swap the attack
transient, listeners can no longer identify the instrument AT ALL — a
piano with its attack removed is unrecognizable; a trumpet given a piano's
attack is heard as a piano. Timbre identity does not live in the steady
state; it lives in the first 50 ms. A renderer that starts all partials
at t=0 with steady-state harmonic ratios and a smooth ADSR fade-in
produces a tone that "fades in" instead of being STRUCK — the signature
of an organ or tonewheel, the canonical "phony" quality. No steady-state
improvement (better formants, better vibrato, better room) can compensate
for a missing strike, because the ear made its instrument judgment before
the sustain even began. H2 is also the mechanism that composes most
cleanly: H1's modal body is what the strike EXCITES — H2 without H1 is a
strike into a dead room, H1 without H2 is a beautiful body that is never
struck. The pair is the minimal viable rescue of a single note.

2. WHY THE CLIP MEASUREMENTS SUPPORT IT

  broken (H1). The ear hears a wash that never gets struck AND never

STEELMAN 3 — H1 "DEAD PARTIALS": NO COUPLED BODY
(Thesis: a real instrument is ONE excitation driving a COUPLED modal
structure; the renders' partials are either plastic (correlation 1.000)
or disembodied (~0). The ear reads the 0.6-0.9 correlated-but-alive zone
as "one physical object vibrating.")
====================================================================

1. WHY THE MECHANISM MUST BE REAL (physics + psychoacoustics)

A string, bridge, and body form one coupled system. Every partial is
driven by the SAME physical event through shared coupling, so partial
amplitude envelopes co-modulate: correlated but not identical, because of
differential damping (high modes die faster), mode coupling, wolf notes,
and beating between near-coincident partials. The auditory system uses
this common-fate grouping (Bregman: correlated envelopes = one source)
to fuse partials into a single object; the RESIDUAL decorrelation (the
0.6-0.9 zone, not 1.0) is what says "physical, alive" rather than
"electronic." Two failure modes, both phony: (a) fixed amplitude ratios
times one global envelope -> pairwise envelope correlation EXACTLY 1.000
-> the ear hears "plastic," over-correlated, organ-like; (b) fully
independent partial envelopes -> correlation ~0 -> "disembodied," a chord
of unrelated sines, no object at all. Real bodies live strictly between.
This also explains the V11 voice result at one remove: formants ARE the
voice's body — 0% formant frames meant the partials had no shared
resonant structure shaping them, i.e., dead partials in the vocal domain.
And H1 subsumes H6 for free: run vibrato FM through a FIXED modal body
and the body's resonances convert FM into coupled AM automatically —
real vibrato's amplitude component emerges with no extra module, which is
precisely the correlated shimmer the hypothesis is about.

2. WHY THE CLIP MEASUREMENTS SUPPORT IT

- The HNR ~ -2..0 dB readings ELIMINATE failure mode (a): a fixed-ratio
  additive stack would measure HNR > +30 dB (autocorrelation peak ->
  ~1.0). The renders are nowhere near that. What remains is failure
  mode (b) or worse: partials that do not lock into a coupled harmonic
  structure at all — independent, drifting, or detuned partials whose
  envelopes share no common fate. The ear cannot fuse them into an
  object; it hears an inharmonic wash. This is "dead partials" in its
  most extreme form, and it is consistent with every number in the file:
  low HNR (no harmonic lock), low ZCR 0.015-0.016 (no rich harmonic
  series crossing zero either — the energy sits in a few incoherent
  low partials + noise), centroid 1556-2269 Hz (energy present but
  unstructured).
- The AR/PAR centroid SPLIT (2269/2054 vs 1556 Hz) with HNR INVARIANT is
  itself a dead-partials signature: two renderer lineages place their
  (uncoupled) energy in different bands, but neither lineage produces a
  coupled stack — the defect is structural to how partials are generated,
  not a tuning choice. A coupled modal bank driven by one exciter cannot
  produce "different band, same deadness" across lineages; independent
  partial generation can.
- tilt_lohi 0.75-0.98 dB: nearly flat spectral tilt — no body formant
  bumps shaping the spectrum. A real coupled body imposes its resonances
  (2-4 broad bumps); the renders show none.

2. R_PHYS IS PLAN-DRIVEN — A PERFECT MODEL STARVED OF CONTROL

Read R_PHYS's own spec: the excitation burst level is "tied to vel";
the attack burst is 20 ms; formants glide 5-10%; wet gain comes from a

release_ms=0, no overlap — and the physical model faithfully renders:
identical excitation bursts at identical levels, formants gliding on a
fixed schedule regardless of phrasing, tails exposing hard digital note
boundaries the plan put there. A perfect source model does not INVENT
musical information; it REALIZES the plan's information with higher
fidelity. If the plan contains no phrase arch, no agogic accents, no
overlapping resonances, the output contains none — rendered beautifully.
The consensus's fix improves the fidelity of a dead performance. That is
not a rescue; it is a more expensive corpse.

3. THE SCENE-LEVEL CATEGORY ERROR (why the single-note killer misfires)

The consensus's sharpest weapon — "one isolated rendered note already
fails, so the plan never gets a vote" — is a category error against the
actual verdicts. Micah judged 4-second SCENES, not isolated notes. The
cues that make a scene real are irreducibly plan-level: note overlap
(real performances: decay of note N audible under attack of N+1; overlap
fraction > 0.35 vs < 0.05 for quantized plans), phrase arch (6-12 dB
phrase contrast, brightness coupled to loudness), rubato correlated with
dynamics (r > 0.3 between timing deviation and per-note RMS), room
loading (loud passages exciting the space). An isolated note CANNOT carry
scene phoniness, so an isolated note passing or failing a discriminator
proves NOTHING about why a 4-second motif was judged horrible. The
discriminator's own F7/F7b are plan bars — B predicts C=(P_TNN,R_PHYS)
FAILS them — and then asks us to believe C might "still sound real to the
blind judge" anyway. If Micah's ears are the judge (Prong 2, and ears
outrank metrics), a metronomic-dead, flat-velocity, non-overlapping motif
does not sound real no matter how physical each note is. The consensus
wants it both ways: plan features in the discriminator, plan irrelevance
in the conclusion.

4. THE UNCANNY VALLEY OF SYNTHESIS (why better timbre can sound WORSE)

There is a worse outcome than "R_PHYS doesn't fix it": R_PHYS makes it
sound MORE phony. As timbre approaches real while the plan stays dead,
the percept flips from "obviously a synthesizer" (forgivable, even
charming) to "a bad player in a wrong scene" (uncanny, irritating). The
judges' word was "horrible" — not "synthy," HORRIBLE. A row of
physically-perfect notes with flat velocity, grid timing, and zero
overlap is not a performance; it is a demonstration of a physical model,
and human ears punish wrong musical behavior more harshly than wrong
timbre. The consensus's fix spends the loop's budget climbing INTO the
uncanny valley. The plan fix (H4 bounded micro-variation, phrase arch,
overlap, agogic timing) is what climbs OUT of it.

5. THE INHARMONICITY RE-READ (the measurements cut both ways)

The consensus leans hard on HNR ~ -2..0 dB as a renderer signature. But
"pitch without a physical source model" has a KNOWN measurement
signature, and it is NOT 0 dB: V11's ARTIC (correct F0, no tract) measured
