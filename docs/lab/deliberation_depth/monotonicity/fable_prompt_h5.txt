=== SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (take as given) ===

- A pure-Zag (deterministic, zero-RNG, byte-identical reruns) deliberation harness judges items by repeated rounds. One round = one propose → critique → eliminate cycle, with a work contract: a counted round MUST perform ≥1 evidence gather or ≥1 hypothesis elimination.
- Items present a small hypothesis set (kill-board) and a fixed-length evidence stream. Evidence items are weighted supports/attacks on hypotheses (frozen integer weights). Frozen elim margin = 900, refute threshold = 600, evidence cap = 64.
- Deliberation ends by a stopping rule, then the leading hypothesis is the judgment.
- Confidence is a DETERMINISTIC function of deliberation state via a harness-frozen margin formula: c_r = clamp(support(leader) − support(runner-up), 0, 1000) in thousandths; 1000 if only one hypothesis survives. Empirically observed values are only {400, 500, 700, 710, 764, 800, 1000} — coarse and uncalibrated. It never fires as a warning sensor: on the 127 trap items, the shallow depth-1 run is wrong on ALL 127 at the file's MAXIMUM confidence, and no warning is emitted.
- Two stopping rules were measured: (a) §6 gain-window rule: stop after round r ≥ k=3 iff the last k=3 absolute per-round confidence gains |c_i − c_{i−1}| are all < ε=0.02; hard cap 16/64. (b) The residual-flip bound: stop the moment the leader is provably unflippable given the remaining evidence stream (a sound reachability check on the bounded stream). The bound guarantees verdict == exhaustion verdict (verified: 0/997 per-item diffs vs full-exhaustion).

=== VERIFIED EMPIRICAL FINDINGS (take as ground truth; do not re-derive) ===

1. NO universal depth knee. Saturation is family-dependent: admit/revoke/logic/cost items saturate at depth 1; trap and dose-curve items at depth 8; plateau-then-flip (P) items still rising at depth 64 (+0.25 accuracy per doubling, true ceiling at or beyond 64); OVERTHINKING (O) items are strictly ANTI-MONOTONE: accuracy 1.00 → 0.75 → 0.50 → 0.25 → 0.00 as depth goes 1→2→4→8→16→32→64 (40/40 items flip correct→wrong with depth).
2. The pooled "knee at depth 8" on the ceiling battery was a CANCELLATION ARTIFACT: P gains (+0.25/doubling) exactly cancel O losses (−0.25/doubling) from depth 8 on, while neither family is saturated. Never pool anti-monotone and monotone families again.
3. §6 (gain-window) stopping fails on plateau-then-flip: a long-enough flat wrong plateau with a live runner-up defeats ANY fixed gain window — §6 stops at round 5 wrong on 40/40 P items (accuracy 0.000) where full depth reaches 1.000. This is a property of stream shape, not of the threshold.
4. The residual-flip bound fixes P (1.000, rides to the flip at rounds 6/12/20/40, 2.6–4.6× cheaper than §6 on honest batteries) but is strictly WORSE than §6 on O (0.000 vs 1.000): it preserves exhaustion, not truth. On matched misleading-tail streams, exhaustion itself is wrong. Neither rule survives both adversarial batteries. The verdict stands: the bound becomes the stopping law ONLY under an explicit written scoping assumption — valid-late-evidence (late evidence is honest disconfirmation). Where the tail may be misleading, the bound guarantees same-as-exhaustion and exhaustion may be wrong; that world needs source-trust machinery (who/what is feeding the stream), not a different stopping threshold.
5. On no-chain items (84.5% of the logic battery), depth is pure confidence theater: depth-1's trace is already maximal-quality; deeper runs are idle re-confirmation that only add rounds. Deeper traces are strict prefix-extensions of the natural trace — "eliminate earlier" is impossible by construction; depth only adds tail. Depth buys reasoning IFF there is a chain to complete (multi-hop elimination chains, misleading-prefix overturns, sub-threshold accumulation); depth HURTS iff the tail is misleading.
6. User-set depth 1 is catastrophic on traps: 0/127 correct, ALL at maximum confidence, sensor gives no warning. Ship decision: user sets a budget cap (default 8, revisable cost-budget default — NOT a truth number), TNN chooses depth inside it. Cap 8 survives only as a cost default.
7. THE NEW LAW (from the program owner, overriding all prior depth conclusions): MORE DEPTH MUST NEVER MAKE IT WORSE. Better or same, never worse. The system must be SCAFFOLD-AND-RELEASE to not lie: it must not be an overconfident machine — no confident wrong answers; when it cannot certify, it must abstain explicitly, audited.
