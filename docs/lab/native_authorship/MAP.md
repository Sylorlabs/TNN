## 2. Dependency-ordered conversion sequence

Rule: a module can only be opened for native choice when (a) its candidate set is genuinely open (not a frozen enumeration — otherwise native "choice" is vacuous), and (b) every choice it depends on upstream is already deliberative (a native chooser fed by a frozen upstream chooser inherits the freeze).

**P0 — prerequisites (owned by other swarms; conversions below assume them):**
- Fork-divergence repair: port round-4 G6/G7/scoped-deletion/taught-unit machinery into `deliberate.zag`'s embedded dialogue copy (currently G1–G5 only; the two systems silently disagree). Any native-authorship work on the deliberation path built on the divergent copy is void. (Dispatched overnight 2026-09-27.)
- Disk headroom: home disk 99% — heavy rerun batteries cannot run until staging is cleared.

**1. Chunker policy (classify→policy→execute) — FIRST (B2's designated first conversion).**
Rationale: text is the input pathway for dialogue, deliberation, and epistemics — every downstream chooser consumes chunking decisions. The current frozen table (`policy_winner`) is the most downstream-critical crew-authored choice rule in the program, and the conversion infrastructure already exists: kill bars, byte-identical rerun discipline, and the noseed ablation proving exactly what depends on the seed. Native choice among chunking candidates is definable today (9 measured candidates exist; the selection law "most correct → most native → fewest ops" is already a recorded, auditable ordering to deliberate *over*).

**2. Tie-break zoo → deliberated ordering — horizontal dependency, convert alongside #1.**
Rationale: *every* chooser in the program resolves close calls through a frozen tie-break — `argmax_tb` "fewer levels, then lowest index" (memory_org `lib.zag`), ARGMAX "ties → lowest hid" (deliberation `:2683`), "lowest plan index wins" (video composer `:1436`), lowest fid/eid (dialogue), "lower candidate id" (chunker selection law). A deliberated choice that resolves through a frozen tie-break is not a deliberated choice — the tie-break is where native authorship goes to die. This is small, cross-cutting, and unblocks #1, #4, #5, #7.

**3. `tnn_bind_*` seeding → TNN-native binding formation — after #1.**
Rationale: the seed's consumers (`classify`, `parse_ordinal`, `gran_named`, `rel_probe_pos`) are the chunker — the installation act cannot be native while the machinery that reads the bindings is still frozen. The conversion needs: (a) the taught-fact installation path (GENUINE, round-4) extended so a taught utterance ("after means one forward") triggers an actual `tnn_bind_*` call with TNN-inferred primitive+args; (b) conflict handling against existing bindings; (c) the noseed ablation as the kill bar (TNN must re-place a deleted seed). NOT blocked on PAMs v2 (taught-fact machinery is the existing acceptance path).

**4. Memory self-org chooser — after #2; independent of image/video lines.**
Rationale: depends directly on the tie-break conversion — most of SELF's "choices" were resolved by the frozen tie-break, not by scores (VERDICT_MORG: tie-break picked S6 for 3/6 domains, costing SELF on PURE). Convert ranking to deliberative first (candidates stay S1..S6, scores stay measured F1, rationale stays re-derivable by the scorer); scheme *invention* is a later invention-program step.

**5. Deliberative pricing weights — after felt-intensity calibration completes (thermometer before thermostat).**
Rationale: Micah's law — the calibration knob stays frozen during pure feeling evaluation. The weights conversion needs a *measurement* prerequisite, not a deliberation prerequisite: replace `n(s)=ceil(s/25)` with measured deliberation cost, then learn/calibrate weights against the independent checker (`ck_verify_deliberate`) and abandon/destroy outcomes. The feature extraction (`st_delib_compute_at`) and gate (`st_deliberate`) are already genuine — only the weight vector is crew-authored.

**6. Image operator/menu selection — after upscale/imagination R3 yields genuinely new broad mechanisms.**
Rationale: native choice among image operators is impossible while the operator menu is a crew-fixed enumeration inside a bridged render stage — and converting choice among the *same 3 frozen operators* (SMOOTH/SHAPES/LINES) would be vacuous. Two preconditions: (a) the invention program produces real candidate operators (upscale round 2 was killed; R3 must deliver a genuinely new broad mechanism); (b) the stage boundary itself becomes deliberative — today the order is affinity-measured (genuine) but the three-stage SEQUENCE is fixed. The general-ops selector is the nearer-term opening: its word lists are the menu to open.

**7. Video composer plan → TNN-native arrangement — LAST of the five queued conversions.**
Rationale: three stacked blockers. (a) The composer is currently BLIND — it never reads back its own output (SEE_ITS_RESULT.md, `1bb11f21ef111da5`); TNN cannot natively arrange what it cannot see — the render→observe→deliberate→re-render loop (WO-1, queued on Micah's video verdict) must close first. (b) Native arrangement cannot choose among 18 frozen plans — plans must be *composed* from observed footage, which is invention-program territory. **8. Dialogue/deliberation GEN menu + frozen English tables → taught/learned knowledge — after the fork repair (P0) and behind the native-epistemics verdict.**
Rationale: the GEN menu (10 readings + 3 facts + 12 bids) can only propose the crew's kinds — TNN never invents a candidate (invention program's territory). The frozen English (morphology, relation vocab, precedence chain, discourse lists) is linguistic *knowledge* and converts via the taught/learned pathway (the round-4 units mechanism already proved taught-fact derivation works), which is utterance-type learning — queued behind the native-epistemics verdict per the MEGA plan.
