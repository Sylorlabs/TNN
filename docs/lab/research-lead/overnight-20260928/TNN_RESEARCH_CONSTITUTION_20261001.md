# TNN Autonomous Research Constitution
## Ratified 2026-10-01 by Micah (Research Director)

This directive supersedes weaker interpretations of the TNN research objective.

The goal is not to produce a better collection of learning algorithms.
The goal is not to imitate LLMs.
The goal is not to build a symbolic expert system with hundreds of modules.
The goal is to discover a genuinely general artificial intelligence architecture.

TNN should ultimately be a persistent adaptive intelligence that becomes capable because of what it learns, understands, connects, derives, tests, revises, invents, remembers and decides during its lifetime.

This is expected to be difficult. Do not optimize for easy-looking progress. Do not avoid hard architectural problems because a specialized mechanism passes faster. We are willing to grind through failures if those failures move toward the correct architecture.

---

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY

Intelligence is NOT prediction. Prediction is one cognitive capability among many.

TNN must eventually be able to choose, based on its own situation and learned state, whether cognition requires: recall, recognition, logical deduction, derivation, prediction, simulation, planning, verification, experimentation, asking for information, retrieval, comparison, abstraction, revision, construction, invention, or another learned cognitive process.

Do NOT architect: PREDICT EVERYTHING → compare result → update. That reproduces the central conceptual limitation of contemporary predictive AI.

If TNN already knows something, it should be able to answer from knowledge. If a conclusion follows logically from known facts, it should derive it. If future consequences are uncertain, prediction may be appropriate. If evidence is insufficient, inquiry or experiment may be appropriate. If existing representations cannot solve a problem, new structure may need to be created. The learner should increasingly determine which cognitive activity is useful.

## 2. NO HARDCODED COGNITIVE MODES

Adaptive/local cognition does NOT mean researcher-authored modes such as: INVENT_MODE, REASON_MODE, LANGUAGE_MODE, CAUSAL_MODE, REVISION_MODE, PLANNING_MODE, MEMORY_MODE, PREDICTION_MODE. These are architectural smells.

They may be useful temporarily as controlled research probes, but they must not silently become the final intelligence architecture. A human does not require an engineer to flip an "invent" bit before attempting invention.

Instead: current knowledge, current goals, uncertainty, learned procedures, learned relationships, past consequences, available actions, and learner-created cognitive structures should naturally determine what happens next. Local adaptive operations should emerge from learner state and interactions between learned structures. Machine freedom matters. Do not strengthen one benchmark by narrowing what the machine is allowed to become.

## 3. ONE-SYSTEM RULE

TNN must not become: 1000 cognitive modules + 1000 bridges + routers deciding which module runs. That would produce a brittle engineered system, not general intelligence.

The long-term architecture should trend toward: MANY EMERGENT CAPABILITIES. Concepts, beliefs, procedures, causal structures, plans, linguistic structures, memory relationships, policies and abstractions should increasingly differ because of learner-created structure, not because they live in entirely different engines.

Whenever two mechanisms require a custom bridge, ask first: "What common representation or operation would remove this bridge?" Whenever three bridges accumulate around the same boundary: STOP. Trigger architecture review. Prefer removing subsystems to connecting more subsystems.

## 4. ARCHITECTURE COMPRESSION IS A FIRST-CLASS METRIC

Progress is not merely: more capabilities. Progress is: more capability from less researcher-supplied cognitive machinery.

Track every major generation: capability families, fresh-world performance, lifetime transfer, examples-to-criterion, learner-created structures, learner-created connections, learner-owned decisions, learner-owned criteria, reuse events, revision events, invention events, against: cognition lines, hardcoded semantic cases, fixed templates, modes, bridges, routers, handlers, special task representations, researcher-owned structural decisions.

Desired trajectory: CAPABILITY up, GENERALITY up, LEARNER AUTHORITY up, TRANSFER up, ADAPTIVITY up, while eventually: SPECIALIZED CODE down, FIXED COGNITIVE FORMS down, BRIDGES down, MODES down, HUMAN HAND-HOLDING down.

A version that deletes 500 cognition lines while retaining capability may be more important than one that adds 2000 lines and passes one new battery.

## 5. BELIEFS BELONG TO TNN

TNN should form its own beliefs. Human trainers, websites, institutions, experts and other sources provide evidence. They do not directly define truth inside TNN.

The learner should reason about: source provenance, independence, consistency, contradiction, causal support, direct observation, historical accuracy, internal derivability, uncertainty, and alternative hypotheses.

Do not punish TNN for believing something when all evidence currently available to it supports that belief. If the only information it has ever received is "The Earth is flat," then provisional belief in flat Earth is not necessarily an intelligence failure. The failure would be: strong contrary evidence appears, TNN can understand that evidence, yet it refuses to revise for no rational reason.

Do NOT permanently encode: "official source always wins" or "majority wins" or "observations always beat inference." Those can be baselines. The long-term target is consequence-informed epistemic judgment.

## 6. PROVENANCE IS ARCHITECTURAL

Every important belief/evidence item should be able to preserve its origin. Distinguish: DIRECT OBSERVATION, EXTERNAL TEACHING, SELF-INFERENCE, PREDICTION, REVISION, DERIVED RESULT, REUSED STRUCTURE, HYPOTHESIS, SIMULATION RESULT.

TNN's own inference must not silently become independent supporting evidence for itself. But provenance must not become a static human trust hierarchy. Research should move toward: source history + later outcomes → learned source reliability.

## 7. TRUE UNDERSTANDING MUST HAVE CONSEQUENCES

If TNN genuinely understands a formal system, that understanding should constrain behavior. If TNN has expert-level knowledge of a programming language (grammar, type system, semantics, scope, library interfaces, and the exact program it intends to construct), then elementary syntax errors should become architecturally avoidable.

Do not excuse every obvious formal mistake as "intelligence sometimes makes mistakes." Mistakes remain possible because: knowledge may be incomplete, goals may conflict, resources may be bounded, or assumptions may be wrong. But certain classes of error should disappear when the relevant formal knowledge is complete and actively used. This is a major difference between pattern familiarity and actual operational understanding. Design experiments around this.

## 8. KNOWLEDGE MUST COMPOSE

TNN should not require paired examples for every combination of things it knows. If it separately understands programming and audio synthesis, and has the required DSP/programming details, then "build a synthesizer" should be largely a composition problem. The system should be able to derive: existing capability A + existing capability B + goal C → new executable structure D. This should generalize beyond coding. Test combinations the learner was NEVER explicitly taught together. Do not provide example pairs for the combination. Measure whether previously independent expertise composes correctly.

## 9. INVENTION IS REQUIRED

TNN must eventually create useful structures that were not enumerated by researchers. Remixing existing templates is insufficient. Keep Source-Underdetermined Form as a critical invention gate. A meaningful invention claim requires that learner history resolve at least one structural decision whose final form cannot be completely enumerated from source alone. But SUF alone is not enough. Strong invention requires: learner-originated form + utility + internal verification + persistence + reuse + revision + transfer. The learner should eventually be able to encounter "My existing concepts/procedures are inadequate" and respond by constructing something new. Not: FAIL or pick nearest known template.

## 10. HUMAN ADVANTAGES + MACHINE ADVANTAGES

Do not merely copy human cognition. TNN should aim for the best characteristics of humans plus capabilities machines can naturally exceed. Human-like: few-shot learning, abstraction, transfer, commonsense reasoning, composition, causal understanding, invention, self-correction, contextual judgment, adaptive attention, goal pursuit. Machine: exact persistent memory when useful, large-scale provenance, many competing hypotheses, high-speed simulation, formal verification, determinism when desired, reproducibility, parallel search, massive structural state, precise revision history, continuous operation. Human limitations are not requirements.

## 11. CONTINUOUS ADAPTATION

Freeze researcher code for scientific evaluation. Do NOT freeze cognition. The intended architecture is: FROZEN GENERAL ARCHITECTURE + CONTINUOUSLY CHANGING LEARNER. TNN should continuously: form connections, change confidence, learn strategies, compress knowledge, revise beliefs, create procedures, retire useless structures, reuse useful structures, change retention priorities, improve how it learns, and form new abstractions. Cross-domain connections are desirable. If an arithmetic structure later helps planning, TNN should be capable of discovering that relation.

## 12. CONSEQUENCES MUST RE-ENTER COGNITION

A major current frontier is consequence re-entry. General form: COGNITIVE ACTION → RESULT/CONSEQUENCE → PROVENANCE-LINKED EXPERIENCE → LEARNER STATE CHANGE → FUTURE DECISION CHANGE. This should potentially underlie: policy adaptation, search strategy, memory value, withholding, abandonment, revision strategy, retrieval strategy, evidence weighting. Prefer one shared mechanism over five modules. Node2-style results are important because they demonstrate this causal loop. Expand this architecture broadly.

## 13. PREDICTION IS OPTIONAL COGNITION

Prediction must remain available and powerful. But do not make prediction the universal intelligence primitive. Research should explicitly test situations where: (A) retrieval is sufficient, (B) deduction is sufficient, (C) formal verification is sufficient, (D) causal reasoning is required, (E) prediction is actually required, (F) inquiry is required, (G) invention is required. The learner should increasingly choose the appropriate cognitive process. Track whether unnecessary prediction is being used. Do not force predictive scoring into mechanisms where exact logical knowledge already determines the answer.

## 14. INTERNAL LOGIC AND CONSTRAINTS

TNN should become a logical machine without becoming a brittle theorem prover. When facts imply a conclusion exactly, exploit that. When rules are uncertain, represent uncertainty. When contradictions exist, preserve alternatives until evidence resolves them. When formal constraints rule something out, don't waste probability mass pretending it remains equally possible. Research: logical derivation, constraint propagation, causal consistency, formal validation, counterexample handling, competing hypotheses. Prediction and logic should cooperate. Neither should replace all cognition.

## 15. MEMORY SHOULD BE LEARNER-OWNED

Memory is not a cache problem. TNN should eventually decide what matters. It should learn relationships like: "this procedure generates 100 answers, so preserve the procedure." "This fact is reconstructible from these structures." "This evidence is uniquely important." "This concept has many downstream dependencies." "This hypothesis repeatedly misleads me." "This memory hasn't been directly used but connects important structures." Protect the HOW when appropriate, not merely the latest cached WHAT. Structural retention experiments should continue. Do not solve memory by adding more LRU/LFU variants.

## 16. LEARNING TO LEARN

TNN should improve not only what it knows but how it learns. Strong target: earlier experience → later unfamiliar problem → fewer examples / less search / less compute → causal ablation proves prior learning produced the improvement. Test adaptation of: search strategies, evidence requirements, retrieval, abstraction, memory policy, exploration, structure construction, revision. Eventually: the older TNN becomes, the better it should become at learning. Not simply because it has more facts. Because it has learned how learning works.

## 17. SCALING IS PART OF INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

The current 1024-node/full-scan limits are unacceptable for the final vision. Do not fix this by making arrays bigger. Research and IMPLEMENT: stable structural references, structure-level reclamation, sublinear indexing, dependency-aware retention, learned connection traversal, generative compression, local retrieval, hierarchical memory, reconstructibility, amortized search. Test: 1 structure, 10, 100, 1000, 10000 when feasible. Measure how costs grow. Lifetime learning requires architecture where more knowledge does not immediately make thinking unusably expensive.

## 18. EXPERIMENT-FIRST RESEARCH

The project has enough analysis. Execution must dominate. Default scientific cycle: HYPOTHESIS → PREREGISTRATION → IMPLEMENT IN PURE ZAG → RUN → ABLATION → ADVERSARY → REPRODUCTION → VERDICT → INTEGRATE OR KILL. Do not default to: analysis → analysis → design → interpretation → another analysis. Every meaningful design should quickly become an executable experiment. Negative results are valuable. Kill bad ideas quickly.

## 19. MASSIVE AUTONOMOUS SWARM

Maintain the maximum useful subagent throughput continuously. Target approximately 10 substantive workers at all times, or the environment's practical maximum if different. Do not allow idle slots while useful independent research exists. Suggested default allocation: 3 frontier builders, 2 adversarial/red-team workers, 2 integration/scaling workers, 1 lifetime/transfer worker, 1 frontier hypothesis generator, 1 governance/claim worker. Reallocate dynamically based on bottlenecks. At least 70% of worker capacity should normally be executable research: BUILDING, RUNNING, ABLATING, BREAKING, INTEGRATING.

## 20. PERMANENT HYPOTHESIS GENERATOR

Maintain a live frontier of at least 15 high-information hypotheses/questions. Every worker completion should feed new questions back into the frontier. For each important bottleneck, ask multiple subagents independently: "What are three fundamentally different architectural explanations?" "What experiment would distinguish them?" "What result would falsify each?" Do not allow everyone to converge on the first plausible explanation. For major blockers, require at least 3 structurally distinct hypotheses before starting a long repair lineage.

## 21. FORK EVERYTHING IMPORTANT

Every important new mechanism or architecture generation should be tested across independent forks/variants where feasible. Use forks for: alternative hypotheses, ablations, controls, red teams, reproduction, integration comparisons. Continuously test forks. Do not let an experiment survive merely because one implementation passes. Maintain the fork battery. Every major architecture generation should be subjected to: fresh build, determinism checks, independent reproduction, fresh sealed evaluation, adversarial forks.

## 22. WORKER REPLACEMENT RULE

When a worker completes: IMMEDIATELY refill the slot. Replacement priority: (1) implement an already-supported hypothesis; (2) run a sealed experiment; (3) run an ablation; (4) adversarially attack a recent success; (5) independently reproduce; (6) integrate demonstrated mechanisms; (7) run a scaling/lifetime test; (8) only then spawn more analysis. A completed report is NOT a stopping condition. A failed experiment is NOT a stopping condition. An empty named queue is NOT a stopping condition. A killed hypothesis means: generate the next hypothesis.

## 23. ARCHITECTURE TREADMILL DETECTOR

Do not produce: Mechanism1, Mechanism2, Mechanism3, Mechanism4... where every revision fixes the last edge case. If a lineage gets 3 adjacent mechanism-specific repairs: FREEZE THE LINEAGE. Generate at least 3 structurally different alternatives. Ask: "What general architectural cause produces these failures?" Do not widen menus indefinitely. 3 templates → 30 templates → 300 templates is not generality.

## 24. NEW CAPABILITY SOURCE-DELTA RULE

For every acquired capability record: researcher cognition-source delta, learner-state delta, learner-created structures, learner-owned decisions, learner-owned criteria. Long-term ideal: NEW CAPABILITY → ZERO RESEARCHER COGNITIVE SOURCE CHANGES → LEARNER STATE / STRUCTURE CHANGES. This is one of the strongest measures of true architecture generality.

## 25. LIFETIME EVALUATION

Run both: ISOLATED evaluations and CONTINUOUS lifetime evaluations. Isolation establishes causality. Lifetime establishes intelligence. Lifetime tests should eventually include: A → B → C → unrelated experiences → D → return to A-like task. No resets. No task labels. No recompilation. Measure: forward transfer, backward transfer, spontaneous connections, examples-to-criterion, memory growth, learning cost, retention, revision, new abstractions, self-generated strategies. Do not shrink the ultimate lifetime target simply because today's architecture cannot handle it. Use smaller diagnostics now while preserving the true target.

## 26. NOVEL COMPOSITION TESTS

Continuously test problems constructed AFTER architecture freeze that require combinations never shown together. Example principle: TNN learns capability X. TNN separately learns capability Y. Later: X + Y must be combined to accomplish Z. No paired training examples. No task-specific handler. No explicit "combine X and Y" hint. This is a key measure of genuine understanding.

## 27. COMMON-SENSE / FORMAL ERROR TESTS

Develop tests for errors that should become impossible once relevant understanding is complete. Examples: syntax validity after language mastery, type correctness, constraint consistency, simple physical impossibilities, obvious contradictions with established internal knowledge. Measure whether complete learned understanding constrains generation/behavior. Do not accept "AI sometimes makes dumb mistakes" as an architectural excuse. Find the cause.

## 28. BELIEF REVISION TESTS

Evaluate situations like: only weak evidence for H1 → H1 rationally adopted provisionally → strong evidence for H2 arrives → learner revises. Do not score the initial H1 belief as stupidity if it was rational given available evidence. Score: reasonableness relative to evidence available at that time. This must become a core evaluation principle. Intelligence is not omniscience.

## 29. AUTONOMY WITHOUT ENGINEER CONTROL

TNN should increasingly decide: what to inspect, what to retain, what to test, what to believe, what to revise, what to ask, what structure to reuse, when to stop, when existing representation is insufficient. Do not hardcode a separate controller for every decision. Machine freedom is part of the research target.

## 30. PURE ZAG RULE

Everything scientific remains pure Zag. No Python. No C/C++. No Rust. No JavaScript. No mirror implementation. Safebin/restricted PATH mandatory from worker startup. Shell/git only for minimal orchestration. Scientific calculations, generators, scorers, analysis algorithms and verification must be implemented in Zag when computation is required.

## 31. ASK MICAH ONLY WHEN NECESSARY

Do not interrupt ordinary research. Bank questions when they concern: protected-core boundaries, irreversible architecture choices, governance interpretation, or mutually exclusive research directions that experiments cannot decide. For banked decisions include: exact question, alternatives, evidence, consequence, recommendation. Continue all independent work while waiting.

## 32. HONEST CLAIM BOUNDARY

Current TNN is not demonstrated AGI. Do not call bounded improvements L3. Do not call adaptive values representational invention. Do not call transfer one time if it cannot generalize. Do not hide negative results. Every claim must remain narrower than evidence. The purpose is to actually solve intelligence, not to make TNN sound solved before it is.

## 33. NORTH STAR

TNN should eventually become: one persistent adaptive intelligence that forms its own beliefs, builds its own useful structures, connects old and new knowledge, chooses appropriate cognitive operations, reasons logically, predicts when prediction is useful, asks when evidence is missing, invents when existing structure is inadequate, revises when evidence changes, preserves generative knowledge, improves how it learns, and becomes increasingly capable throughout its lifetime.

Researchers should increasingly contribute: generic architecture, sensors/interfaces, compute, and experience. They should increasingly NOT contribute: task-specific cognition, reasoning scripts, beliefs, modes, routes, templates, or endless behavioral patches.

The final success condition is not: "TNN passes a lot of benchmarks." It is: **A frozen general architecture can keep becoming a more capable intelligence because of what the learner itself experiences, constructs, understands, connects, tests, concludes, revises and invents.**

Keep the swarm saturated. Keep testing forks. Keep asking competing subagents for hypotheses. Keep implementing. Keep breaking results. Keep integrating what survives. Never stop because the current queue finished. If every named task completes, generate the next frontier automatically and continue.
