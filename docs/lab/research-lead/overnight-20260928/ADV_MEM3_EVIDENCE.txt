## X-M3-4 source audit record

Audited file: mem3_learn.zag at commit 903a2c16c (mechanism = lines 1-352,
everything before "fn main" at line 353; harness = fn main).
Method: full read of the mechanism plus a complete diff against
mem2_learn.zag at the same tree (400 diff lines, all deltas match the five
documented repairs R1-R5; no undocumented mechanism change).

  (a) R1 selected-policy counterfactual: pressure() computes
      pv3=victim(bestp,ev,1) and uv=victim(bestp,ev,0) where bestp is the
      argmin winner; verdict logic: pv3<0 -> fallback-path :0 message;
      pv3==uv -> ":0 (probation changed nothing for the selected policy)";
      else -> ":1 (probation shielded proc<uv pid> from churn)". Matches prereg.

      FALLBACK-ALL-PROTECTED when the protected victim is <0; pressure()

      consistently for the printed candidate table and is_strict_up.
  (c) R3 dist_differs(): compares per-proc frequency vectors (16 proc slots)
      of the future vs the W=win window (lo clamped at 0 when nq<win); all
      call sites pass win=20. Order-insensitive by construction (frequency
      vectors, not sequences); a same-multiset-different-order future would
