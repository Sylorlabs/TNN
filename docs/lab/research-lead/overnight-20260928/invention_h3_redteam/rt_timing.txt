Visit counts match the full-enumeration law visits = b*(b^P-1)/(b-1)
EXACTLY at every config (verified: 3*(3^5-1)/2=363, 6*(6^7-1)/5=335922,
etc.). The pruning (last_rel at final level, rel_exact reachability)
contributes nothing in the dense case: the tree is fully explored.

Wall time: 13 configs x (counter + real invent_try) = 26 full traversals,
~1.08M total visits, ~48 s wall (~44 us/visit incl. teach/init overhead).

Extrapolation at max depth P=7 (hard cap in invent_try):
  b=10:  ~11.1M visits  -> ~8 min per query
  b=15:  ~183M visits   -> ~2.2 hours per query
  b=20:  ~1.34B visits  -> ~16 hours per query
Infeasibility knee: roughly b=12..15 outgoing facts per value at P=7.
The mechanism is feasible in practice ONLY because plen is hard-capped
at 7 (attack 2, A2d: plen=8 rejected outright). That cap is both the
safety rail and the scope ceiling: no chain longer than 7 can ever be
invented, by construction.
