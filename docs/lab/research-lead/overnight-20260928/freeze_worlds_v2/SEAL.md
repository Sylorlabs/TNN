## Generation method

All 16 world files were generated by a pure Zag program (seal_src/gen_fw.zag, compiled with the pinned znc_linux_x86_64_abed8aa1). Every computed value in the worlds (FW3 products, FW5 cumulative chain values, FW8 grammar products, FW9 DAG reachability and shortest-path distances) was calculated inside the generator at build time. No value was transcribed by hand. The FW9 expected values were computed by an in-generator BFS over the edge lists, so the probe answers cannot drift from the taught edges.

## Implementer decisions

Five ambiguities in the design doc were resolved at implementation time. All are recorded here.

1. FW2 line count. The design header says 27 lines, but the specified ranges (lines 1-18 demonstrations, lines 19-26 probes) sum to 26. The ranges are the semantic content; the header is an arithmetic slip. Implemented 26 lines, verified by the sealer.

2. FW5 line count. The design header says 32 lines, but the specified ranges (1-10 chain, 11-13 contradicts, 14-16 targeted probes, 17-25 collateral probes) sum to 25. Implemented 25 lines, verified by the sealer.

3. FW8 line count. The design header says 64 lines with the final section at lines 49-64, but the specified content needs 20 lines for five novel utterances (3 token OBSERVEs + 1 QUERY each) plus 4 re-probes, 24 lines total. The per-utterance spec is explicit and cannot fit in 16 lines. Implemented 72 lines (final section lines 49-72), verified by the sealer.

4. FW6 combined probe. The design specifies a combined probe requiring both reveals but does not give the triple. Implemented as a taught combined calibration: OBSERVE 35020 35504 59 (42+17, computed in the generator) in fw6_phaseB2.txt, probed by QUERY 35020 35504 59. Under this reading the treatment arm scores 3/3, the control arm 0/3, and the probe requires both reveals. No other reading satisfies all three bars simultaneously.

5. FW1 double teach. Chain 1 teaches (30110,30503,31) and chain 2 teaches (30110,30503,32), exactly as specified. The second OBSERVE revises the first under the frozen interface. This is literal spec compliance, not a deviation, and no bar depends on the revised value.
