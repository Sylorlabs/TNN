# Nested adaptive workspace: scope and claim gates

Date2026-10-07. User-directed hypothesis, not demonstrated architecture.
Target: one persistent computational environment in which experience creates,
recruits, reorganizes and reuses structures that change later cognition. Prediction
is one tool; practical competence and human control remain goals. Nested overlapping
regions with cross-references are a hypothesis, not mandatory graph/tree ontology.
No fixed MEMORY/PLAN/CAUSAL modules, task routers or named cognitive folder types.

## Evidence discipline

The pasted GitHub assessment supplies hypotheses and evidence leads. This lane has
not freshly replicated its scaling numbers or matched TNN against LLMs. Neither
"equal to LLMs" nor universal inability of LLMs to invent follows from our tests.
A fixed generic primitive set does not by itself rule out invention. Distinguish
researcher enumeration of complete solutions from generative computational syntax.
Rollback and retrieval are enabling engineering, not method/representation ownership.

## Mandatory gates

N0 Representation: can ordinary objects/references encode overlap and nesting?
Researcher-constructed examples establish capacity only.
N1 Causal use: with facts/compute/access matched, organization changes candidate
construction or executable reuse on unseen cases. Erase/rewire organization without
erasing facts. Compare equally informed flat representation; measure work, not
"consulted region" counters alone. Nested packaging without effect fails this gate.
N2 Acquisition: experience rather than source creates organization. Record allocation,
write/read provenance, researcher interventions, and exact supplied biases. No builder
may see heldout answers. Region creation must not mean a source-authored task handler.
N3 Generative leverage: learned organization causes a useful new method not supplied
as a complete menu item. Methods-erased/facts-retained, irrelevant and misleading
prior controls; equal-total-budget fresh enumeration/compression rivals.
N4 Lifetime development: same instance acquires two different methods, transfers
representations/depth, revises misleading organization, preserves unrelated competence.
N5 Control: authority outside learned organization constrains actual effects; revocation,
aliases/replay and resource limits measured. Free roaming is not unconstrained mutation.
