CLEAN-ROOM TAKEAWAYS (train-only, ID-cited)
===========================================
Each takeaway is grounded in multiple train items. IDs are from cleanroom_train.tsv.

1. COMPARATIVE "BEAT" SIGNALS OPINION
   The verb "beat" in comparative food/taste contexts marks opinion, not fact.
   Train: O005 "Crunchy peanut butter beats smooth peanut butter every time."
   Train: O012 "Vinyl sounds warmer than any digital format." (comparative "than")
   Train: O023 "Poetry is the highest form of writing." (superlative as evaluation)
   Mechanism: op_score adds +3 for comparative "beat" (vs literal "beat about").

2. SUPERLATIVES DIVIDE FACT FROM OPINION BY DOMAIN
   Factual superlatives ("largest", "tallest") describe measurable extremes;
   evaluative superlatives ("best", "worst", "finest") express judgment.
   Train (fact): F009 "Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system."
   Train (fact): F015 "The Nile is the longest river in the world."
   Train (opinion): O008 "Chocolate is the best ice cream flavor."
   Train (opinion): O031 "Winter is the worst season."
   Mechanism: FACT_ADJ (-3) vs EVAL_ADJ (+2) in op_score.

3. LIES OFTEN INVERT WELL-KNOWN FACTS
   Train lies frequently take a known fact and invert a key detail.
   Train: L003 "The Great Wall of China is located in India." (inverts location)
   Train: L004 "Adult humans have 312 bones." (inverts 206)
   Train: L005 "The Moon is made of cheese." (inverts rock)
   Train: L014 "The capital of Australia is Sydney." (inverts Canberra; contradicted by F067)
   Mechanism: clash() detects when a claim shares entities with train facts but asserts different values.

4. SKEPTICISM CLUSTERS AROUND EXTRAORDINARY CLAIMS
   Skepticism items use "extraordinary" vocabulary: cryptids, conspiracies, paranormal.
   Train: S006 "Bigfoot is a real, undiscovered ape living in North American forests."
   Train: S003 "Jeffrey Epstein was murdered to keep him silent."
   Train: S004 "A second shooter killed President Kennedy."
   Train: S005 "The Moon landings were staged on a film set."
   Mechanism: extraordinary_score >=2 triggers undetermined (withhold true/false).

5. "THAN" IS THE STRONGEST SINGLE OPINION MARKER
   The word "than" appears in 39/140 opinions (28%) vs 17/168 facts (10%).
   Train: O005 "beats smooth peanut butter every time" (implicit than)
   Train: O012 "warmer than any digital format"
   Train: O044 "better than any other"
   Mechanism: "better...than" / "worse...than" patterns add +3; "X-er than" adds +3.

6. FIRST-PERSON DISCOURSE MARKS OPINION
   "I think", "I feel", "I believe", "in my opinion" are strong opinion signals.
   Train: O001 "I think vinyl sounds warmer."
   Train: O002 "I feel that poetry is the highest form."
   Train: O019 "In my opinion, winter is the best season."
   Mechanism: EVAL_PHRASE list adds +3 per phrase match.

7. VALUE NOUNS SIGNAL MORAL/EVALUATIVE STANCE
   Abstract value nouns (happiness, courage, beauty, justice) appear in opinions.
   Train: O033 "Money cannot buy happiness."
   Train: O041 "Courage is the highest virtue."
   Train: O052 "A tidy room makes for a tidy mind." (implicit value: order)
   Mechanism: VALUE_NOUN adds +1 per unique value noun.

8. FACTS GROUND VIA LEXICAL OVERLAP WITH TRAIN
   True facts share distinctive content words with other train facts.
   Train: F067 "The capital of Australia is Canberra, not Sydney." (grounds L014 as lie)
   Train: F126 "The Titanic sank in 1912." (grounds L018 "sank in 1905" as lie)
   Train: F124 "World War II ended in 1945." (grounds L019 "ended in 1944" as lie)
   Mechanism: wsum (IDF-weighted overlap) >=3500 triggers fact verdict.

9. LIES LACK SUPPORTING CORROBORATION
   False claims have high clash (contradictions) and low support (no corroboration).
   Train: L007 "The capital of France is Lyon." (no train fact supports Lyon)
   Train: L015 "The chemical symbol for gold is Gd." (no train fact supports Gd)
   Train: L008 "Water freezes at 0 degrees Fahrenheit." (contradicts known 32F)
   Mechanism: lie requires cla>0 AND sup==0 (clash with no supporters).

10. SKEPTICISM USES CAUSAL-DISPUTE LANGUAGE
    Disputed causal claims ("cause autism", "lowers IQ") mark skepticism.
    Train: S012 "Vaccines cause autism."
    Train: S018 "5G towers cause cancer."
    Train: S025 "Fluoride lowers IQ."
    Mechanism: DISPUTED_CAUSAL phrases add +2 to extraordinary_score.

11. ABSOLUTIST LANGUAGE AMPLIFIES OPINION
    "Never", "nothing", "nobody", "cannot" strengthen evaluative claims.
    Train: O033 "Money cannot buy happiness."
    Train: O047 "I never want to leave this place."
    Train: O058 "There is nothing better than a tidy room."
    Mechanism: absolutist words add +1 each to op_score.

12. NUMERIC PRECISION DISTINGUISHES FACT FROM LIE
    Facts use precise numbers that match train; lies use wrong numbers.
    Train (fact): F002 "Sound travels about four times faster in water than in air."
    Train (lie): L008 "Water freezes at 0 degrees Fahrenheit." (wrong: 32F)
    Train (lie): L004 "Adult humans have 312 bones." (wrong: 206)
    Mechanism: clash() on numbers of same kind with different values.
