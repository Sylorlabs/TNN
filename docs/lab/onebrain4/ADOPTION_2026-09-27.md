## What is adopted

The one-brain full-deliberation mode — parallel sub-deliberations sharing one
ledger/state, with the reintegration gate — is adopted as TNN's canonical
deliberation architecture.

Adopted configuration:
- Shared-ledger one-brain deliberation: YES (adopted)
- Reintegration gate: ON (adopted — R4: reintegration carries +11, 35/44 vs 24/44)
- Fourth voice (V4, branch duel / challenger): EXCLUDED from the canonical core —
  remains experimental under the continued V4-envelope test line (see below)

## Evidence basis

- Round 3 pass: onebrain > single deliberation (+18) — replicated in Round 4 on a
  fresh 44-item set (onebrain 30/44 vs single 12/44), all modes byte-identical
  across 3 reruns, zero RNG.
- The minimal scaffold matches the full mode (min 30/44 = onebrain 30/44) — the
  gain is TNN's own decision, not scaffolding.
- Reintegration alone carries +11 over the matched no-reintegration condition
  (nov4 35/44 vs nov4nG 24/44).
- Round 4 red team: 7 attacks, no kill; independent rebuild byte-identical;
  hardcode audit clean.
- Evidence commit: `c39b3b7a52d62c79b808486f36f0ccfadca65e6f` (`docs/lab/onebrain4/`).

## Explicitly not adopted (still open lines)

- V4 stays experimental: R4 measured a net −5 (onebrain 30 vs nov4 35), causally
  pinned to the inert duel + `audit_invalidate` on q01–q05, but V4 HELPED on fresh
  item r02 (design-entangled harm, not a general law). A continued test series with
  pre-registered help-vs-harm predictions and fork-gate margin sweeps is running to
  characterize the honest envelope. V4 enters the canonical core only if that line
  finds a reliable help region and Micah signs a further amendment.
- The per-item mechanism of WHY reintegration helps (+14 fixes) and harms
  (3 harms, q06–q08) is under white-box investigation with intervention proofs; the
  outcome may refine the reintegration rule, not the adoption of the architecture.
