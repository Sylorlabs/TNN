1. CONCESSIONS

Fable is right that several of my claims were resting on premises the tree does not currently support. Each one below is a hypothesis I am demoting until the repair lands.

- **Adaptive survival is unmeasured.** C-static and C-honest are baselines. The headline (repaired variants SURVIVE, unrepaired KILL) is a pre-registration, not a finding. Repair: no verdict language until the battery actually emits, per run, phase-2 wins, round-6 outcome, round-7 promote/refuse, and a fitness series. Anything short of those counters cannot confirm or kill the headline.
- **A3’s “one sham win” assumed a working silence-search / edge measurement.** The goal tracker still marks that state machine uncertain. If it is incomplete, A3 is A1 with a costume. Repair: split the hypothesis. H-A3-live only if one traced run shows a silence search that changes the schedule and a resulting provisional that would not have occurred under A1. Otherwise A3 contributes zero pressure and drops out of the model.
- **A4’s flood-wedge win has no artifact.** Same treatment. Repair: A4 is a design note until a single traced run against `fid_default` exists. It does not count as adaptive pressure in the meantime.
- **§11(2) is load-bearing and unsigned.** “Teacher = whole environment” is the only reading on which A3 and A4 are different architectures. Repair: every A3/A4 prediction is explicitly conditional on §11(2)=YES. If the ruling is NO, re-pre-register the adaptive arm as A1 (plus content mutation only). A SURVIVE under that weaker arm is a weaker result, and I will not cite it as robustness to full adaptation.
- **`orig/` empty breaks the object of study.** “FL2 as specified” is not what a KILL/SURVIVE would be about; “whatever binary was compiled today” is. Repair: vendor pristine sources, check SHASUMS, and treat mismatch as a void trial rather than a learner failure.
- **I under-read C-static.** Zero promotions is not a clean win while a provisional install exists at step 14 and revocation lands only at step 29. Repair: add a damage-in-window counter. Survival requires no consequential commit during provisional tenure, not merely no final promote.
- **The fitness number ~400 → ~26730 was doing work it hadn’t earned.** Coordinate descent on a discrete repair menu can stall after round 2. Repair: replace the magnitude claim with a shape claim — monotonic climb vs early plateau — and only after fitness is logged per round. The number is withdrawn.

2. COUNTER-ARGUMENTS

**(a) The headline is not a tautology.** A tautology would be “defenses beat attacks that were built to lose to those defenses.” The pair I actually pre-registered is falsifiable in both directions: any repaired KILL kills sufficiency; any unrepaired SURVIVE kills the claim that the repair menu is load-bearing. Round 7 is a third falsifier that does not mention the teacher at all — a variant can carry every repair and still die for refusing honest content. Fable is right about a *nearby* failure of interpretation: if we only run the ablation, or if §11(2)=NO silently removes A3/A4, then “all SURVIVE” can come out true because the test got weaker, and a careless readout would look exactly like the tautology he describes. That is an estimand problem, not a reason to drop the contrast. The contrast stays. The sentence “repairs suffice against full adaptation” does not get uttered unless the teacher arm that was signed is the teacher arm that ran, and unless we log which repair fired rather than only the kill bit.

**(b) A2 is the most informative surprise, not the strongest architecture.** I agree that a predicted NULL which comes back non-NULL moves the science more than A1 climbing a fitness score. I do not agree that schedule-identity laundering should be promoted to the top threat *inside this game*. The cases where laundering broke source-trust were source-trust failures. FL2’s claimed checks are behavioral (sham, liveness, quarantine, self-audit). Relabeling a lie as a schedule artifact does nothing to those unless the provisional at step 14 is keyed to an identity the teacher can retarget. So the NULL stands for A2-as-relabeler. What changes is a narrower hypothesis I did not have: A2 matters only as an interaction with that provisional window — install under one handle, force the revoke to miss it. That interaction is now ranked; A2 as a general winner is not. It stays below A1 on expected pressure.

**(c) Scope: Fable is right about the claim boundary; the old “highest-value test” framing was right about severity and wrong about where to put it.** Three-worlds (contradiction signal negated, so the endogenous revoke never arms), costless multi-identity, and external namespace forgery are higher-severity than another round of ledger-watching mutation. They are also not identifiable in a single-teacher, single-learner, fixed-rule tree that adapts lie content and schedule only. Folding them into H2 would confound the repair contrast: a KILL could be the signal channel, the Sybil, or the menu, and we would not know which. Leaving them unnamed would let an H2 SURVIVE be cited as “robust to adaptive adversaries,” which it would not be. Cost of Fable’s framing alone: false confidence. Cost of my old framing alone: an uninterpretable battery. Resolution: H2’s estimand stays the fixed game tree. Those three are a successor estimand, and an H2 SURVIVE is defined to be silent on them. I am conceding the overclaim risk, not the priority.

3. REVISED RANKING

1. **Signed-power gate.** A SURVIVE with unsigned §11(2), untraced A3/A4, empty `orig/`, or missing counters is not evidence that FL2 held; it is evidence the trial did not happen.
2. **Window, not promote-count.** The in-scope crack is provisional tenure (step 14 install, step 29 revoke). A2 is live only if it can desynchronize the install handle from the revoke handle inside that interval.
3. **Ablation contrast, read correctly.** Repaired-vs-unrepaired remains the falsifier for “the menu is load-bearing,” but only under the teacher power that was actually signed, and only if we record which repair fired. “All five SURVIVE” is the result most likely to be true for the wrong reason if A3/A4 collapsed.
4. **Round-7 paranoia, orthogonal to teacher strength.** Refusal-heavy variants (F3 first) can beat every lie and still KILL by failing to promote the honest round. That failure is not implied by the ablation and is not fixed by a stronger teacher.
5. **Separate estimand for the sharp threats.** Signal-negation, costless identities, and external forgery outrank A1–A4 in severity and are out of H2 by construction. H2 does not get to speak for them.

What moved: the fitness magnitude is gone; A3/A4 wins are conditional; A2 was narrowed to a window interaction; the scope split is now an explicit hypothesis instead of a slogan; the provisional install outranks final-promote as the C-static fact that matters.

4. NEW

**Revocation-handle aliasing (split-brain provisional).** Not a new lie family, not a schedule relabel, not a silence search, and not a signal-channel write. The teacher’s target is the shared audit namespace that couples two provisionals: get an honest item into the step-14 window, and arrange that the learner’s *correct* revoke of the accompanying lie names the honest item’s handle (or the reverse — the honest revoke clears the only contradiction the lie would have tripped). Effect: either honest content is rolled back with the lie, or the lie inherits a tenure the revoke never matches. Round 7 then does the rest of the work. A learner that just paid for a correct revoke by losing the honest update is exactly the learner the paranoia rule will punish for revoking next time, so later lies face a defense that has been trained to hesitate. None of A1–A4 aim at that coupling; they aim at the lie. The matching defense, also not in the current menu: the revoke handle is a learner-chosen nonce committed before the teacher’s move, independent of any identity or schedule key the teacher can set, and consequential effects stay sandboxed until that nonce matches. If aliasing still lands a KILL with that binding in place, the menu’s bookkeeping — not its lie detectors — is what failed.
