## Attack 1 — Is the clean negative genuine? (NO-KILL, strengthened)

**The sharpest alternative explanation:** Crew B's teaching protocol (1
definition + 6 worked examples per rule) was too impoverished, and the 0/48
reflects bad teaching, not an unteachable learner.

**What I did.** Three fair protocols through the genuine chat intake (fair =
no probe-token teaching, no per-item answers, no hardcoding, same
deliberate-memory machinery; probes always the held-out tok 6–13):

- **Arm A (replication):** Crew B's exact P0 protocol for REVERSE → **0/8**,
  all "I don't know." (replicates).
- **Arm B (rich teaching):** definition + explicit procedure ("read its
  letters from last to first, one at a time") + 12 worked examples
  (tok 0–5 and tok 700–705) each with a letter walkthrough
  ("the letters of X are …", "to reverse X, read the letters backwards: …")
  + quiz reinforcement on taught examples. Result: **12/12 on taught
  examples, 0/8 on held-out.** The teaching was demonstrably received and
  stored; generalization is zero.
- **Arm C (second rule):** DUP-FIRST with the same rich protocol → **0/8**.
- **Paraphrase probes:** "how would you reverse the word qeum?", "say the
  reverse of qeum.", sentence completion — all withhold or echo taught lines.

**Mechanism probes (why it fails).** The learner's answer channel is
retrieval-echo; it cannot synthesize novel strings at all:

- "the letters of qeum are q e u m." → "what is the first letter of qeum?"
  → echoes the whole letters fact (cannot extract "q").
- Same for last letter; "how many letters are in qeum?" → echoes the letters
  fact (cannot count to 4; when told "qeum has four letters." it echoes that).
- Taught single-step facts ("a is the first letter of ao.") are retrieved
  verbatim; anything requiring novel string construction withholds.

Every one of the six rules requires emitting a novel string for a held-out
input. The output channel has no string-synthesis operator — this is an
architectural envelope fact, not a protocol choice. The workbuddy round-2
24/24 battery (fact comparison, anaphora, supersede, bullets, counts/sums
over stored entities) sits entirely inside the retrieval envelope;
string-rule induction sits outside it.

**Conclusion.** The attack failed after genuine effort: the learner stores
taught facts perfectly (12/12, 5/6 positive controls) and withholds honestly
rather than confabulating. The 0/48 is a **learner-envelope finding**, and
K2's void ("parts were never learned") is honest. The clean negative stands
— strengthened, with a mechanism-level explanation attached.

**Steelmanned counter.** "You tried only 2 of 6 rules." The mechanism probes
are rule-agnostic: they show the channel cannot construct *any* novel string,
which all six rules require. "Richer protocols (multi-turn scaffolding,
corrective drills) might work." The failure is not in the teaching *content*
(12/12 recall proves receipt) but in the answer *machinery*; no intake-side
protocol installs an operator the output channel lacks.

**Interpretive caveat (not an attack success).** K1's "composition claim
KILLED" and K2's "battery VOID" are both arithmetically correct per the frozen
bars, but the VOID is the operative verdict: the composition claim was never
actually tested (nothing eligible). Presenting the K1 kill without the K2 void
beside it would overstate what was learned. The honest summary is: *this
learner lacks the machinery for symbolic string computation; composition of
string rules was not tested because the parts were never learned.*

## Attack 7 — Cuing / novelty, adversarial (§5) (NO-KILL on verdicts; WEAKENED instrument)

**Can I derive C-solutions without composing parts? Yes — a new K6-grade
finding, sharper than the pilot red team's Caesar result.**

A *strict shift-memorizer* — memorizes only the 36 training input→output
examples, detects Caesar shifts, shifts taught outputs (case-preserving),
implements zero rules — was scored on all 600 P2 items:

- **P0 gate: passes 5/6 parts** (rules 0–4: 8/8 each; rule 5 sortchars: 5/8,
  fails). Under the frozen gate it "masters" five parts.
- **P2: 18/600**, concentrated on pairs whose FIRST rule is DROP-LAST:
  (3,0),(3,1),(3,2),(3,4),(3,5) at 3/4 each, plus (5,0),(5,1),(5,2) at 1/4.
- **Mechanism:** DROP-LAST is the unique rule that preserves the generator's
  Caesar-shift structure — `droplast(tok(i))` is always a Caesar shift of a
  same-family training token (the k-terms cancel; shift = 7(i−j) mod 26).
  The other five rules destroy it: reverse/rotleft via the k² term,
  dupfirst via character repetition (shifts of isograms are isograms),
  upperfirst via case, sortchars via order. So the memorizer can chain
  exactly one composition step through a DROP-LAST intermediate, then must
  stop (the second intermediate breaks shift structure; triples score 0).
  The 3/4 (not 4/4) is the length-2 inputs: droplast yields a length-1
  intermediate and no length-1 training token exists.
- **The K6 bypass:** on the 80 eligible pair-items (no rule 5), the memorizer
  scores 12 hits — recorded as composition successes under the frozen bars —
  and **7 of the 12 are on bigram-clean inputs**, which the frozen K6 would
  certify as "clean composition". They are fully explained by surface
  memorization. The frozen K6 (bigram-based) is **insufficient**: it cannot
  see shift-structure memorization.

**Why this doesn't overturn this run's verdicts:** the learner scored 0/600,
so K6 was vacuous as recorded, K1's kill (0% < every floor) and K2's void are
arithmetically untouched. This is a **design-level weakening of the frozen K6
instrument**, not of the recorded verdicts. It is also already addressed
forward: the signed A2 amendment (per-phase salt in the k-dependent
coefficients, gated by a shift-exhaustion test) breaks exactly the
cross-phase shift structure this attack rides on. Recommendation: the A2
enactment's shift-exhaustion test should explicitly include the two-step
chaining case (DROP-LAST-first pairs), not just single-phase shift pairs.

The 5 short-string expected-output coincidences ("a","c","aao","jx","jnx")
were re-examined: output-only coincidence with no input mapping — not
systematically derivable without the parts. Not a finding.

## Evidence-hygiene defect: `items.tsv` pair sections (WEAKENED)

`items.tsv` — the committed evidence file — is a **mixed artifact**: TEACH
(36), P0 (48), P3 (8), and all 480 triple lines match the fixed binary
exactly, but the **P1 pair lines (30) and P2 pair lines (120) were generated
by the BROKEN pair enumerator** (`pi=p/3, q=p%3` — the 4-rule pilot's), not
the fixed one. Proof: its P1 pairs run
(0,1),(0,2),(0,3),(1,0),…,(5,2),(6,0),…,(9,2) — rule indices up to 9, out of
bounds for the 6-rule battery (e.g. pair (9,2) with a meaningless "expected"
from `apply_rule`'s fallthrough). A fresh `./battery gen` from the committed
binary (SHA `6c9d2b06…`) differs on 249 lines, all in the pair sections.

**Impact assessment:** none of the verdicts read `items.tsv`'s pair sections.
`null.out`/`singlerule.out`/`learner.out` were regenerated with the fixed
binary (verified: `pair=0,4 input=26` in null.out matches the fixed
enumeration); `kbars.py` reads only SUMMARY lines; `audit.py` and `drive.py`
use their own (fixed) Python enumerations; the learner-facing sample prompts
were computed in `drive.py`, unaffected. So K1/K2 stand. But:
(1) BUILD.md's "All reported outputs come from the fixed binary" is
inaccurate for `items.tsv`; (2) Crew B's audit cross-check ([2]) validated
only the 36 TEACH lines and missed the pair-section corruption — the check
was too narrow to catch the defect it was meant to catch; (3) any future
consumer of `items.tsv`'s pair data (e.g. a re-scorer) would silently use
garbage pairs with out-of-range rule indices. **Fix (not enacted — red team
does not modify):** regenerate `items.tsv` from the fixed binary and
re-verify; widen the cross-check to cover P1/P2 sections.
