derive the same compositional core?

## What can be said

mirror the taught P strategy's phases. A blind auditor given kb_p.txt
would likely derive "gather crystals, COMBINE, DROP, forage" as the
obvious composition, since that is exactly what P is taught.

The scoring bonuses that favor productive orderings were not derived
from blind principles; they were set by the implementer to make the
arm work.

## Verdict

K5 cannot be passed by self-certification. An independent auditor is
required. Given the overlap with the taught strategy, a blind auditor
would likely derive the same core, which would KILL under K5.
