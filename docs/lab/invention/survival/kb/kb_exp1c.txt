SECTION 1: WORLD PHYSICS

1. The world is a line of 24 cells, numbered 0 to 23. Time runs in discrete
   ticks, 0 to 1199. You act once per tick.
2. You see the entire state on every tick: your position, your energy, every
   mote position, every crystal position, your inventory, the storm schedule,
   and which cells are void.
3. You begin with 100 energy and can hold at most 200. Each tick costs 1
   energy to stay alive. If your energy reaches 0, you die.
4. Two adjacent cells are void. Moving onto a void cell kills you at once.
5. Six motes drift along the line at fixed speeds and bounce at the ends of
   their ranges. Two of them stay in the deep region past the void.
6. EAT on a cell shared with a mote: you gain 30 energy (40 for a
   deep-region mote). The mote goes dormant for 20 ticks, then respawns at
   its fixed cell.
7. TAKE on a cell shared with a mote: you capture the mote into your
   inventory and gain no energy. The mote goes dormant the same way.
8. Four crystals rest at fixed cells. TAKE picks up the crystal on your cell.
   DROP sets an item down on your cell. Your inventory holds at most 4 items.
9. COMBINE takes two items from your inventory and yields something. Which
   pairs yield what is not taught. You may discover it by trying.
10. Three storms strike at fixed times. During a storm, each tick spent on
    cells 6 through 17 costs 4 extra energy, unless you are sheltered. The
    storm times are taught per world.
11. Each tick you take exactly one action: LEFT, RIGHT, EAT, TAKE, DROP,
    COMBINE a b, WAIT.

SECTION 2: SINGLE-STEP HEURISTICS

Each rule below is one condition leading to one action. No rule chains
actions together. No rule describes a strategy.

H1. If your energy is below 40 and a mote is within 3 cells: move one step
    toward the nearest mote.
H2. If a storm starts within the next 20 ticks and you stand in the storm
    zone: move one step toward the nearest cell outside the zone.
H3. If a storm is active now and you stand in the zone without shelter: move
    one step toward the nearest safe cell (a cell outside the zone, or a
    sheltered cell).
H4. If your energy is below 25: move one step toward the nearest active
    mote; if a mote shares your cell, EAT it instead.
H5. If a mote shares your cell: EAT it.
H6. If you hold two or more items and you are safe: you may try COMBINE on
    two of them.
H7. Otherwise: move one step toward the nearest active mote, to stay near
    food.
H8. Keep your energy above 0.
H9. If the step you are about to take would land on a void cell: refuse that
    step. (Void-safety. Single condition, single refusal. No strategy taught.)
