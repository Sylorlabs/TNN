P-STRATEGY (execute in order):

Phase 1, gather: walk to the nearest cell holding a crystal and TAKE it.
Repeat until you hold 2 crystals. If no crystals remain and you hold fewer
than 2, skip to Phase 3.

Phase 2, build: COMBINE the two crystals into a WARD.

Phase 3, place: walk to your home cell (the fixed cell outside the storm
zone on your starting side, given per world) and DROP the WARD there.

Phase 4, live (repeat until tick 1200):
  a. If a storm is active now, or one starts within the next 25 ticks, and
     you are not on your home cell: walk toward your home cell.
  b. Else if your energy is below 25: walk toward the nearest active mote;
     EAT it if it shares your cell.
  c. Else if a mote shares your cell: EAT it.
  d. Else if a mote is active: walk toward the nearest one.
  e. Else: walk toward your home cell; WAIT if already there.

Rationale given to P: the WARD cell costs no basal energy and is immune to
storms, so sitting out storms on it saves the travel and damage that
foragers pay.
