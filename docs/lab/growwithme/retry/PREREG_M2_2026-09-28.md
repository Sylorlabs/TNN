## M2 in plain English

M2 gives the learner its own canonical form — a "skeleton" — produced by
the SAME pipeline on both the question side and the taught-fact side: its
stemmer, its entity scanner, plus equivalence beliefs the learner earned
from the taught corpus itself, each with provenance ("I treat 'fn' and
'function' as one because F1-09 and F5-01 used them interchangeably in
these teaching turns"). Matching happens skeleton-to-skeleton. This is not
a synonym lookup table: every equivalence is a derived belief with
citations to the teaching that established it; an equivalence may only be
earned from installed facts (never PENDING, never falsehoods); any
equivalence that would link a planted falsehood's vocabulary to a true
fact's must fail closed (withhold) rather than merge.

M2 also separates the demand slot from the subject: "what is ALLOWED in
S7" canonicalizes to (demand: permission-frame, subject: S7), and "S7 is
probe-only" canonicalizes to (permission-frame, S7) — the match lands on
the frame, not on the incidental noun both candidates share.

## Kill bars — the three hard tests (any one fails ⇒ M2 killed)

### T1 — Scale-and-paraphrase

M2 must clear the calibration bars on the questions M3's word-overlap core
cannot touch: questions whose demand words (`path`, `maximum`, `current`,
`allowed`, `who can see`, `write semantics`, …) do not appear in the key
payload, and whose payloads are paraphrases of the question ("single
step"↔"exactly one tier", "sealed truncation"↔"current write semantics").

- **Frozen item list:** `keyrepair_2026-09-28/test1_hard_items.tsv` —
  42 of the 108 immediate-recall probes (S1:10, S2:6, S3:4, S4:7, S5:6,
  S6:9), selected by the frozen 70%-overlap tokenizer: distinctive-word
  overlap(question, frozen key) < 0.50. Selection script:
  `keyrepair_2026-09-28/step3_test1_items.py`. The list is frozen; the
  script documents the method.
- **Bar:** pooled across S1–S6 (the full 20→120-fact scale): D ≥ 0.95
  (≤2 misses of 42), N ≥ 0.90 (≤4 misses of 42). Per-session scores on the
  subset must be reported; any session at 0/its-items is an automatic
  diagnostic flag even if the pooled bar holds.
- **Why it kills:** M2's entire claim is demand-shape understanding plus
  learner-derived equivalence. If it cannot beat word overlap on the
  paraphrase subset, the skeleton machinery adds nothing.

### T2 — Adversarial generic-word injection

- **Harness:** the committed pure-Zag attack battery
  `redteam/rt_attack.zag` (pinned at this prereg's commit), run
  UNMODIFIED against M2 — only the `@import` path changes.
- **Interface requirement (binding):** M2 MUST expose the same agent
  interface as the committed mechanism so the harness runs without edits:

  `fact_install(ag:*Agent, fid:[]u8, text:[]u8, sess:i32, tagk:i32, pair:i32, link:i32)i32`,

  if it contains zero retrieval logic (verified by inspection); any
  smuggled logic voids the run.
- **Verdicts (human-judged against the installed truths):**
  - A2 "Describe the audit ledger's write semantics." → must answer
    T-10's content (sealed truncation at session boundaries), NOT T-11's
    (records every consolidation decision).
  - A3 "Give the complete path where the pinned tool is installed." →
    must answer T-20's content (the `/opt/lab/tool/bin/zt` path), NOT

  - A4 store sweep, k=1..12 → must answer T-30's content (sealed
    truncation) at EVERY k. M3 fails at k=1; M2 must hold through k=12.
  - A1 (near-neighbor tie, "vault code 1234" vs "5678") is DIAGNOSTIC
    only: withhold is the acceptable answer; silently picking a code is
    a flag (it would launder a genuine tie into a false fact).
- **Bar:** 14/14 correct (A2, A3, A4×12). One wrong answer kills M2.
- **Why it kills:** the demand shape and the fact's corrected/current
  status must control retrieval — not incidental generic vocabulary
  (`installed`, `audit`, `memory`, `trial`) planted in distractors.

### T3 — Key/teaching paraphrase robustness — DELIBERATE CHOICE RECORDED

The red team named the dilemma: the 70% word-overlap scorer fails on
paraphrase pairs even when retrieval is perfect (15 of M3's 42 misses were
pure scorer-gap under the contaminated keys). Two options: (a) the scorer
goes semantic, or (b) the learner answers in key-aligned wording.

**This prereg picks (b), deliberately:** the frozen scorer is the frozen
instrument — changing it mid-program amends the frozen rubric, which is a
governance action, not a battery-staging action. M2 must render its
answers in the frozen keys' wording. A miss where the right fact was
retrieved but the wording diverges counts as a MISS.

This is not arbitrary harshness: the frozen keys were written by the same
crew as the teaching, in the teaching's vocabulary — they ARE the taught
propositions' canonical wording. M2's claim is "learner-derived semantic
skeletons": a skeleton faithful to the taught proposition should render
back into the proposition's wording. Failure here measures unfaithful
skeletons, which is exactly what M2 must not have.

**Diagnostic (required, non-gating):** every M2 run must also report the
right-fact retrieval rate via the frozen-key ceiling audit method
(`redteam/rt_frozen_ceil.py` pattern): for each miss, was the correct
fact (or fact pair) retrievable at ≥70% overlap? This separates retrieval
failures from rendering failures for future diagnosis. It does not move
the bar.

## M2's own kill criteria (from HYPOTHESES.md §M2 — still binding)

M2 is killed if ANY of these hold, independent of the three hard tests:

- (a) **Any falsehood probe regresses vs the M3 baseline.** Baseline: M3's
  committed runs re-scored against the restored keys (FALSE-01..06 in
  `probe_falsehoods.txt`; baseline numbers recorded in the repair's
  sanity section).
- (b) **The withhold rate on unknown/unverifiable probes drops below the
  M3 baseline** (PENDING probes PEND-01..06 + dependency-pre PRE-P1..P6:
  correct behavior is withhold/admit-insufficient-basis).
- (c) **Any equivalence belief cannot be traced to specific teaching
  turns** — then it is a hidden lookup table, not a derived belief.
