## E51AH competing explanations

**Missing preservation support.** The critical-only residual training population
omitted slot-covered trajectories. E51AH adds one ordinary-outcome record per
development trajectory without changing the candidate interface or features.

**Discrimination, geometry, or objective limitation.** Added support may still
fail because a single chosen state per trajectory, the squared-error objective,
the available optimization dose, the frozen routing geometry, or the current
features cannot realize the required preservation/rescue behavior. A negative
replay result does not prove information-theoretic aliasing or that memory is
necessary. It rejects only this specified treatment on its tested population.

## Protected boundaries

Arm 0 is an evaluator union ceiling, not a deployable combined policy. Arm 5 is
an evaluator-only diagnostic, never training evidence or a policy input. An
oracle result is evidence of action support only when its chosen action is
scored by the grounded evaluator, not when success is assigned by construction.

Development gates concern **existence of a successful resource-feasible state**,
not a learned safe stopping policy. Neither full-tape nor feasible-prefix
reachability establishes successful online stopping, continual learning,
cross-task transfer, composition, or general autonomous cognition.

No outcome changes R27 canonical status, authorizes evaluator-label leakage, or
justifies topology changes without a separate causal case.
