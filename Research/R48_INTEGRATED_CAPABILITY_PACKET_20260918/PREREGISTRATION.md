# R48 integrated capability packet — preregistration

Date: 2026-09-18  
Status: **ARCHITECTURE/TRAINING/EVALUATION BOUNDARY FROZEN BEFORE RUN**

## Purpose

R48 is the first integrated work packet defined by `TNN_CAPABILITY_MASTER_PLAN_20260918.md`. It is a bounded research-platform qualification, not a claim of general intelligence.

The packet implements and evaluates:

1. immutable architecture identity and separate learned-state identity;
2. training/evaluation manifests plus post-freeze fresh-seed custody;
3. a multi-hypothesis symbolic reasoning store with evidence links, support/contradiction updates, counterexample search, discriminating-test selection, and abstraction output;
4. causal hypothesis competition and intervention selection;
5. learner-controlled exact-memory retention under pressure, compared with fixed controls and including a held-out video/timestamp-like exact-recall panel;
6. compact learner-owned persistent connection fabric at 50k, 250k, and 1M edges, with 5M attempted only when the 1M resource result is sane;
7. a four-family local training-source manifest with provenance, license field, hashes, dedup fingerprints, source caps, and held-out designation;
8. controlled-language logic with provenance-bearing proof traces;
9. tiny code/program synthesis with hidden executable verification;
10. three invention families: symbolic law discovery, sorting-network algorithm discovery, and causal experiment design;
11. adversarial controls: update-disabled, reward/target scramble, identifier permutation, structural permutation, information denial, clean-state rerun, source/answer scan, and post-freeze fresh worlds.

## Scientific boundary

- Generic arithmetic, search, hashing, sparse arrays, verification, provenance, serialization, and task generators are architecture/evaluator machinery.
- Learned-state updates are kept separately from architecture source.
- Fresh evaluation seeds are generated and recorded only after the architecture hash and trained-state hash are frozen.
- Evaluator truth and hidden targets are never inserted into learner features.
- A result only receives the evidence class justified by its controls. Infrastructure success does not count as intelligence.
- `learn_authority = 0`; canonical R27 is unchanged.

## Frozen success criteria

- architecture/training/evaluation identity record is internally consistent;
- fresh symbolic-rule recovery >= 80% across generated held-out worlds and materially above update-disabled control;
- causal experiment selector resolves >= 80% of held-out ambiguous mechanisms with fewer interventions than random median control;
- learned exact-memory policy beats FIFO and random on aggregate held-out exact recall and does not lose the video exact-recall panel to FIFO;
- connection fabric successfully exercises 50k/250k/1M with complete declared compact provenance fields and bounded memory; 5M is conditional on <= 128 MiB logical storage at 1M and successful update/retrieval probes;
- controlled-language logic reaches 100% on its exact verifier; this is architecture/inference evidence unless learning contributes;
- code synthesis solves >= 80% of fresh generated tasks with hidden tests;
- sorting-network discovery returns a fully verified 4-input network without a solution string embedded in source;
- symbolic-law discovery returns a fresh rule absent from direct training references and passes hidden points;
- adversarial closeout records failures as failures; no threshold is relaxed after seeing results.
