# R31 Failures and Boundaries

- Quantitative R31 experiments are REFERENCE_ONLY; native Zag execution was blocked by unavailable `znc` in this runtime.
- R27 remains canonical.
- Exact 0%/100% values are diagnostics, never accepted without harder challenge.
- Chunk-only sensory representation is rejected: it compresses strongly but loses hidden grounding capability.
- Predictive-surprise/giant-span objectives are rejected as primary chunk criteria when compression gains outpace grounding.
- Always-reinspect is rejected; context-sensitive reinspection is better and cheaper.
- Naive regime banks are rejected due extreme model switching/thrashing.
- Global probe-budget learning is rejected: too conservative and weak on true ambiguity.
- Generic state-dependent RF stopping is rejected: hard correctness ~0.676 and ambiguity abstention ~0.544.
- Best sequential evidence policy still abstains on only ~0.572 of genuinely no-unique-answer cases. Epistemic ambiguity remains open.
- Learner-driven chunk split/merge exists in the Zag source but lacks native quantitative validation.
- Natural continuous human speech/video self-chunking remains unqualified; current tests are controlled synthetic/reference worlds.
