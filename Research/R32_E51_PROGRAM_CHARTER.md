# R32 E51 Program Charter

Date established: 2026-08-31

Status: active research program; R27 remains canonical

## Frontier amendment — 2026-09-01

E51AG remains the latest authoritative result. Its stable negative initially
pointed toward context or representation, but a lower-layer support defect was
then identified: the residual training set omitted slot-covered preservation
experiences. Under the charter's escalation ladder and the master program's
credit-assignment track, E51AH must test that ordinary-outcome preservation
credit before memory, representation, routing, or topology is expanded.

E51AH is frozen at stages 108/109/110 and has not exposed validation or
confirmation. Its result will decide whether the next branch stays in
curriculum/credit/replay or advances to short-context memory and representation.

## Purpose

E51 exists to determine whether R32 can move from brittle task-specific success
toward a more general learner that can select, combine, suppress, revise, and
extend learned behavior without evaluator leakage or hand-coded task structure.

Central question:

> Can a Zag-native learner acquire progressively more general decision-making
> mechanisms from experience while preserving uncertainty, avoiding destructive
> interference, and remaining evaluator-blind?

E51 is a research program, not a sequence of score patches. A result enters the
scientific baseline only after its lineage, controls, integrity evidence, and
replication status are explicit.

## Immutable scientific baseline

The baseline package must pin:

- canonical R27 reference and current R32 experimental state;
- mature slot learner, direct candidate learner, and strongest frozen controls;
- every E51 preregistration, implementation contract, hardcoding ledger, result,
  and authoritative evidence identity;
- exact compiler, native source, binary, raw-ledger, and workflow identities;
- world-generation, evaluator-isolation, and hardcoding contracts;
- promotion gates, experimental lineage, negative closures, and open branches.

Every new experiment records its parent commit and scientific state, exact
hypothesis and intervention, causal controls, development and sealed data,
confirmation rule, hardcoding ledger, source/compiler/binary hashes, and frozen
outcome. One favorable score is never sufficient for baseline promotion.

## Capability tracks

The program tracks distinct capability dimensions rather than repeatedly
modifying one residual mechanism:

1. representation;
2. uncertainty and abstention;
3. memory;
4. credit assignment;
5. context discrimination;
6. candidate generation;
7. candidate arbitration;
8. continual learning;
9. self-created mechanisms;
10. planning and temporal reasoning;
11. transfer and generalization;
12. self-evaluation;
13. resource adaptation;
14. structural plasticity;
15. integrated autonomous cognition.

Each track requires falsifiable milestones, causal ablations, fresh-world tests,
and explicit closure criteria. Language and continuous media are later grounding
environments for the general substrate, not architecture-specific shortcuts.

## Experiment discipline

The required loop is:

`observation -> competing hypotheses -> cheapest discriminating experiment -> result -> update`

Each substantial failure must generate at least two plausible explanations.
Negative results are first-class evidence and must state the hypothesis rejected,
scope of rejection, surviving alternatives, and what should not be repeated
without new evidence.

Safe parallel work includes independent diagnostics, preregistration review,
infrastructure validation, and disjoint implementation branches. Multiple agents
must not tune the same treatment after viewing sealed outcomes.

## Escalation ladder

Architectural escalation follows this order unless evidence closes an earlier
layer:

1. training quantity;
2. curriculum;
3. contrastive objectives;
4. memory, context, delay, and noise;
5. representation;
6. uncertainty;
7. routing and arbitration;
8. prediction;
9. learner-created non-core mechanisms;
10. structural plasticity;
11. protected-core redesign.

No topology or protected-core change is justified merely because it is novel.

## Replication and promotion

Replication tiers are fresh seed, multiple fresh worlds, distribution shift,
related unseen task, composed task, and longitudinal continual learning.

Capability status advances only through:

`EXPERIMENTAL -> REPRODUCED -> ROBUST -> TRANSFERRED -> INTEGRATED -> PROMOTION_CANDIDATE`

A promotion candidate must beat appropriate controls, replicate, survive fresh
distributions, preserve prior capabilities, remain evaluator-blind, execute in
native Zag v2, build deterministically, and add capability beyond benchmark
optimization. R27 remains canonical until that complete package exists.

## Permanent integrity rules

- UNKNOWN truth, ambiguity membership, validation membership, task IDs, mode IDs,
  resource IDs, world IDs, and benchmark answers are never learner-visible.
- UNKNOWN remains a learner-owned consequence of insufficient positive warrant,
  not a supplied label or fixed positive bias.
- Python may support assembly, analysis, and infrastructure; promotable cognition
  is native Zag v2.
- Hardcoding ledgers classify generic substrate, preregistered constants,
  verifier-only state, evaluator-only state, learner-visible state, and prohibited
  learner information. Leakage fails closed.
- Deterministic double builds, source manifests, raw output, integrity gates,
  confirmation status, and artifact hashes are mandatory for authoritative native
  claims.

## Current frontier at charter creation

September 4 qualification: historical E51AE/E51AG oracle counts were assigned
success by construction. They do not independently establish expressivity.
The learned-arm negative and omitted-preservation-support observation remain
the motivation for E51AH; its own oracle will score the selected action.

E51X established exact terminal action reachability on untouched validation and
sealed confirmation using a trajectory-critical objective at sufficient dose.
E51Y then showed that five-way continuation value remained miscalibrated. E51AA
identified a resource-feasible action-support limit; E51AB-E51AC established that
direct candidate actions are useful but incomplete; E51AD rejected the tested
low-capacity router; E51AE rejected the tested additive candidate residual; and
E51AG reproduced that residual failure on three fresh partitions.

E51AG's stable signature is additional known-case reachability coupled to a much
larger loss of no-unique preservation. The candidate-action oracle is exact, so
the next causal branch concerns evaluator-blind discrimination and preservation,
not candidate expressivity. The first new experiment must distinguish whether
the failure is caused primarily by insufficient instantaneous representation,
missing short context/memory, or an unsuitable low-capacity decision geometry
before structural escalation.

## Long-range milestones

- M1 reliable arbitration;
- M2 endogenous uncertainty;
- M3 contextual memory;
- M4 continual acquisition;
- M5 structural transfer;
- M6 composition;
- M7 learner-created mechanisms;

with an isolated benchmark score increase.
