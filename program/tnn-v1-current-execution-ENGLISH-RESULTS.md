## Training variations

The construction-induction tournament compares exact fixed templates, character n-gram prediction controls, Adaptive Motifs, and provenance-aware Adaptive Motifs under small-clean, large-noisy, clean/noisy order changes, and duplicated misinformation.

**Adaptive Motif** means a reversible learned segment or construction over raw input. It is a research replacement for a fixed transformer token, not a renamed token vocabulary.

The tournament is controlled construction learning. Passing it is not teenager English.
