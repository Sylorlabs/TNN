## What is and is not established

E45–E48 establish a failure of these frozen terminal-controller combinations.
They do not reject the R32 architecture, grounded UNKNOWN, endogenous richer
causal representation, or a small nonlinear grounded commit-value mechanism.
The next experiment must preserve the evaluator, target semantics, fresh seed
separation, full-cell safety gate, and known-performance gate while changing one
such mechanism only.
