Before qualification, the fixed claims are:

- The learner receives only R33 observation packets plus delayed scalar outcomes. The hidden world regime remains evaluator-owned.
- The learner chooses between two physically distinguishable objects, navigates through observation-derived actor position, and confirms local object identity via the inspect-rendered physical feature before touch.
- Touch consequences arrive three world ticks later and must match the learner's pending causal action ID before credit is accepted.
- An isolated untrained regime-A baseline is exactly 8/16 positive outcomes under the neutral alternating tie rule.
- After 24 online regime-A experiences, zero-update evaluation is 16/16 positive.
- After a hidden switch and 24 online regime-B experiences, zero-update evaluation is 16/16 positive and a second latent context has been allocated from outcome surprise rather than a regime label.
- On return to regime A, stored A behavior is reused after one surprise probe: 15/16 positive with no score/count updates during the return evaluation.
- Exactly 48 score/count updates occur across A+B training.
- An update-disabled regime-B control remains at zero updates and 12/24 positive under the neutral alternating tie rule.
- A reward-scrambled training control fails regime-A acquisition, with 0/16 actual positive outcomes at zero-update evaluation.
- Equal seeds and equal experience streams produce exact learner, world, and ingress state.
- Learner state has an internal SHA-256 integrity digest. The outer 12-section R33 checkpoint atomically carries learner state, world state, and ingress state.
- A fresh-process checkpoint taken while delayed credit is pending must continue identically to uninterrupted execution.
- A checkpoint whose outer digest is recomputed after corrupting the learner payload must still be refused by the inner learner digest.
- A torn outer checkpoint must be refused.
- Resource use is observed natively. The campaign records CPU and RSS telemetry.

The geometry and action set are bounded properties of this R33 test world; no claim of general perception, general intelligence, or superiority to LLMs follows from this campaign.
