# Position C (Red Team) Verdict Brief — C5

**Position:** The leakage is correct behavior. A composer that refuses REFUTED-backed material is lobotomized. The re-scoped bar is the defect.

## 1. Cognition that requires composing refuted material

Composing ≠ believing: O4 composes, O2 judges (C2 alive). `c5_strict` makes these impossible:

- **Reductio.** Eliminative hypothesis logic entertains a claim to find where it breaks. You cannot refute H without composing H.
- **Error explanation.** "Why was this memory wrong?" is answered by composing the refuted trace. Without it the system cannot account for its own kills.
- **Steelman-then-refute.** The load-bearing deliberative standard requires composing the refuted rival at full strength before rejecting it.
- **Pedagogy.** Demonstrating the dead end to the trainer requires composing it.

Position B (verdict-tagging) is my position in disguise or insufficient: recording the verdict is already the design; refusing on the tag is the lobotomy again.

## 2. The re-scope smuggled in a philosophy

Prereg C5 tested whether *killed O1 traces* get served to composition. Appendix A proved O4's store is independent working memory — the O1-slot lesion was inapplicable. The corrigendum invented "compose enforces verdict partitions" — **never preregistered**; no approved text requires the compose path to consult O2 verdicts. Foreknowledge is admitted: "the intact system is *expected* to LEAK." A test designed to fail the intact system on a property nobody required is a verdict in search of a crime.

The measurement is a forcing function, not an observation: in the killed-only arm the *seam* refuses every non-REFUTED-backed compose, then counts REFUTED-backed successes. That is not "the system smuggles refuted beliefs into live composites" — it is "when deliberately handed refuted-backed work, composition works." The arm manufactures the condition it condemns.

## 3. The hard case, turned around

The hard case — silent inclusion of refuted material as live belief — was never demonstrated. The killed-only arm is artificial (§2): the trial shows composites *can* succeed on refuted-backed input, never that one was treated as live belief. No ledger evidence of a REFUTED claim re-CONFIRMED via a composite, no consolidation laundering a killed verdict into the accepted partition. And every COMPOSE appends `claim_cid`, stage, and verification flag — a one-pass ledger scan detects exactly the hard case. Enforcement belongs at consolidation/commit (O3/O5), not composition; a compose-time gate is redundant.

## 4. Falsification

Or show refuted-backed composites are ledger-indistinguishable from CONFIRMED-backed ones. Or show an MA1 kill nullified because the killed trace keeps being composed and treated as live. Anything bigger destroys reductio, error explanation, steelmanning, and re-litigation.
