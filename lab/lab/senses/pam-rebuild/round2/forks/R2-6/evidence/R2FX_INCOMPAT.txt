R2A fixture-format incompatibility (empirical)
==============================================
sense_r26 rejects every R2A .r2fx F-span with "error=bad-size".

Reproduction (2026-09-23):
  $ python3 -c "extract F span of r2a_colordisc_0.r2fx -> fx_cd.bin (6144 B)"
  $ sense_r26 colordisc fx_cd.bin
  approach=R26
  task=colordisc
  error=bad-size

Per-task comparison (what sense_r26 requires vs what R2A r2fx F-spans are):
  colordisc : needs 8-byte [128,64] header + 128*64*3 RGB = 24584 B
              r2fx F = 6144 B raw (two 32x32x3 patches, no dims, no header)
  colorconst: needs 8-byte [128,64] header + 24576 B RGB
              r2fx F = 13824 B raw (48x96x3, no dims, no header)
  shapetrans: needs 8-byte [96,96] header + 96*96*3 RGB = 27656 B
              r2fx F = 9216 B raw (96x96x1 grayscale, no dims, no header)
  pitchdisc : needs 8-byte [sr:u32][cnt:u32] + i16 samples; internal layout
              tone=cnt*10/22 with ~9% gap assumed
              r2fx F = 64000 B raw i16 (32000 samples @16kHz:
              toneA[0,14400) gap[14400,16000) toneB[16000,30400) pad) — no
              header; tone/gap boundaries do not match the binary's assumption
  timbredisc: needs 8-byte [sr][cnt] + i16; spans are cnt-halves
              r2fx F = 64000 B raw i16, no header (32000 samples vs the
              design's 12800-sample 0.8s assumption)
  motiondir : needs 12-byte [f=8,w=64,h=64] header + 8*64*64*3 = 98316 B
              r2fx F = 51200 B raw (8 frames of 80x80x1 grayscale, no header)

Conclusion: 4 of 6 tasks are structurally incompatible (different geometry /
color depth, no recoverable mapping without inventing pixels); the 2 audio
tasks would need a header plus a layout the binary does not assume. Writing a
translator = redesigning the measurement front-ends = a post-results build
change, barred by the frozen prereg ("No retroactive bar changes").
The R2A evaluation the prereg mandates cannot be executed on the frozen build.
