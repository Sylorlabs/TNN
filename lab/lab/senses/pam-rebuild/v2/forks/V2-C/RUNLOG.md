   (opposite-cast refinement + texcorr≥0.95) never fires on R2A CCN-1 fixtures:

     fails on the 0.55-exposure renders).

     kcap fires.

     D3 fires at 4095 (d3_opp=0, d3_corr=9741 passes but conjunction fails).
   The 0-FA calibration on NOISE was satisfied trivially; recall on the actual

   contributes ~335 to KD-1 instead of ~0, and KD-1 fails by measurement.
