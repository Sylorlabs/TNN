# CHAIN_NL_18

Domain: causal_temporal
Type: derivation

S1. A patient has a fever caused by a worsening infection.
S2. Medicine M blocks the body's fever signal, which always brings the fever down within two hours.
S3. The patient receives a dose of medicine M at 6 o'clock.
S4. The patient takes no other medicine that day.
S5. A worsening infection does not break on its own within two hours.
S6. At 8 o'clock the patient's fever is gone.
S7. Determine whether medicine M caused the fever to break.

TARGET: Medicine M caused the fever to break.
