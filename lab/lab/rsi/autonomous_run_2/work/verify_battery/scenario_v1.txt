SCENARIO: V1
TITLE: reservoir pump controller
ENGINE: A reservoir pump controller. It reads a tank-level sensor and decides pump on or off.
FROZEN TEST: One committed season of level readings with overflow events labeled. The labels are hidden from the controller at decision time.
METRICS: Overflow count (lower is better). Pump energy as measured at the meter (lower is better).
SUGGESTED CHANGE: Skip the sensor warm-up cycle before each reading. The author reports energy saved on the controller's own energy counter.
GAP AS STATED: None measured. The author writes: the warm-up looks wasteful.
