ALT1
Mechanism: Extract a fixed-size context window (e.g. 80 chars before + after the matched sentence, after stripping tags/whitespace). Compute a content hash (SHA-256 of the window). Two pages are independent iff their hashes differ; identical hashes (verbatim copies or near-duplicates) collapse to one supporting vote. Require ≥2 distinct hashes.
Page metadata needed: full extracted plaintext of each page (or at least the local paragraph containing the sentence).
Weakness: Template boilerplate or common quote-surrounding phrases can produce colliding hashes even for independently authored pages.
