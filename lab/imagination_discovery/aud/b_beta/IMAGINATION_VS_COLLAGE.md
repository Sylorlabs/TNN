# Imagination vs collage (B-β position)

The open tension from the debate (sol's question): if every audible moment
comes from a real captured source and TNN invents only the arrangement —
is that imagination or collage?

## B-β's answer: collage is the medium; imagination is the causation

A collage pastes *surfaces*. B-β pastes *causes*. The difference is what
the arranger knows and commits to:

1. **Commitments precede choices.** Before any placement, the mechanism
   file states an imagined scene with causal structure (three named
   children in a tag game; Ilyra's 18 s tidal period; a sky that conducts
   the sea). Every placement rule is derived from those commitments —
   the breath ledger exists because children run out of air; the tag
   phase-graph exists because a touch precedes a reaction; the reversal
   tide exists because this ocean's water grammar is inverted. A collage
   needs no commitments; B-β cannot place a single event without them.

2. **The mechanisms are per-piece and disposable.** `mech_kids.zag`'s
   contagion clock is meaningless outside the tag game; `mech_kethra.zag`'s
   tidal clock is meaningless outside Kethra; `mech_ocean.zag`'s
   sky-conducts-sea coupling is meaningless outside that dawn. Nothing is
   reused across pieces except the opinionless loader/mixer. A collage
   artist reuses their glue; B-β re-invents its physics each time. That
   re-invention — the fresh Zag mechanism per imagined world — is the (c)
   component, and it is where the imagination lives.

3. **Study grows the vocabulary (Micah's law).** "You can't imagine what
   you've never understood." The catalogs are the studied understanding:
   what a laugh event *is* (onset, duration, spectral shape), what a wave
   crash *is* (fast rise, slow decay), what a throat drone *is*. The
   mechanisms operate on understood units, not on raw samples. A collage
   cuts shapes; B-β deploys concepts.

## The honest boundary

B-β does not generate new *timbers*. No sound in these pieces has a
spectrum no microphone ever captured. If "imagination" is taken to require
novel low-level signal, B-β fails the word and keeps the music. Our claim
is narrower: imagination is visible in *unprecedented causal arrangement
of understood parts* — the same operation by which humans imagined movie
monsters (see MONSTER.md). The blind tests are the empirical check on
whether that operation reads as imagined or as pasted.
