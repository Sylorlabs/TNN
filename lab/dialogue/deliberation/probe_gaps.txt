## Flip 1 — G3 demand-focus, ALL semantics (not ANY)

Probe:
  U when was big ben dedicated?

The KB teaches that Big Ben is a landmark and is 96 meters tall, but never
teaches a dedication year for it. G3 (structural focus) demands that EVERY
demand-focus word be covered by the fact's keys: "dedicat" is not a key of
any Big Ben fact, so the gate fires.

  pre-repair:  A I don't know.
  repaired:    A I don't know that one. What I know about big ben: Big Ben is
               a landmark in London. Big Ben is 96 meters tall. Did you mean
               one of those?

The flip is plain withhold -> informative clarify. The gate fires (G3, ALL:
"dedicat" uncovered), then pred_mismatch sees a named entity (ne4>0) whose
predicate is untaught and clarifies with what IS known, instead of the bare
"I don't know." An ANY-semantics G3 would have let the partial match
("big ben") through and emitted a wrong fact.

## Flip 2 — F3 surname-only resolution with trailing punctuation

Probe:
  U how much older is darwin than curie?

"darwin"/"curie" are surname-only mentions; "curie?" carries a glued trailing
"?". The F3 arithmetic path resolves entities with cmp_scan (distinctive
single words, same resolver the which-comparison engine uses) over the
punctuation-stripped tail.

  instrumented: F3DBG ne_d=2 e0=2 e1=3
  pre-repair:  A I don't know.
  repaired:    A I don't know.

The entities resolve (Darwin=e2, Curie=e3); the old gaz_scan path failed
single names outright and "curie?" never matched the exact-length fallback.
The answer withholds honestly here because the downstream year lookup finds
no taught year-difference for this pair -- matching the adopted reference,
which withholds identically. The flip is at the mechanism level: resolution
succeeds (ne_d=2) instead of dying before the arithmetic is reached.

## Flip 3 — correction after withhold

Probe:
  U who wrote dune?
  U no, i meant the martian.

"dune" is unknown: the first turn withholds. The correction names a new
entity; the general correction fallback reuses the prior resolved query
shape ("who wrote X") with the new entity instead of answering a
different question about the entity.

  pre-repair:  A The Martian was published in 2011.
  repaired:    A Andy Weir wrote The Martian.

The flip is wrong-fact (publication year) -> correct author. The pre-repair
fork answered "what was published" about the Martian; the repaired correction
path re-resolves "who wrote" against the new entity and retrieves the
authorship fact. The adopted reference answers identically.
