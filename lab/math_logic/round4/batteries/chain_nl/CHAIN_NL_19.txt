# CHAIN_NL_19

Domain: causal_temporal
Type: derivation

S1. A bakery's process for each batch: the dough must rise for 60 minutes, then be shaped for 10 minutes, then bake for 30 minutes, then cool for 20 minutes before sale. Only one batch fits in the oven at a time.
S2. Batch 1 starts rising at 6:00.
S3. Batch 2 starts rising at 6:30.
S4. Prove that batch 2 can be sold at 8:30 and no earlier.

TARGET: Batch 2 can be sold at 8:30 and no earlier.
