# PARA_PAIR_04

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_06
Kind: paraphrase

S1. A surveyor is measuring a pair of right-triangular plots.
S2. Plot one has legs of 6 and 8 meters, and its hypotenuse is painted red.
S3. Plot two is right-angled; one of its legs is 24 meters and the other matches the red hypotenuse of plot one in length.
S4. Determine the hypotenuse of plot two.

TARGET: The second plot's hypotenuse is 26 meters.
