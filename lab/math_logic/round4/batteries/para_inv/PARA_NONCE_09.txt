# PARA_NONCE_09

Base problem: CHAIN_NL_15
Kind: nonce variant

S1. The zib was wug before it was thneed.
S2. It was thneed before it was drok.
S3. It was drok before it was glim.
S4. It was glim before it was plor.
S5. It was plor before it was blap.
S6. It was blap before it was qux.
S7. It was qux before it was zorp.
S8. If one event happened before a second and the second before a third, then the first happened before the third.
S9. The wibble was bought after the sorp but before the dorp.
S10. Prove that the zib was wug before it was zorp, that the wibble was bought after the zib was glim, and that the wibble was bought before the zib was qux.

TARGET: The zib was wug before it was zorp; the wibble was bought after the zib was glim and before the zib was qux.
