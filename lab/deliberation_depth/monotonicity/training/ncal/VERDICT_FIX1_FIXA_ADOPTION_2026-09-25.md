## FIX1 — applied

**Spec:** frozen `v3b/job1/ADDENDUM_V3B_JOB1_FIX1_PRERUN_2026-09-25.md`
(RT-F white-box diagnosis in `v3b/job1/VERDICT_JOB1.md`). `nec_cmp_id`
compared at most 63 id bytes and returned 0 whenever `id.len > 63`;
`nec_store` persisted only the first 63 bytes + NUL. Ids longer than 63
bytes never matched their own slot.

unchanged); `nec_cmp_id` compares the FULL id bytes against `ibuf`;
`nec_find` gains an `ibuf` parameter; `nec_store(id, idoff, …)` records
`(offset, id.len)` with no byte copy and no length cap.
