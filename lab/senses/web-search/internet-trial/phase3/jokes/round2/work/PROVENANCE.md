Date: 2026-09-23. Method: mechanical whole-word occurrence of every phrase in
`work/phrases_r4.json` against (a) the 301 training item bodies
(`work/training_items_r4.jsonl`, folded), (b) the 64 held-out texts
(`corpus/heldout.json`, folded); training support of every pattern in
`work/patterns_r2.json` against training `absurdity.pattern` annotations.

Provenance codes: **(T)** derived from training items · **(H)** derived from
the held-out audit or held-out text — test-set influence on the design ·
**(K)** seeded from the ROUND2.md §4 component-fact KB (crew-built schema;
glosses, code inventory, example phrases).

Derivation-chain notes (from the build scripts, not just occurrence):
- `extend_vocab_r3.py` (2026-09-23) claims "Every addition is training-side",
  but added `F_CONTAM: [yellow snow, ...]`, `F_IRRIT: [garlic, ...]`,
  `F_CAUGHT: [caught speeding, ...]` — phrases with zero training occurrence
  that are verbatim held-out D-A words. The claim is not mechanically
  supportable for these phrases.
- `PHRASE_ADDITIONS_R4.md` additions (`glitter`, `putting`, `retread`,
  `battery`) are all (T): each fires on its motivating training item
  (t4_001/t4_002/t4_003). Clean.
- The round-3 re-annotations (`reannotate_r3.py`) are (T): honest pattern
  annotations on training items. Note t2_062/t2_063/t2_164/t2_165 mirror
  held-out hda12/hda13/hda17/hda14 texts — the training corpus contains the
  same meme class; the annotations are text-honest, but the *selection* of
  which training items to re-annotate is plausibly held-out-informed. This
  is recorded, not flaggable mechanically.

Design-level (H) influence (cannot be mechanically excluded): ROUND2.md §4's
component-fact schema and §5's contradiction table were written AFTER the
held-out set froze, against the held-out set's own D-A1…D-A4 taxonomy
(toxic ingestion / body application / device destruction / absurd action).

`rattlesnake`, `helium`, `dish soap`, `marker` — verbatim held-out D-A words.
So even (K)-seeded phrases carry second-hand held-out influence at the design
level. Config C removes the *direct* (phrase/pattern-level) influence; the
design-level influence is documented here as irreducible without a redesign.

## 3. Config derivation

- **Config A** (all patterns, all phrases): includes all 48 (H) phrases and
  18 (H) patterns. Tests compositional generalization *from the vocabulary
  as built* — with the (H) influence documented above.
- **Config B** (support rule): patterns with ≥4 distinct training groups.
  Verified from item bodies + `distinct_group` fields: only **P_DA3E**
  (da3e_iphone_microwave, da3e_quickdry_phone, da3e_cellphone_ringtest,
  da3e_battery_microwave). P_DA1C has 3 groups; P_DA3B has 2; P_DA3F/P_DA4B/
  P_DA3D/P_DA3S have 1 each (7/4/4/4 items, single jokes). Full phrase
  vocabulary retained (the rule as applied in round 4 gates patterns).
- **Config C** (training-provenance-only): drops the 48 (H) phrases and the
  18 (H) patterns. Keeps 275 phrases (136 T + 139 K) and 14 (T) patterns.
  The design-level (H) influence (§1, last paragraph) remains by
  construction and is the residual caveat on any Config C PASS.

## 4. Bottom line

(H)-provenance items exist: **48 phrases + 18 patterns flagged**. The audit
trigger for Config C is met. The gate therefore runs all three configs.
