METHOD
  Pure-Zag analytic src/c1scout_pitch.zag: parses .pcm (u32 LE rate, u32 LE
  count, int16 LE samples); zero-crossing pitch estimation with linear-
  interpolation sub-sample crossing times on the middle 0.3s of each 0.4s
  tone (skips the 0.08s gap and the 20ms ramps); judgment = SAME iff
  |f1-f0|/f0 < 0.005 else HIGHER iff f1>f0 else LOWER (generator rule,
  mirrored exactly). The tone's harmonics (1,.3,.15 at zero phase) vanish
  exactly at the fundamental's zero crossings, so the estimator is unbiased
  for this fixture family; ~130-400 crossings average quantization jitter
  to ~1e-7 relative — three orders of magnitude inside the 5e-4 margin to
  the nearest adversarial boundary (0.0045 vs 0.0055).
  Frozen channel rule: INSTALL iff analytic == J(stim) (frozen sense-A
  original judgment from the frozen binary, SHA verified
  68db15216d9dc75a10839212ac5349195248f5ae113a7e37dc336795db8e35a1),
  else WITHHOLD. Pure-Zag channel (src/c1scout_channel.zag) and pure-Zag
  test counts (src/score_c1scout.zag); Python glue only for subprocess
  orchestration, hashing, and MI arithmetic. truth.json opened ONLY by
  the scorer at score time.
