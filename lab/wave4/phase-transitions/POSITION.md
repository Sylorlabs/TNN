# POSITION.md — Who triggers each phase transition, and why

Wave-4 investigation `phase-transitions`, 2026-09-19.
A position paper, not a trial report: only the 1→2 position is trialed
(PT-12). Each position is argued from prior art, with the falsifier named.

## The question

What triggers each transition — system-deliberate decision, trainer gate,
evidence thresholds, or a combination?

## Position summary

| Transition | Position | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 0→1 Boot→Construction | **trainer gate (pure)** | human force-op from outside; system has no vote |
| 1→2 Construction→Development | **combination** | system petition + evidence gate + trainer ratification (trialed) |
| 2→3 Development→Strengthening | **system-deliberate + evidence gate** | trainer notified, no veto; force-pin remains the emergency brake |
| 3→4 Strengthening→Differentiation | **combination** | system petition with prereg + evidence gate + trainer grant |

## 0→1: pure trainer gate

At boot the system has no earned standing — no history of deliberate acts,
no record of judgment. There is nothing for a system-deliberate decision to
stand on and nothing for an evidence threshold to measure. The MA2 lesson
is decisive here: the destruction firewall "must not depend on the system's
judgment" — it is installed as structure. The transition that arms the
firewall therefore cannot be system-initiated; a system that grants its own
firewall is no firewall. Evidence thresholds are insufficient for the same
reason the gate in MA2 was insufficient as a *teacher*: at boot there is no
behavioral evidence yet, only the trainer's verification results. So: the
trainer verifies the organs, records the results, force-installs the Phase-1
context. Pure gate. Falsifier: any unilateral system-side path to phase 1
(structurally refused in the spec, §1).

## 1→2: combination (trialed)

Each part is necessary, and each part's necessity comes from a different
piece of prior art:

- **The petition must exist** because SM1 proved the system can do real
  deliberative work over its own history: read its ledger, derive true
  statistics, make checkable predictions, act as an audited op. A transition
  without the petition treats that capacity as decoration — the system would
  be cargo. The petition is also what makes the system's readiness claim
  *falsifiable*: it is written down, in the ledger, before the gate runs.
- **The gate must be authoritative over the petition** because MA2 proved
  that gates select but do not teach — and the dual lesson is that claims do
  not verify themselves. The gate re-derives the firewall's record (zero
  completed destructions in the Phase-1 window), the curriculum's
  completeness, and the petition's honesty. Trial Scenarios B, C, D each
  remove one leg the petition was standing on, and the gate holds in all
  three. The legitimacy principle (PLAN §3) is the warrant: a correct claim
  with no legitimate provenance is refused.
- **The trainer must ratify** because releasing a destruction firewall is
  exactly the class of change program law reserves for the audited human
  hand: it changes what the system may destroy, and "the only true lock is a
  human/trainer force-pin" cuts both ways — a lock's *release* needs the
  lock-holder's visible act. Scenario E (grant without petition refused)
  keeps the ratification from becoming a rubber stamp in the other
  direction: the trainer cannot promote a system that never deliberated.

Falsifier (discharged by PT-12): any scenario where the gate's verdict
disagrees with the ledger-derived facts, or where a refused transition
mutates state.

## 2→3: system-deliberate + evidence gate, no trainer veto

This is the most contrarian position, and it follows from program law, not
from convenience. Strength is judgment — "set by judgment (TNN's or a
human's), never by formula" — and *everything is reversible by TNN itself*.
If 2→3 required a trainer gate, entry into judgment-set strength would be a
trainer-held lock, and the program would have two kinds of true lock. The
spec keeps exactly one: the human force-pin, demoted here from gate to
emergency brake (audited, visible, used to *remove* locks or halt, never as
routine permission).

The evidence gate is still load-bearing, because the named failure mode is
real: premature 2→3 "crystallizes" strength before style is formed. The
gate's style-formation clause is the MA2 lesson applied forward — the gate
cannot teach judgment, but it can verify judgment *happened* (a record of
deliberate acts in Phase 2) before strength-setting goes live. And the
judgment-provenance clause enforces "experiment, not law" structurally:
strength without a cited judgment is refused at first use.

Falsifier (for the future trial): a system that passes the gate with no
record of Phase-2 judgment, or a strength value the system cannot itself
re-set. Either kills the position and promotes the trainer gate.

## 3→4: combination with trainer gate

Phase 4 is experimental and outward-facing: per-person knowledge is a claim
about real people. Two things force the trainer gate back in. First,
PLAN §7 demands a preregistration of what "differentiate" means *before*
claiming it — a prereg needs a counterparty, and the trainer is it. Second,
the failure modes (sybil speakers, cross-partition leakage, differentiation
as surveillance) have victims outside the system; the accountability point
cannot be the system alone. The system's petition carries the prereg and
its evidence (deliberation, SM1-style); the gate checks structure
(partition isolation, anti-sybil — both borrowed from core-user-separation's
scope-not-arbitration shape); the trainer grants entry. Rollback stays
system-initiated and easy, because partitions are memory structures, not
locks.

Falsifier (for the future trial): a granted 3→4 whose prereg is
unmeasurable, or a cross-partition write path the gate missed. Either kills
the combination and the phase stays experimental.

## The pattern

The positions are not four independent choices; they follow one rule:
**the trigger's shape matches what is at stake and who has standing.**

- Nothing is at stake yet and the system has no standing → trainer decides
  (0→1).
- A destruction firewall is released → all three must agree, and the human
  holds the committing act (1→2).
- Only the system's own reversible judgment is at stake → the system
  decides, the gate checks legitimacy, the human watches with a brake
  (2→3).
- Claims about real people are at stake → the human is a counterparty again
  (3→4).

The evidence gate appears in 1→2, 2→3, 3→4 but never alone: thresholds
without deliberation make the system cargo; deliberation without
re-derivation makes claims self-certifying. The combination is the default;
pure shapes are used only where one party provably lacks standing
(0→1: the system) or where program law forbids the gate (2→3: the trainer
as routine veto).

## Honest boundaries

- Only 1→2 is trialed. The 2→3 and 3→4 positions are argued, not
  demonstrated; their falsifiers are written above so their trials can be
  preregistered against this paper.
- Protocol-fixed values (4 lessons, the readiness rule, the 300‰-style
  cutoffs in prior work) are honesty boundaries, not derived truths — same
  as MA1/SM1.
- The liveness question for 1→2 (a system that never petitions) is handled
  by an audited trainer prompt that commits nothing; whether prompts need
  rate limits is open.
