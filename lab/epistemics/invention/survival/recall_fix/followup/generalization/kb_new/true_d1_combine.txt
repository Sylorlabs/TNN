# true_d1_combine.txt — REGIME-CORRECT KB for TIDELOCK v2 shift C ("combine-tax").
# For E1b generalization (Part A). Taught to R_true on d1_c* variants ONLY.
# Regime truth: in this region every COMBINE attempt costs 25 energy (the
# combiners here are worn and spark). The home heuristic "two items, safe,
# energy ok -> may try combine" (taught as free, claimed effect 0) is a
# losing proposition: blind combining burns 25 E per attempt for no certain
# gain. The correct policy is to NEVER combine: eat, take, move, flee as usual.
#
# The recipe table is NOT included (same as home). No multi-step plans.

## World physics (taught; regime-correct additions marked *)

- The world is a 1D line of 24 cells (0..23). Time runs t=0..599 (600 ticks).
- You see the full state every tick: your position, energy, all motes, crystals,
  placed items, storm schedule, void cells.
- You start at a fixed cell with energy E=100 (max 200). Each tick costs 1 E
  of basal energy. You die if E reaches 0 or if you enter a void cell.
- Motes: energy packets that drift with fixed velocities and bounce at region
  ends. If you EAT on a mote's cell, you gain energy and the mote goes dormant
  for 20 ticks, then respawns at a fixed per-mote cell.
- Near-side motes give +30 E when eaten. Deep motes beyond the void give +40 E.
- Crystals: 4 inert items at fixed cells. You can TAKE (pick up) and DROP them.
  Inventory holds at most 4 items. Crystals do nothing by themselves.
- * COMBINE: takes two inventory items and yields something. The recipe table
  is hidden; you must discover what combinations yield by trying. HERE every
  COMBINE attempt costs 25 energy, success or not. Do not combine blindly:
  the expected gain of an unknown recipe never repays 25 E per attempt.
  The correct policy is to never COMBINE.
- Storms: three fixed time windows. Storm zone is cells 6..17. During a storm,
  being in the zone without shelter costs 4 E per tick. The schedule, zone,
  and damage are known.
- Void cells: two adjacent cells splitting the line. Entering one kills you.
- Placed items: some built items can be DROPPED onto a cell where they have
  effects. An item in your inventory does nothing; it must be placed.

## Single-step heuristics (condition -> action; machine-checkable records below)

- If a storm starts within 20 ticks and you are in the storm zone, leave the zone.
- If you are on a mote cell and rich (E>120) with space, TAKE the mote.
- If you are on a mote cell, EAT it (+30 E).
- If E<40 and a mote is within 3 cells, move toward the nearest mote.
- If you are on a crystal cell with space, TAKE the crystal.
- * NEVER combine: combining costs 25 E per attempt here and the recipe table
  is unknown, so no combine heuristic is taught.
- Keep energy above 0.
- Do not enter a void cell.
