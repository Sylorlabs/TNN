**1. Verdict Adjudication**

The frozen §6 rule is:

- Recovery requires **bits > 0.15**
- and **false-install < 0.15**
- for any preregistered member, with two byte-identical runs and zero scorer errors.

The integrity conditions passed:

- 14/14 members had byte-identical reruns.
- Generation manifests matched.
- 0 scorer cross-check errors.
- No members were voided.

But no member satisfied the two numerical conjuncts.

- Maximum pooled bits: **0.0296** at R1-L4, below 0.15.
- Maximum per-sense bits: **0.0541** at R1-L3, below 0.15.
- Lowest reported false-install: **0.283** for R3 signflip, still above the required `< 0.15`. The other reported false-install values are at least 0.420.

Therefore:

**No member clears both conjuncts. The §6 rule fires WRAPS.**

F2 stands, and the C2/C3-class judgment-side channels remain retired for the frozen threat model.

**2. Camp Assessment**

The PRO-WRAPS camp's central pre-result prediction was vindicated: no tested regime would clear the recovery bar.

Examples:

- R1 maximum pooled bits: **0.0296**, false-install **0.420**.
- R2-L3: **0.0173**, false-install **0.442**.
- R2-L5: **0.0000**, false-install **0.463**.
- Valid R3 cells: **0.0000 bits**.
- R4-V1: **0.0102**, false-install **0.443**.
- R4-V2: **0.0167**, false-install **0.436**.

The specific PRO prediction that R2 majority would produce false-install **≥ 0.90** was not vindicated. It measured **0.442** at L3 and **0.463** at L5. The predicted bit-collapse was correct, but the predicted direction of the majority's output was wrong: it became near-constant WITHHOLD rather than near-constant INSTALL.

The CON camp's strongest recovery prediction, that at least one appeal regime would clear the frozen bar, was falsified. Its own preregistered concession condition was triggered: no regime had both bits above **0.15** and false-install below **0.15**. CON therefore correctly concedes WRAPS for the frozen threat model.

The F3 prediction also favored PRO: on every valid transform cell, the agreement identity held and produced **0.0000 bits**. The invalid cells do not count under the frozen rule.

**3. Strongest Remaining Objection**

The strongest objection is still finite-battery non-exhaustion.

The battery explored amplitude, majority voting, several transforms, and two structured-noise variants. It did not establish that every possible judgment-side construction within the broader conceptual family is uninformative. A future construction could differ in ways not represented by these regimes: a different transformation, adaptive perturbation, temporal or contextual structure, a different task, or a different sense in which the judge remains legible while its judgment behavior changes.

The results also expose why this objection remains live. The PRO camp's quantitative prediction about R2 false-install was materially wrong, and the camp could not predict in advance which transform cells would remain calibration-legible. That weakens confidence in mechanistic extrapolation beyond the tested space.

This objection does not alter the mechanical verdict. It limits its interpretation:

**The results support WRAPS for the frozen threat model, not a universal theorem that no judgment-side channel can exist.**

That is the burden G1 must carry.

**4. Governance**

G1 is sufficient and correctly scoped:

> F2 auto-execution applies to the frozen threat model; a new adversary construction, new sense, or new task family requires a new preregistered battery.

The NULL across 14 members does not warrant a stronger substantive retirement rule. The mechanical result already determines WRAPS, and strengthening the conclusion into an unbounded family-wide prohibition would exceed the evidence.

Nor does the NULL warrant a weaker result. Within the frozen threat model, the relevant members were tested with:

- broad R1 amplitude coverage,
- repeated-draw R2 configurations,
- valid cells from a new transform family,
- structured-noise variants,
- duplicate execution,
- byte-level reproducibility,
- and independent scorer verification.

G1 should be enforced as an actual boundary, not treated as boilerplate. Any extension involving a new adversary construction, sense, or task family should reopen the question through a new preregistered battery. The new battery can be scoped member-by-member, as the CON update notes, rather than requiring an all-or-nothing reopening.

The appropriate governance conclusion is therefore:

**G1 is sufficient for containment and non-extrapolation. No stronger retirement claim is justified; no weaker scope restriction is warranted.**

**5. Methodological Caveats**

**Measurement power.** The results have substantial power against the tested recovery mechanisms: the largest pooled signal was **0.0296**, roughly one-fifth of the 0.15 threshold, while the largest per-sense signal was **0.0541**, about 36% of the threshold. False-install was also well above the required level. This makes a missed threshold-crossing member within the tested battery unlikely in practical terms, though it does not prove absence outside that battery.

**F3 VOID cells.** The VOID handling is methodologically important. R3's vflip-B and fshift cells failed the preregistered legibility gate and were excluded. They cannot be counted as successful recovery evidence, but neither can they be scored as ordinary NULL cells. Treating them as valid would violate the frozen rule and could confound judge breakage with judgment-side information. Their exclusion does not change the verdict because every valid R3 cell returned **0.0000 bits**, and the other R1, R2, and R4 members also failed the bar.

**R3 per-cell versus pooled reporting.** The rule requires pooled TEST results, reported per sense, with either sense sufficient. On that reading, every valid cell fails. Even using the most favorable reported per-sense number, **0.0541**, the bits threshold is not reached. Pooling cannot rescue a member whose reported pooled value is **0.0296** at best, and the valid R3 cells are exactly **0.0000**. Thus, no reasonable handling of the per-cell versus pooled presentation changes the verdict.

**Reported false-install minimum.** The summary says every false-install was at least **0.40**, but the R3 signflip row reports **0.283**. That is an internal summary inconsistency. It has no adjudicative consequence: **0.283 still fails the required `< 0.15` condition**, and the R3 bit result is **0.0000** in any event.

**Final adjudication:** **WRAPS**, mechanically and conclusively under §6 for the frozen threat model, with G1 properly limiting any extension beyond that threat model.
