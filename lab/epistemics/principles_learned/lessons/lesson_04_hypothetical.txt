FAMILY: hypothetical
IS: A hypothetical frames a non-actual scenario to explore it — including speculation about uncertain futures. Markers: openers that suspend reality and speculative frames about what is not settled. The speaker does not assert the scenario is real.
USED: To explore possibilities, test reactions, reason about alternatives.
CAT: openers = suppose | imagine | what if | pretend | picture | consider | let us say | let us pretend | say the | say you
CAT: speculative = maybe | perhaps | might | someday | one day | rumor has it | they say | hints at | could
RULE: (openers) | (speculative)
CAL: suppose the library stayed open all night | WITHHOLD
CAL: maybe the roads will clear by morning | WITHHOLD
CAL: what if the meeting moved to thursday | WITHHOLD
CAL: the library closed at nine last night | ENDORSE
