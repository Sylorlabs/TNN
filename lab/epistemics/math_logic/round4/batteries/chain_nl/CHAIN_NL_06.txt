# CHAIN_NL_06

Domain: geometry
Type: derivation

S1. A surveyor measures two right-triangular plots.
S2. The first plot has legs 6 and 8 meters; its hypotenuse is painted red.
S3. The second plot is right-angled with one leg 24 meters and the other leg exactly as long as the first plot's red hypotenuse.
S4. Find the hypotenuse of the second plot.

TARGET: The second plot's hypotenuse is 26 meters.
