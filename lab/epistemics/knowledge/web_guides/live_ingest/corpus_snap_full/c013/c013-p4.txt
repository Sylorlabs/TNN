description: Learn fractions the fun way! In this kid-friendly math lesson, children explore 1/2 (one half) and 1/4 (one fourth) by cutting and matching
shapes—so fractions feel simple and real. Great for preschool, kindergarten, 1st grade, and 2nd grade.

In this lesson, kids will practice:

•What a fraction is (equal parts of a whole)

•Halves and fourths using familiar, shape-based examples

•Spotting when parts are equal (even if they’re cut a different way)

Quick FAQ (for grown-ups)

•What are fractions for kids? Fractions are parts of a whole.

•What does 1/2 mean? One out of two equal parts.

•What does 1/4 mean? One out of four equal parts.
