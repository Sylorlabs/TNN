## The claim

crew_servo's gated necessity law (4 adapts / 0.36 FS dsum / 0.003 engagement on the nominal fixture) still contains **unnecessary choppiness**. All 4 of its nominal adapts fire on blocks where the *plan model itself* slewed 21.5–52.9% within one block (attack/release transients) — i.e. the analytic target T, not the render, moved. The servo was chasing model error.

**X / MEE (model-error exclusion):** crew_servo's gate plus one evidence rule — a block counts as gate evidence only when the plan-target slew `|T−Tprev|·100/max(T,Tprev)` is within the existing 15% threshold. Slew-excluded blocks neither increment nor reset the FSM counters. No new constant.

## Documented boundary (not a regression)

On the dense 16-voice chord regen, X ties gated at 35 adapts / 3.29 dsum. Trace analysis shows the servo chasing inter-voice beating (m oscillating ±20% around static T, dev never converging, gate open rb=106–187) — **sustained oscillatory model error**. MEE is designed not to intervene here: with low plan slew, it cannot distinguish beating from a genuine sustained fault without new machinery (and the prereg forbids new constants). This is the honest limit of the approach — and it *increases* confidence in the gated law's remaining adapts: they are the ones no slew-based exclusion can remove either.
